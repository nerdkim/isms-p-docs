# 2.2.2 Security Pledge  _( Annex 7-3 )_

## Certification criterion

Employees, temporary staff, external parties and others who handle information assets or are granted access rights shall submit an information protection pledge appropriate to the characteristics of their duties, so that they clearly recognize compliance matters such as internal policies, related laws, and confidentiality obligations.

## Key checkpoints

1. When hiring new personnel, is an information security and personal information protection pledge that specifies information security and personal information protection responsibilities being obtained?
2. When granting access rights to information assets to external parties such as temporary staff and outsourced service personnel, is a pledge being obtained that specifies responsibilities for information security and personal information protection, confidentiality obligations, and the like?
3. Upon an employee's resignation, is a separate pledge related to confidentiality being obtained?
4. Are information security, personal information protection, and confidentiality pledges stored securely and managed so that they can be easily located when needed?

## Detailed explanation

> * This item is a **relaxed certification criterion (Annex 7-3)** under the special provisions for information security management system certification. The certification criterion / key checkpoints above are the relaxed/adjusted official original text, while the detailed explanation / related laws / evidence / nonconformity examples below are the content of the "ISMS-P Certification Criteria Guide" for Annex 7 [2.2.3 Security Pledge] within the same control domain (under the relaxed criteria, these are applied within the scope of the certification criterion / checkpoints above).

When hiring new personnel, an information security and personal information protection pledge that specifies information security and personal information protection responsibilities shall be obtained.

- When new personnel join the organization, they sign a pledge that specifies the necessity of and responsibilities for information security and personal information protection, compliance with internal policies and related laws, and confidentiality obligations
- When significant changes such as changes to employment conditions occur, measures such as re-executing the pledge are carried out

When granting access rights to information assets (including personal information), information systems, and the like to external parties such as temporary staff and outsourced service personnel, a pledge that specifies responsibilities for information security and personal information protection, confidentiality obligations, and the like shall be prepared.

- Includes necessary content such as responsibilities for information security and personal information protection, confidentiality obligations, the duty to comply with internal regulations and related laws, and liability for damages in the event of an incident/accident arising from non-compliance with the relevant obligations

Upon an employee's resignation, a separate pledge related to confidentiality shall be obtained.

- A confidentiality pledge is collected so that the departing employee is clearly made aware that legal liability follows in the event of an information leak (included within the resignation procedure)

Information security, personal information protection, and confidentiality pledges shall be preserved securely and managed so that they can be easily located when needed.

- Stored/managed securely in a lockable cabinet or a document archive with access control applied, so that they can be used as evidence for legal liability in the event of a legal dispute

## Related laws

_No individual legal provision directly corresponding to this item is specified._

## Evidence (examples)

- Information security and personal information protection pledge (employees, external personnel)
- Confidentiality pledge (departing employees)

## Nonconformity examples

- The case where, although it is stipulated in the onboarding procedure that new hires must submit a security pledge, some recently hired employees have not completed a security pledge
- The case where security pledges are obtained from employees, but security pledges are not obtained from outsourced personnel who can directly access the information processing system
- The case where management is inadequate, such as a file binder collecting the submitted information security and personal information protection pledges being left on an office desk in a state accessible to unauthorized persons
- The case where only a security pledge is obtained from personal information handlers, but the security pledge contains only content on confidentiality and does not include responsibilities and content regarding personal information protection
