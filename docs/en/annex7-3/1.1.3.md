## Certification criterion

The chief executive officer shall establish and operate a working-level organization for the effective implementation of information security.

## Key checkpoints

1. Is a working-level organization with the necessary expertise established and operated in order to systematically carry out the organization's information security activities?

## Detailed explanation

> ※ This item is a **relaxed certification criterion (Annex 7-3)** under the special provisions for information security management system certification. The certification criterion and key checkpoints above are the official text as relaxed/adjusted, while the detailed explanation, related laws, evidence, and nonconformity examples below are drawn from the "ISMS-P Certification Criteria Guide" for the same control area, Annex 7 [1.1.3 Organizational structure] (under the relaxed criteria, they apply within the scope of the certification criterion/checkpoints above).

Considering characteristics such as the size of the organization and the importance of its business, the basis for the organizational structure necessary to build and continuously operate the information security and personal information protection management system shall be specified in documents such as the information security and personal information protection policy, and a working-level organization with the necessary expertise shall be established and operated.

- Specify matters concerning the composition/operation of the information security and personal information protection organization, such as the Chief Information Security Officer, the Chief Privacy Officer, the personal information protection working-level organization, and committees, in the policy, internal management plan, etc.
- The composition form and size of the working-level organization shall take into account the size of the enterprise-wide organization, the nature of the business and services, the importance and sensitivity of the information and personal information handled, laws and regulations, etc.
- The working-level organization may be composed as a dedicated organization or a concurrently held organization, but even when composed as a concurrently held organization, roles and responsibilities shall be formally assigned so that it can perform its actual role
- The members of the working-level organization shall consist of staff with information security and personal information protection expertise as well as strong understanding and experience of various services (holding relevant degrees and certifications, having practical experience, completing relevant training, etc.)

A committee capable of reviewing, approving, and making decisions on important information security and personal information protection matters across the entire organization shall be established and operated.

- The committee shall consist of executives, officers, the Chief Information Security Officer, the Chief Privacy Officer, and other executives and staff with actual review and decision-making authority, so that it can represent the interests within the organization and make decisions regarding information security and personal information protection
- Convene the committee regularly or as needed depending on the matter
- The committee shall review, approve, and make decisions on major matters across the entire organization

※ Major matters requiring review and decision-making by the committee (examples)
- Enactment/amendment of information security and personal information protection policies/guidelines
- Risk assessment results
- Allocation of information security and personal information protection budget and resources
- Measures for internal security incidents and major violations
- Internal audit results, etc.

For enterprise-wide information security and personal information protection activities, a working-level council composed of information security and personal information protection personnel and personnel from each department shall be established and operated.

- Determine the members and organizational structure of the working-level council according to the size of the organization and the importance of services within the scope of the management system
- The working-level council shall share/coordinate/review/improve information security and personal information protection matters at the working level, and when decision-making and management support are needed, refer them to the committee for discussion

## Related laws

- Personal Information Protection Act Article 29 (Duty of safety measures)
- Standards for Measures to Ensure the Safety of Personal Information Article 4 (Establishment/implementation and inspection of the internal management plan)

## Evidence (examples)

- Information security and personal information protection committee regulations/minutes
- Information security and personal information protection working-level council regulations/minutes
- Information security and personal information protection organization chart
- Internal management plan
- Job descriptions

## Nonconformity examples

- The information security and personal information protection committee was established, but it does not include executives or other members of management and is instead composed of heads of working-level departments, so it cannot decide matters concerning the protection of the organization's important information and personal information
- An information security and personal information protection working-level council composed of the heads (team-leader level) of key information processing departments and personal information processing departments was established in accordance with internal guidelines, but there has been no operational record for a long period
- The information security and personal information protection committee was convened, but major information security and personal information protection matters such as the annual information security and personal information protection plan, training plan, budget, and personnel were not reviewed and decided
- An information security committee was established and operated for deliberation/resolution on information security and personal information protection matters, but only the operations and IT security organizations participate while the personal information protection organization does not, so matters concerning personal information protection cannot be decided
