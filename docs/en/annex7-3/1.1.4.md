## Certification criterion

Considering the organization's core services, the scope of the management system shall be defined, and the related services as well as the organization, assets, physical locations, and so on shall be documented.

## Key checkpoints

1. Is the scope of the management system defined so as to include the core assets that may affect the organization's core services?
2. When there are exceptions within the defined scope, are the related grounds, such as clear reasons and consultation with relevant parties/approval by the responsible person, recorded and managed?
3. In order to clearly confirm the scope of the information security management system, is a document containing the relevant content (major services and business status, list of information systems, document list, and so on) prepared and managed?

## Detailed explanation

> * Note: This item is a **relaxed certification criterion (Annex 7-3)** under the special case of information security management system certification. The certification criterion/key checkpoints above are the relaxed/adjusted official original text, while the detailed explanation/related laws/evidence/nonconformity examples below are the content of the "ISMS-P Certification Criteria Guide" for the same control domain, Annex 7 [1.1.4 Scope Definition] (under the relaxed criteria, they apply within the scope of the certification criterion/checkpoints above).

The scope of the management system shall be defined so as to include the core assets that may affect the organization's core services and personal information processing.

- The management system scope shall include, without omission, tangible/intangible core assets such as executives and employees, information systems, information, and facilities related to the business (service).
- In particular, for entities obligated to obtain the information security management system certification, the scope shall be defined so that information and communications services and related information assets required by legal requirements are mandatorily included.

When there are exceptions within the defined scope, the related grounds, such as clear reasons and consultation with relevant parties/approval by the responsible person, shall be recorded and managed.

- When the scope of the information security management system and that of the personal information protection management system differ, the list of information assets within the certification scope (personal information, systems, networks, and so on) shall be clearly identified and defined from the perspectives of both the information security management system and the personal information protection management system.
- For services, information systems, and so on excluded from the certification scope, the reasons and grounds shall be recorded and managed after internal consultation and approval by the responsible person.

In order to clearly confirm the scope of the information security and personal information protection management system, a document containing the relevant content (major services and business status, list of information systems, document list, and so on) shall be prepared and managed.

- Major services and business status (including the status of personal information processing tasks)
- Status of the organization related to service provision (organizational chart, and so on)
- Status of the information security and personal information protection organization
- List of major equipment
- List of information systems and network configuration diagram
- Asset identification criteria and asset status for information assets and personal information related assets
- List of information security and personal information protection systems
- Service (system) configuration diagram and personal information (collection, use, provision, storage, management, destruction) processing flow
- Document list (e.g., policies, guidelines, manuals, operation specifications, and so on)
- Methods and procedures for establishing the information security and personal information protection management system, review of related legal compliance, internal audit
- Status of outsourcing (consigned) companies such as customer centers, IDC, IT development and operation, and so on

## Related laws

_No individual legal provision directly corresponding to this item is specified._

## Evidence (examples)

- Information security and personal information protection management system scope definition document
- List of information assets and personal information
- Document list
- Service flow diagram
- Personal information flow diagram
- Company-wide organizational chart
- System and network configuration diagram

## Nonconformity examples

- A case where development and test systems, outsourcing company employees, PCs, test terminals, and so on related to the development of information systems and personal information processing systems are omitted from the management system scope
- A case where core organizations (personnel) such as executives and employees who perform the role of key decision-makers, and business departments, for the service or business set as the scope of the information security and personal information protection management system, are not included in the certification scope
- A case where development and test systems, developer PCs, test terminals, development organizations, and so on related to the development of information systems and personal information processing systems are omitted from the management system scope
