# 1.1.1 Participation of Executive Management  _( Annex 7-3 )_

## Certification criterion

The chief executive officer shall establish and operate a reporting and decision-making structure so that executive management can participate throughout the establishment and operational activities of the information security and personal information protection management system.

## Key checkpoints

1. Are the responsibilities and roles for reporting and decision-making documented so that executive management can participate throughout the establishment and operational activities of the information security and personal information protection management system?
2. Are reporting, review, and approval procedures established and implemented that enable executive management to actively participate in decision-making regarding information security and personal information protection activities?

## Detailed explanation

> Note: This item is a relaxed certification criterion (Annex 7-3) under the special provisions for information security management system certification. The certification criterion and key checkpoints above are the relaxed/adjusted official text, and the detailed explanation, related laws, evidence, and nonconformity examples below are the content of the "ISMS-P Certification Criteria Guide" for the same control area, Annex 7 [1.1.1 Participation of Executive Management] (under the relaxed criterion, they apply within the scope of the certification criterion and checkpoints above).

The responsibilities and roles for reporting, decision-making, and the like shall be documented so that executive management, which holds decision-making authority, can participate throughout the establishment and operational activities of the information security and personal information protection management system.

- Specify the basis for activities in the information security and personal information protection policy or implementation documents so that executive management can participate in important matters of management system operation, such as the enactment/revision of information security and personal information protection policies, risk management, and internal audits.

Reporting, review, and approval procedures that enable executive management to actively participate in decision-making regarding information security and personal information protection activities shall be established and implemented.

- Define the important activities within the information security and personal information protection management system in which executive management participates, and establish a reporting structure accordingly (regular/ad hoc reporting, committee participation, etc.).
- Determine the reporting and decision-making procedures, targets, cycles, and so on to fit the organization's size and characteristics, so that executive management can effectively participate in the establishment/operation of the management system.
- In accordance with the established internal procedures, executive management receives reports on and participates in decision-making for key matters within the information security and personal information protection management system.

## Related laws

_No individual legal provision directly corresponding to this item is specified._

## Evidence (examples)

- Information security and personal information protection reporting structure (communication plan, etc.)
- Minutes of the information security and personal information protection committee
- Information security and personal information protection policies/guidelines (including executive management approval records)
- Information security plan and internal management plan (including executive management approval records)
- Information security and personal information protection organization chart

## Nonconformity examples

- The information security and personal information protection policy specifies that the information security and personal information protection status shall be reported to executive management on a quarterly basis, but the relevant reporting has not been performed for an extended period.
- While performing important information security activities (risk assessment, determination of the acceptable risk level, review of information security measures and implementation plans, review of the implementation results of information security measures, security audits, etc.), executive management or a person delegated with the authority of executive management did not participate in the related reporting, approval, or other decision-making, or the relevant evidence cannot be confirmed.
