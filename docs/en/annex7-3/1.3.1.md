## Certification criterion

Operational activities that must be performed on a continuous or periodic basis in accordance with the management system established by the organization, and their execution records, shall be recorded and managed so that they can be identified and traced, and management shall periodically verify and manage the effectiveness of the operational activities.

## Key checkpoints

1. Are the information security and personal information protection activities that must be performed periodically or continuously for the operation of the management system documented and managed?
2. Does management periodically verify the effectiveness of the management system operational activities and manage them?

## Detailed explanation

> ※ This item is a **relaxed certification criterion (Annex 7-3)** under the special case for information security management system certification. The certification criterion and key checkpoints above are the relaxed/adjusted official text, while the detailed explanation, related laws, evidence, and nonconformity examples below are drawn from the "ISMS-P Certification Criteria Guide" content for Annex 7 [1.3.3 Management of Operational Status], which belongs to the same control domain (under the relaxed criteria, they apply within the scope of the certification criterion/checkpoints above).

For the effective operation of the management system, the organization shall identify the information security and personal information protection activities that require periodic or continuous performance on a daily/weekly/monthly/quarterly/semiannual/annual basis, and shall prepare and manage a document (operational status table) that defines the performance cycle and timing, the responsible party (responsible department, person in charge), and so on, so that the operational status can be easily verified.

※ Periodic information security and personal information protection activities (examples)
- Review of access records of key job holders and personal information handlers
- Review of access rights of key job holders
- Holding of regular information security and personal information protection committee meetings
- Information security and personal information protection training
- Office security inspections
- Review of amendments to information security and personal information protection policies/guidelines
- Review of legal compliance
- Breach response drills, IT disaster recovery drills
- Internal audits, etc.

Management shall periodically verify the effectiveness of the management system operational activities and, where problems are found, manage them by taking improvement measures.

- Periodically verifying whether the management system operational activities are being carried out periodically/continuously in accordance with the operational status table, and reporting to management
- Management evaluates the effectiveness of the management system operational activities and, where necessary, takes improvement measures (changing the responsible party, adjusting the performance cycle, adding/changing/deleting operational activities, etc.)

## Related laws

- Personal Information Protection Act Article 31 (Designation of a Personal Information Protection Officer)
- Network Act Article 45-3 (Designation of a Chief Information Security Officer, etc.)

## Evidence (examples)

- Annual information security and personal information protection plan
- Information security and personal information protection operational status table
- Results of inspections on whether information security and personal information protection activities were performed

## Nonconformity examples

- Where, among the operational status of the information security and personal information protection management system, the status of activities requiring periodic or continuous performance was not documented
- Where the operational status of the information security and personal information protection management system was documented, but periodic review of that operational status was not carried out, so that some information security and personal information protection activities requiring monthly and quarterly performance were omitted and, for some, implementation could not be verified
