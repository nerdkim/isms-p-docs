## Certification criterion

Resident registration numbers may not be processed, including collection or use, except where there is a legal basis, and even where processing of resident registration numbers is permitted, an alternative means must be provided on internet websites and the like.

## Key checkpoints

1. Are resident registration numbers processed only where there is a clear legal basis?
2. Is the specific legal provision that serves as the basis for collecting resident registration numbers identified?
3. Even where resident registration numbers are processed under a legal basis, is a method provided that allows a data subject to register as a member without using their resident registration number at the stage of registering as a member through an internet website?

## Detailed explanation

> Note: This item is a relaxed certification criterion (Annex 7-3) under the special provisions for information security management system certification. The certification criterion and key checkpoints above are the relaxed/adjusted official text, while the detailed explanation, related laws, evidence, and nonconformity examples below are the content of the "ISMS-P Certification Criteria Guide" for the same control area, Annex 7 [3.1.3 Restrictions on Processing Resident Registration Numbers] (under the relaxed criterion, these apply within the scope of the certification criterion/checkpoints above).

Resident registration numbers may not be processed, including collection, except where there is a legal basis, as follows.

- Cases where processing of resident registration numbers, including collection, is permitted (collection based on consent is not allowed)

| Personal Information Protection Act Article 24-2(1) | Network Act Article 23-2(1) |
| --- | --- |
| 1. Where an Act, Presidential Decree, National Assembly Regulations, Supreme Court Regulations, Constitutional Court Regulations, National Election Commission Regulations, or Board of Audit and Inspection Regulations specifically require or permit the processing of resident registration numbers<br>2. Where it is deemed clearly necessary for the urgent life, bodily, or property interests of the data subject or a third party<br>3. Where processing of resident registration numbers is unavoidable and is prescribed by notice of the Protection Commission | 1. Where designated as an identity verification agency<br>2. Where a telecommunications business operator that resells mobile communications services and the like received from a key telecommunications business operator under Article 38(1) of the Telecommunications Business Act collects/uses the resident registration numbers of users in connection with the identity verification duties of a mobile communications business operator designated as an identity verification agency under Article 23-3 |

Where resident registration numbers are processed, the specific legal provision serving as the basis for such processing must be identified so that it can be substantiated.

- "Where an Act, Presidential Decree, National Assembly Regulations, Supreme Court Regulations, Constitutional Court Regulations, National Election Commission Regulations, or Board of Audit and Inspection Regulations specifically require or permit the processing of resident registration numbers" means that a specific provision requiring or permitting a personal information controller to process resident registration numbers exists in at least one of such Acts and the like
- Since Personal Information Protection Act Article 24-2(1)1 limits the scope of laws under which resident registration numbers may be processed, resident registration numbers may not be processed on the basis of enforcement rules
- Under Personal Information Protection Act Article 24-2(1)2, processing of resident registration numbers is exceptionally permitted where it is deemed clearly necessary for the urgent life, bodily, or property interests of the data subject or a third party
- Unless one of the exceptional grounds prescribed in each subparagraph of Personal Information Protection Act Article 24-2(1) applies, collecting resident registration numbers, providing them to third parties, or storing/retaining them is also prohibited

Note: Examples of judging the lawfulness of processing resident registration numbers
- Enforcement rules, directives/established rules/notices of administrative agencies at each level, and ordinances/rules of local governments cannot serve as a basis for collecting resident registration numbers
- If a law merely prescribes an obligation to verify identity or age, this does not amount to specifically prescribing a basis for processing resident registration numbers
- Collecting/using not the entire resident registration number but only the last 7 digits utilizes the assignment system of the resident registration number, that is, it uses the unique characteristics of the resident registration number, namely its uniqueness and identifiability, so this can be regarded as collecting/using the entire resident registration number (the same applies where only part of the last 7 digits is processed)
- Until a job applicant is finally accepted and becomes an employee, there is no provision in an Act or Presidential Decree requiring the company to process that applicant's resident registration number, so requiring the resident registration number to be entered on a resume/application form and the like is prohibited. However, after final acceptance, it is possible for the company to collect and otherwise process the applicant's resident registration number in accordance with the relevant Act or Presidential Decree for purposes such as enrollment in the four major insurances including employment insurance and withholding of salary
- Visually checking an identification card bearing a resident registration number for the purpose of identity verification and returning it is not an act of collecting the resident registration number, so it does not violate the principle prohibiting processing of resident registration numbers

Even where resident registration numbers are processed under a legal basis, a method (an alternative means of membership registration in place of the resident registration number) that allows a data subject to register as a member without using their resident registration number must be provided at the stage of registering as a member through an internet website.

- Examples of alternative means of membership registration in place of the resident registration number: i-PIN, mobile phone, credit card, certificate, etc.

Note: Identification values using alternative membership registration means (examples)
- CI (Connection Information): a one-way encrypted 88-byte string generated when identity verification is performed through an alternative means at an identity verification agency; it should be used only as external connection information, and it is desirable not to use it for internal identification purposes
- DI (Duplication Information): a one-way encrypted 64-byte string generated when identity verification is performed through an alternative means at an identity verification agency; used to restrict the creation of duplicate accounts by a user within a site

## Related laws

- Personal Information Protection Act Article 24-2 (Restrictions on Processing Resident Registration Numbers)
- Network Act Article 23-2 (Restrictions on Use of Resident Registration Numbers)

## Evidence (examples)

- Personal information collection forms (website membership registration screen, event participation, membership application form, etc.)
- Online personal information collection forms (screen providing alternative membership registration means such as identity verification)
- Where resident registration numbers are processed, evidence of the basis for processing resident registration numbers
- Personal information processing policy

## Nonconformity examples

- Where, in connection with website registration, resident registration numbers were collected based on the data subject's consent for the purpose of simple member management such as real-name verification
- Where a data subject's resident registration number was collected on the basis of enforcement rules or a local government ordinance
- Where the last 6 digits of a resident registration number are collected for the purpose of identity verification and the like when a password is lost, but there is no relevant legal basis
- Where a job applicant's resident registration number was collected without a legal basis during the recruitment screening process
- Where a resident registration number was collected for identity verification when inquiring about products or services at a call center
- Where, on the grounds that there is a legal basis for collecting resident registration numbers, only an identity verification and membership registration method requiring entry of the resident registration number was provided at the website membership registration stage, without providing an alternative membership registration means
