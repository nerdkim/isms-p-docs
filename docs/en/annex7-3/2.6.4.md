## Certification criterion

In order to prevent information leakage through the internet, malware infection, internal network intrusion, and the like, an internet access control policy must be established and implemented, including restricting internet access or services (P2P, web-hard, messengers, etc.) for major information systems, terminals used for performing major duties, terminals handling personal information, and the like.

## Key checkpoints

1. Is a control policy established and implemented for the internet access of business PCs, such as terminals used for performing major duties and terminals handling personal information?
2. Is unnecessary external internet access controlled on major information systems (DB servers, etc.)?
3. Where an obligation to block the internet network is imposed under the relevant laws, are the subject persons identified and are internet-network blocking measures applied in a secure manner?

## Detailed explanation

> * This item is a **relaxed certification criterion (Annex 7-3)** under the special provisions for information security management system certification. The certification criterion / key checkpoints above are the relaxed/adjusted official original text, while the detailed explanation / related laws / evidence / nonconformity examples below are the content of the "ISMS-P Certification Criteria Guide" for Annex 7 [2.6.7 Internet access control] in the same control domain (under the relaxed criterion, they apply within the scope of the certification criterion / checkpoints above).

In order to reduce, to an appropriate level, the risks of information leakage through the internet, malware infection, internal network intrusion, and the like, a control policy for the internet access of business PCs, such as terminals used for performing major duties and terminals handling personal information, must be established and implemented.

- Network configuration policy when connecting to the internet
- User access policy for using external email, accessing internet sites, downloading and transmitting software, etc.
- Policy for blocking access to harmful sites (adult, entertainment, etc.)
- Policy for blocking access to sites where information leakage is possible (web-hard, P2P, remote access, etc.)
- Policy related to network separation or internet-network blocking measures (whether network separation is applied, subjects of network separation, network separation method, procedures for transferring data between networks, etc.)
- Policy for reviewing (monitoring) internet access records, etc.

Unnecessary external internet access must be controlled on major information systems (database servers, etc.).

- Restrict external internet access from internal servers (DB servers, file servers, etc.) so that malware inflow, information leakage, reverse connections, etc. are blocked
- Where there is an unavoidable reason, prepare protective measures through risk analysis and allow it after approval by the responsible person

Where an obligation to block the internet network is imposed under the relevant laws, the subject persons for internet-network blocking must be identified and internet-network blocking measures must be applied in a secure manner.

- Subjects to which internet-network blocking measures apply under the Personal Information Protection Act
  - (Personal information controllers subject to the obligation) Personal information controllers for whom the average daily number of users whose personal information is stored/managed during the three months immediately preceding the end of the previous year is at least 1 million
  - (Computers, etc. subject to the obligation) Computers, etc. of personal information handlers who can download, destroy, or set access rights to personal information in the personal information processing system
  - (Measures when using external cloud services) Where a personal information processing system is configured/operated using a cloud computing service under Article 2, subparagraph 3 of the "Act on the Development of Cloud Computing and Protection of Users", apply measures that block the internet except for access to the relevant service
- Apply internet-network blocking measures in a secure manner, considering the following matters
  - Review whether one is subject to the obligation to apply internet-network blocking measures, and, if subject, identify the persons subject to internet-network blocking measures
  - If not subject to the obligation to apply internet-network blocking measures, determine whether to apply internet-network blocking measures according to the results of risk analysis, etc.
  - Apply internet-network blocking measures in a physical (e.g., configuring 2 PCs with separated networks) or logical (e.g., using virtualization technology such as VDI) manner
  - Identify bypass paths for internet-network blocking measures and apply control measures
  - Prepare control methods for secure data transfer from computers to which internet-network blocking measures are applied
  - Perform periodic inspection of the appropriateness of the internet-network blocking environment and the existence of vulnerabilities, etc.

## Related laws

- Personal Information Protection Act, Article 29 (Duty of safety measures)
- Standards for Measures to Ensure the Safety of Personal Information, Article 6 (Access control)

## Evidence (examples)

- Non-business-site (P2P, etc.) blocking policy (non-business-site blocking system management screen, etc.)
- Internet access record monitoring history
- List of persons subject to internet-network blocking measures
- Procedures and processing records for inter-network data transfer (application/approval records, etc.)
- Network configuration diagram

## Nonconformity examples

- Where internet-network blocking measures were applied under the Personal Information Protection Act, but the application of internet-network blocking measures was omitted for some persons subject to the obligation, such as those able to set access rights in the personal information processing system
- Where, as a party subject to the obligation to apply internet-network blocking measures under the Personal Information Protection Act, internet-network blocking measures were applied, but a bypass connection via another server is possible, so that in an environment where internet-network blocking measures are not applied, the personal information processing system can be accessed to download, destroy, etc. personal information
- Where some servers located in the DMZ and internal network can unnecessarily connect directly to the internet
- Where internet-network blocking measures are applied by physically separating the internet PC and the internal business PC, and an inter-network data transfer system is built/operated, but there is no approval procedure for data transfer and periodic review of data transfer records is not carried out
- Where the internal rules provide that when personal information handlers access P2P and web-hard sites, access is allowed only for a specific period after approval by the responsible person, but there are numerous cases in which exceptional access was allowed without going through the approval procedure
