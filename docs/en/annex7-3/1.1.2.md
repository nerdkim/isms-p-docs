## Certification criterion

The top management shall designate a Chief Information Security Officer who oversees information security tasks and a Chief Privacy Officer who oversees personal information protection tasks, appointing them at the executive level so that they can allocate resources such as budget and personnel.

## Key checkpoints

1. Has the top management officially designated a chief officer who will oversee and be responsible for tasks related to information security and personal information processing?
2. Are the Chief Information Security Officer and the Chief Privacy Officer designated at the executive level able to allocate resources such as budget and personnel, and do they meet the qualification requirements under the relevant laws?

## Detailed explanation

> ※ This item is a **relaxed certification criterion (Annex 7-3)** under the special provisions for information security management system certification. The certification criterion and key checkpoints above are the relaxed/adjusted official original text, while the detailed explanation/related laws/evidence/nonconformity examples below are drawn from the "ISMS-P Certification Criteria Guide" content for Annex 7 [1.1.2 Designation of the Chief Officer], which is the same control area (under the relaxed criterion, they apply within the scope of the certification criterion/checkpoints above).

In order to effectively promote information security and personal information protection management activities within the organization, the top management shall officially designate, through procedures such as personnel appointment, a Chief Information Security Officer and a Chief Privacy Officer who can oversee and be responsible for these activities.

- The Chief Information Security Officer and the Chief Privacy Officer shall be officially appointed through personnel appointment, etc., and in the case of an ex officio position, the position shall be specified in the information security and personal information protection policy statement

The Chief Information Security Officer and the Chief Privacy Officer shall be designated at the executive level able to allocate resources such as budget and personnel, and shall meet the qualification requirements under the relevant laws (※ refer to Article 36-7 of the Enforcement Decree of the Network Act).

- The Chief Information Security Officer and the Chief Privacy Officer shall be designated at the executive level able to allocate resources such as budget and personnel, being persons with knowledge and competence related to information security and personal information protection so that they can substantially oversee the organization's information security and personal information protection tasks

  ※ An information and communications service provider shall, for the security of information and communications systems, etc. and the safe management of information, designate an officer or employee who meets the standards prescribed by Presidential Decree as the Chief Information Security Officer and report this to the Minister of Science and ICT. However, the reporting is exempted in cases meeting the standards prescribed by Presidential Decree

- Compliance with the legal requirements for the designation of the Chief Information Security Officer is required (※ refer to Article 45-3 of the Network Act)

  The Chief Information Security Officer performs the following tasks

  1. The Chief Information Security Officer oversees the following tasks.
     - a. Establishment/implementation and improvement of the information security plan
     - b. Regular auditing and improvement of information security status and practices
     - c. Identification and assessment of information security risks and preparation of information security measures
     - d. Establishment and implementation of information security education and drill plans
  2. The Chief Information Security Officer may concurrently perform the following tasks.
     - a. Tasks related to information security disclosure under Article 13 of the "Act on the Promotion of Information Security Industry"
     - b. Tasks of the information security officer under Article 5(5) of the "Act on the Protection of Information and Communications Infrastructure"
     - c. Tasks of the Chief Information Security Officer under Article 21-2(4) of the "Electronic Financial Transactions Act"
     - d. Tasks of the Chief Privacy Officer under Article 31(2) of the "Personal Information Protection Act"
     - e. Implementation of other measures necessary for information security under this Act or related laws

  Requirements for designation of the Chief Information Security Officer (※ Article 36-7(1) of the Enforcement Decree of the Network Act)

  | No | Category (information and communications service provider) | Requirement for designation of the Chief Information Security Officer |
  | --- | --- | --- |
  | 1 | A person with capital of 100 million won or less / a small enterprise / a medium enterprise that is not a telecommunications business operator, a person required to obtain information security management system certification, a personal information controller required to disclose a personal information processing policy, or a mail-order business operator | The business owner or the representative |
  | 2 | A person whose total assets as of the end of the immediately preceding business year are 5 trillion won or more / among persons required to obtain information security management system certification under Article 47(2) of the Act, a person whose total assets as of the end of the immediately preceding business year are 500 billion won or more | A director (including a person under Article 401-2(1)(3) of the "Commercial Act" and an executive officer under Article 408-2 of the same Act) ※ Compliance with concurrent-position restriction requirements is required |
  | 3 | A person who does not fall under item 1 or 2 above | The business owner or the representative / a director (including a person under Article 401-2(1)(3) of the "Commercial Act" and an executive officer under Article 408-2 of the same Act) / the head of the department that oversees information security tasks |

- Compliance with the legal requirements for the designation of the Chief Privacy Officer is required (※ refer to Article 32 of the Enforcement Decree of the Personal Information Protection Act, etc.)

  The Chief Privacy Officer performs the following tasks

  1. Establishment and implementation of the personal information protection plan
  2. Regular investigation and improvement of the status and practices of personal information processing
  3. Handling of complaints related to personal information processing and remedy of damage
  4. Establishment of an internal control system to prevent the leakage and misuse/abuse of personal information
  5. Establishment and implementation of the personal information protection education plan
  6. Protection and management/supervision of personal information files
  7. Establishment/modification and implementation of the personal information processing policy
  8. Management of materials related to personal information protection
  9. Destruction of personal information whose processing purpose has been achieved or whose retention period has expired

## Related laws

- Personal Information Protection Act Article 29 (Duty of Safety Measures), Article 31 (Designation of the Chief Privacy Officer)
- Network Act Article 45-3 (Designation of the Chief Information Security Officer, etc.)
- Standards for Measures to Secure the Safety of Personal Information Article 4 (Establishment/Implementation and Inspection of the Internal Management Plan)

## Evidence (examples)

- Materials related to the appointment of the Chief Information Security Officer and the Chief Privacy Officer (personnel orders, personnel cards, etc.)
- Information security and personal information protection organization chart
- Information security and personal information protection policies/guidelines
- Job descriptions (matters concerning the roles and responsibilities of the Chief Information Security Officer and the Chief Privacy Officer)
- Records of the Chief Information Security Officer report
- Internal management plan (matters concerning the designation of the Chief Privacy Officer)

## Nonconformity examples

- The case where the Chief Information Security Officer is not designated and reported even though the entity is subject to the obligation to designate and report the Chief Information Security Officer under the Network Act
- The case where a person who does not hold substantial authority and standing related to personal information protection is designated as the Chief Privacy Officer, making it difficult to regard them as able to oversee and be responsible for tasks related to personal information processing
- The case where the Chief Information Security Officer and the Chief Privacy Officer are specified in the organization chart but the official designation procedure such as personnel appointment has not been carried out
- The case where an information and communications service provider that is subject to the ISMS certification obligation and whose total assets as of the end of the previous year exceed 500 billion won has the Chief Information Security Officer concurrently holding the position of CIO
