# 2.6.3 Remote Access Control  _( Annex 7-3 )_

| Field | Value |

| Domain | 2. Protective measure requirements |

| Criteria type | Annex 7-3 (special case of certification, relaxed certification criteria) |

## Certification criterion

Management of information systems and processing of personal information from locations outside controlled areas are prohibited in principle, and where remote access is permitted for unavoidable reasons such as telework, incident response, or remote collaboration, protective measures must be established and implemented.

## Key checkpoints

1. Is remote operation of information systems through external networks such as the internet prohibited in principle, and are supplementary measures in place where it is unavoidably permitted, such as for incident response?
2. When information systems are operated remotely through the internal network, is access permitted only for specific terminals?
3. When performing remote work such as telework, remote collaboration, and smart work, are protective measures established and implemented to prevent breaches such as leakage of important information and hacking?
4. Are terminals that remotely access the personal information processing system for purposes such as management, operation, development, and security of the personal information processing system designated as management terminals, and are safeguards applied such as prohibition of arbitrary manipulation and use for purposes other than intended?

## Detailed explanation

> ※ This item is a **relaxed certification criterion (Annex 7-3)** under the special case of information security management system certification. The certification criterion and key checkpoints above are the official text as relaxed/adjusted, while the detailed explanation/related laws/evidence/nonconformity examples below are from the content of the "ISMS-P Certification Criteria Guide" for Annex 7 [2.6.6 Remote access control], which is the same control area (under the relaxed criteria, they apply within the scope of the certification criterion/checkpoints above).

Remote operation of major assets (servers, network equipment, security equipment, etc.) associated with the processing of important information (personal information) through external networks such as the internet, information systems, and personal information processing systems is prohibited in principle, and where it is unavoidably permitted, the following measures must be established and implemented.

- Approval by the responsible person for remote operation and access
- Application of secure authentication means (certificates, OTP, etc.)
- Application of secure connection means (VPN, etc.)
- Granting of temporary access rights and management of the status of authorized persons
- Terminal security for connecting terminals such as antivirus installation and security patching
- Monitoring of remote operation status (periodic review of VPN account issuance/usage status, etc.)
- Logging of remote access records and periodic analysis
- Security awareness training related to remote operation, etc.

When operating information systems remotely through the internal network, access must be permitted only for specific terminals.

- Restricting terminals that can connect by IP address, MAC address, etc.
- Blocking connection paths that bypass the normal remote access path, etc.

When performing remote work such as telework, remote collaboration, and smart work, protective measures must be established and implemented to prevent breaches such as leakage of important information and hacking.

- Definition of smart work types: telework, smart work center, remote collaboration, mobile office environment
- Setting the scope of work permitted according to the smart work type: scope of permitted remote access to internal systems and services
- Smart work approval procedures: application, approval, revocation, etc. of remote access rights for smart work
- Technical protective measures required for remote access: encryption of transmission sections (VPN, etc.), strengthened user authentication (OTP, etc.)
- Security of connecting terminals (PCs, mobile devices, etc.): antivirus installation, security patch application, terminal authentication, measures for loss/theft (reporting procedures, terminal locking, deletion of important information, etc.), prohibition of storing important information (encryption measures when necessary), etc.
- Establishment of information protection guidelines for the smart work environment and training, etc.

For terminals (management terminals or important terminals) that remotely access the personal information processing system for purposes such as management, operation, development, and security of the personal information processing system, the following protective measures must be applied.

- Designation of management terminals and list management
- Measures to prevent unauthorized persons from accessing and arbitrarily manipulating management terminals
- Measures to prevent access from anything other than registered management terminals
- Measures to prevent use for purposes other than the original purpose
- Application of protective measures to prevent infection of management terminals by malicious programs, etc.

## Related laws

- Personal Information Protection Act Article 29 (Duty of safety measures)
- Standards for Measures to Ensure the Safety of Personal Information Article 6 (Access control)

## Evidence (examples)

- Applications for external connection such as VPN
- VPN account list
- VPN access control policy configuration status
- IP management ledger
- Remote access control configuration (server settings, security system settings, etc.)
- Designation and management status of management terminals
- Network configuration diagram

## Nonconformity examples

- The internal regulations state that remote access to the system is prohibited in principle and, where unavoidable, only approved users may access it through IP-based access control, but remote desktop connection and SSH access to the system are not restricted by IP address, etc., so remote access is possible from all PCs.
- A VPN has been established and operated for remote operation management, but it is always permitted without use approval or connection period restrictions for the VPN.
- A business mobile app has been installed and operated on personal smart devices for external workers, but appropriate protective measures (antivirus, wipe, encryption, etc.) to prevent leakage of personal information due to malware, loss/theft, etc. are not applied.
- The network segments and information systems accessible remotely per user are not restricted on the external-access VPN, so a user authenticated for remote access has excessive access to the entire internal network and information systems.
