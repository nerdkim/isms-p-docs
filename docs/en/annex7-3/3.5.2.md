## Certification criterion

The organization shall establish and implement methods and procedures for exercising rights so that data subjects can make requests such as access, correction/deletion, suspension of processing, objection, and withdrawal of consent to their personal information more easily than the collection method/procedure. When a request from a data subject is received, it shall be processed without delay and related records shall be kept. In addition, the organization shall establish and implement criteria for deletion requests, temporary measures, and the like so that information infringing on the rights of others, such as invasion of privacy or defamation, does not circulate.

## Key checkpoints

1. Are methods and procedures for exercising rights established and disclosed so that a data subject or their agent can make requests such as access, correction/deletion, suspension of processing, and withdrawal of consent to personal information (hereinafter "access etc. requests") in a manner no more difficult than the personal information collection method/procedure?
2. When a data subject or their agent makes an access etc. request, are the necessary measures in response to the access etc. request taken within the prescribed period?
3. When a data subject or their agent withdraws consent to the collection/use/provision, etc. of personal information, are necessary measures taken, such as destroying the collected personal information without delay?
4. Where there is an objection to the measure taken in response to a data subject's access etc. request, are the necessary procedures established and provided so that the data subject can raise an objection?
5. Are records kept of data subjects' access etc. requests and the results of processing them?
6. Where the rights of others, such as privacy or reputation, are infringed on an information and communications network, are procedures established and implemented so that the person whose rights were infringed can request the information and communications service provider to delete the information, etc.?

## Detailed explanation

> * This item is a **relaxed certification criterion (Annex 7-3)** under the special case for information security management system certification. The certification criterion/key checkpoints above are the officially relaxed/adjusted text, while the detailed explanation/related laws/evidence/nonconformity examples below are the content of the "ISMS-P Certification Criteria Guide" for the same control domain, Annex 7 [3.5.2 Guarantee of Data Subject Rights] (under the relaxed criteria, they apply within the scope of the certification criterion/checkpoints above).

The organization shall establish and disclose methods and procedures for exercising rights so that a data subject or their agent can make requests such as access, correction/deletion, suspension of processing, and withdrawal of consent to personal information (hereinafter "access etc. requests") in a manner no more difficult than the personal information collection method/procedure.

- Specific methods and procedures by which a data subject can make access etc. requests shall be established, and these shall be disclosed so that the data subject can easily become aware of them
- The data subject's method and procedure for exercising rights shall be at least as easy and convenient as the personal information collection procedure or the membership sign-up procedure, and supporting documents that were not required at the time of personal information collection shall not be additionally required
- It is necessary to provide as diverse methods of exercising rights as possible so that the data subject can conveniently choose (visit, in writing, telephone, e-mail, internet website, etc.)

**Matters to be observed when establishing access request methods/procedures (Article 41(2) of the Enforcement Decree of the Personal Information Protection Act)**

1. They shall be provided by methods that the data subject can easily use, such as in writing, by telephone, by e-mail, or via the internet
2. Except where there is a legitimate reason, such as where continuous operation of the channel through which personal information was collected is difficult, access to personal information shall be able to be requested at least by the same channel or method used to collect the personal information
3. A personal information controller that operates an internet website shall disclose the access request method and procedure on the website

* When establishing methods and procedures, they shall not be more difficult than the collection method and procedure of the personal information concerned

- Whether the person making the access etc. request is the data subject in person or a legitimate agent shall be verified, and the verification method shall be one objectively recognized as a reasonable means (electronic signature, i-PIN, ID verification, etc.)

**Scope of agent (Article 45 of the Enforcement Decree of the Personal Information Protection Act)**

1. The legal representative of the data subject
2. A person entrusted by the data subject (a voluntary agent granted power of representation by the data subject on the basis of a mandate contract or the like)

- Where the personal information controller is a public institution and identity verification is possible through the shared use of administrative information under the "Electronic Government Act," verification shall be made through the shared use of administrative information
- A fee and postage may be charged to the person making an access etc. request within the range of the actual costs necessary to carry out the relevant work; however, where the reason for the access etc. request lies with the personal information controller concerned, the fee and postage may not be charged

When a request for access to personal information is received from a data subject or their agent, the necessary measures shall be taken within 10 days so that the data subject can access the personal information concerned.

- A data subject may request access to the following matters regarding their own personal information processed by the personal information controller

**Matters that may be requested for access (Article 41(1) of the Enforcement Decree of the Personal Information Protection Act)**

1. The items and content of the personal information
2. The purpose of collection/use of the personal information
3. The period of retention and use of the personal information
4. The status of provision of the personal information to third parties
5. The fact of and content of consent to the processing of personal information

- Where there is a legitimate reason that access cannot be granted within 10 days, the data subject shall be notified of the reason and access may be postponed; after access to personal information has been postponed, where the reason ceases to exist, access shall be granted within 10 days from the date the postponement reason ceased to exist
- Where there is a reason to restrict or refuse access to personal information, the data subject shall be notified of the reason, and access may be restricted or refused

**Reasons that may restrict/refuse a data subject's access request (Article 35(4) of the Personal Information Protection Act)**

1. Where access is prohibited or restricted by law
2. Where there is a risk of harm to the life/body of another person, or a risk of unjustly infringing on the property and other interests of another person
3. Where a public institution would face serious difficulty in performing any of the following types of work:
   - a. Work related to the imposition/collection or refund of taxes
   - b. Work related to grade evaluation or admissions selection at schools of each level under the "Elementary and Secondary Education Act" and the "Higher Education Act," lifelong education facilities under the "Lifelong Education Act," and other higher education institutions established under other laws
   - c. Work related to tests on academic ability/skills and hiring, and to qualification examinations
   - d. Work related to an ongoing evaluation or judgment on the calculation of compensation/benefits, etc.
   - e. Work related to audits and investigations underway pursuant to other laws

- Where some of the matters requested for access are subject to a reason for restriction or refusal of access, access to that part may be restricted, and access shall be granted to the part excluding the matters for which access is restricted

Where the organization falls under the persons subject to a legal obligation, it shall establish and implement procedures and measures to respond to requests for transmission of personal information from a data subject or their agent.

- Persons subject to a legal obligation to comply with a data subject's request for transmission of personal information (information transmitters)

| Field | Person subject to legal obligation | Remarks |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Request to transmit to the data subject themselves | A personal information controller that meets the criteria prescribed by Presidential Decree in consideration of personal information processing capacity | Article 35-2(1) of the Personal Information Protection Act |
| Request to transmit to another personal information controller (only within a reasonable range technically permitted) | A personal information controller that meets the criteria prescribed by Presidential Decree in consideration of revenue, the scale of personal information held, personal information processing capacity, and industry-specific characteristics | Article 35-2(2) of the Personal Information Protection Act |

- Information for which transmission may be requested (personal information satisfying all of the following requirements)

* The amended provision of Article 35-2 of the Personal Information Protection Act concerning the right to request transmission of personal information takes effect on a date prescribed by Presidential Decree within the range from the day one year has elapsed after promulgation (2023.3.14) to no more than two years after promulgation

When a request for correction/deletion of personal information is received from a data subject or their agent, and the data subject's request is judged to be justified, the organization shall investigate the personal information within 10 days, take measures such as correction/deletion of the personal information concerned in accordance with the data subject's request, and then notify the data subject of the result.

- Reply with the result of the measure within 10 days from the date the request for correction/deletion of personal information is received
- When there is a request for correction of, or withdrawal of consent to, personal information that has been outsourced or provided to a third party, contact the trustee or the third party to request the measure
- Where the personal information is specified as a subject of collection in another law, the deletion request may be refused; in this case, the fact that the request will not be complied with, the content of the underlying law and the reason therefor, and the method of raising an objection shall be notified to the data subject concerned via a personal information correction/deletion notice within 10 days from the date the correction/deletion request is received (records of contracts/withdrawals of offer under the Electronic Commerce Act, etc.)

When a request for suspension of processing of personal information is received from a data subject or their agent, the organization shall, unless there is a special reason, suspend all or part of the processing without delay and notify the data subject of the result.

- Reply with the result of the measure within 10 days from the date the request for suspension of processing of personal information is received
- Where there is a reason that suspension of processing of personal information may be refused, the relevant fact shall be notified to the person requesting suspension of processing within 10 days from the date the request is received

**Reasons for refusing a request for suspension of processing (Article 37(2) of the Personal Information Protection Act)**

1. Where there is a special provision in a law, or where it is unavoidable in order to comply with a legal obligation
2. Where there is a risk of harm to the life/body of another person, or a risk of unjustly infringing on the property and other interests of another person
3. Where a public institution cannot perform the work under its jurisdiction prescribed by another law unless it processes the personal information
4. Where performance of the contract is difficult, such as where the service agreed with the data subject cannot be provided unless the personal information is processed, and the data subject has not clearly expressed an intention to terminate the contract

When a data subject or their agent withdraws consent to the collection/use/provision, etc. of personal information, the organization shall take necessary measures, such as destroying the collected personal information without delay.

* Measures upon a data subject's withdrawal of consent (examples)
- Destruction without delay of the personal information related to the data subject concerned
- Where a retention obligation is imposed under another law, separate retention for the period prescribed by that law
- In the case of a withdrawal of consent to third-party provision, measures so that personal information is no longer provided to the third party
- Measures so that text messages, e-mails, etc. for promotion, marketing, and the like are no longer sent, and so on

When a data subject or their agent refuses, or requests an explanation, etc. of, a decision made by processing personal information through a fully automated system (including a system applying artificial intelligence technology) (hereinafter "automated decision"), the organization shall take necessary measures.

- A data subject has the right to refuse an automated decision or to request an explanation, etc. of it

**A data subject's rights regarding automated decisions (Article 37-2 of the Personal Information Protection Act)**

(1) Where a decision made by processing personal information through a fully automated system (including a system applying artificial intelligence technology) (excluding automated dispositions by an administrative agency under Article 20 of the "Framework Act on Administration"; hereinafter referred to as an "automated decision" in this Article) has a significant effect on the data subject's rights or obligations, the data subject has the right to refuse the decision with respect to the personal information controller concerned. Provided that this shall not apply where the automated decision is made under Article 15(1)1, subparagraph 2, and subparagraph 4.

(2) Where a personal information controller has made an automated decision, the data subject may request an explanation, etc. of the decision.

- Where a data subject refuses an automated decision or requests an explanation, etc. of it, the organization shall establish and implement relevant procedures so that, unless there is a legitimate reason, it can either not apply the automated decision or carry out necessary measures such as re-processing/explanation through human intervention
- When making an automated decision, the criteria and procedures for the automated decision, the manner in which personal information is processed, and the like shall be disclosed so that the data subject can easily confirm them

* The amended provision of Article 37-2 of the Personal Information Protection Act concerning refusal of, and requests for explanation of, automated decisions takes effect on the day one year has elapsed after promulgation (effective 2024.3.15)

Where there is an objection to the measure taken in response to a data subject's access etc. request, the organization shall establish and provide the necessary procedures so that the data subject can raise an objection.

- In this case, it is necessary to involve external experts or establish an internal check mechanism so that the objection procedure can be operated fairly

The organization shall keep records of the results of receiving and processing data subjects' access etc. requests.

- The results of receiving and processing data subjects' access etc. requests shall be reviewed periodically to confirm that the guarantee of data subject rights is being carried out appropriately, and supplementary measures shall be taken if necessary

Where the rights of others, such as privacy or reputation, are infringed on an information and communications network, the organization shall establish and implement procedures so that the person whose rights were infringed can request the information and communications service provider to delete the information, etc., and shall take necessary measures so that personal information is not exposed to the public through the information and communications network.

- Where the rights of others, such as privacy or reputation, are infringed by information provided for the purpose of public disclosure through an information and communications network, the person whose rights were infringed shall be able to prove the fact of infringement to the information and communications service provider that processed the information concerned and request deletion of the information or posting of a rebuttal
- Where the rights of others are infringed and the information and communications service provider is requested to delete the information concerned, etc., it shall take necessary measures such as deletion/temporary measures without delay and immediately notify the applicant and the person who posted the information
- The content and procedures concerning remedy procedures and other necessary measures in cases where the rights of others are infringed shall be specifically stated in advance in the terms and conditions
- The personal information controller shall ensure that personal information such as unique identification information, account information, and credit card information is not exposed to the public through the information and communications network, and where the Personal Information Protection Commission or the Korea Internet & Security Agency requests it regarding personal information exposed to the public, it shall take necessary measures such as deleting or blocking the information concerned

## Related laws

- Personal Information Protection Act Article 34-2 (Deletion/Blocking of Exposed Personal Information), Article 35 (Access to Personal Information), Article 35-2 (Request for Transmission of Personal Information), Article 36 (Correction/Deletion of Personal Information), Article 37 (Suspension of Processing of Personal Information, etc.), Article 37-2 (Rights of Data Subjects Regarding Automated Decisions, etc.), Article 38 (Methods and Procedures for Exercising Rights)
- Network Act Article 44 (Protection of Rights on Information and Communications Networks), Article 44-2 (Requests for Deletion of Information, etc.), Article 44-3 (Discretionary Temporary Measures)

## Evidence (examples)

- Personal information processing policy
- Procedures and related forms for processing personal information access etc. requests
- Records of measures taken upon personal information access etc. requests
- Membership withdrawal and consent withdrawal procedures

## Nonconformity examples

- Where the methods for requesting access, correction/deletion, or suspension of processing of personal information are not disclosed so that data subjects can become aware of them
- Where a reply to a request for access to personal information is made more than 10 days after the date the access request was received, without notifying a legitimate reason
- Where records of the processing history of personal information access complaints are not kept and retained
- Where an access notification is made without a procedure to verify whether the requester is the data subject in person or a legitimate agent
- Where a reply to a request for correction/deletion of personal information is made more than 10 days after the date the correction/deletion request was received
- Where membership sign-up was easily possible online, but membership withdrawal is made possible only by submitting additional documents such as an ID or only through an offline visit
