## Certification criterion

To process sensitive information and unique identifying information (excluding resident registration numbers), the separate consent of the data subject must be obtained, except where laws and regulations specifically require or permit such processing.

## Key checkpoints

1. Is sensitive information processed only where separate consent has been obtained from the data subject or where there is a basis in the relevant laws and regulations?
2. Is unique identifying information (excluding resident registration numbers) processed only where separate consent has been obtained from the data subject or where there is a specific basis in the relevant laws and regulations?
3. Where it is determined that there is a risk of privacy infringement because the information disclosed in the course of providing goods or services includes the data subject's sensitive information, are the possibility of disclosure of the sensitive information and the method for choosing non-disclosure notified to the data subject in an easily understandable manner before the goods or services are provided?

## Detailed explanation

> Note: This item is a **relaxed certification criterion (Annex 7-3)** under the special provisions for information security management system certification. The certification criterion / key checkpoints above are the relaxed/adjusted official text, while the detailed explanation / related laws / evidence / nonconformity examples below are the content of the "ISMS-P Certification Criteria Guide" for the same control area, Annex 7 [3.1.4 Restrictions on Processing Sensitive Information and Unique Identifying Information] (under the relaxed criterion, they apply within the scope of the certification criterion / checkpoints above).

The processing of sensitive information is prohibited in principle, and may be carried out only where separate consent has been obtained from the data subject or where there is a basis in the relevant laws and regulations.

- Scope of sensitive information
  1. Ideology/beliefs : various ideologies or ideological tendencies, religious beliefs, etc.
  2. Political views : information on positions on political matters or on support for a particular political party
  3. Joining/withdrawing from a labor union/political party : information on joining or withdrawing from a labor union or political party
  4. Information on health and sex life : information on an individual's past and present medical history, physical/mental disability (such as whether there is a disability grade), sexual orientation, etc.
  5. Personal information likely to significantly infringe on privacy
     - Genetic information obtained from the results of genetic testing, etc., and information on criminal records
     - Information on criminal records, such as the imposition or disposition of a sentence of a fine or heavier / exemption and suspension of sentence, protective custody, medical treatment custody, probation, invalidation of suspension of sentence, revocation of suspension of execution, confiscation, additional collection, community service order, or detention order imposed together with a sentence of a fine or heavier
     - Information on an individual's physical/physiological/behavioral characteristics generated through certain technical means for the purpose of uniquely identifying that individual (biometric characteristic information)
     - Information on race or ethnicity

- Cases where sensitive information may be processed
  1. Where consent has been obtained from the data subject separately from consent to the processing of other personal information
  2. Where laws and regulations require or permit the processing of sensitive information

Unique identifying information (excluding resident registration numbers) must be processed only where separate consent has been obtained from the data subject or where there is a specific basis in the relevant laws and regulations.

- Scope of unique identifying information
  1. Resident registration number (however, under the statutory principle on the collection of resident registration numbers, collection based on consent is not permitted)
  2. Passport number
  3. Driver's license number
  4. Alien registration number

- Cases where unique identifying information (excluding resident registration numbers) may be processed
  1. Where consent has been obtained from the data subject separately from consent to the processing of other personal information
  2. Where laws and regulations specifically require or permit the processing of unique identifying information

Where it is determined that there is a risk of privacy infringement because the information disclosed in the course of providing goods or services includes the data subject's sensitive information, the possibility of disclosure of the sensitive information and the method for choosing non-disclosure must be notified to the data subject in an easily understandable manner before the goods or services are provided.

- Where applicable, disclosure in the privacy policy is also required

## Related laws

- Personal Information Protection Act Article 23 (Restrictions on Processing Sensitive Information)
- Personal Information Protection Act Article 24 (Restrictions on Processing Unique Identifying Information)

## Evidence (examples)

- Online personal information collection forms (website sign-up screens, event participation, etc.)
- Offline personal information collection forms (membership application forms, etc.)
- Privacy policy

## Nonconformity examples

- A case where sensitive information on health, such as whether a person has a disability, is collected in order to grant benefits such as fee reductions for persons with disabilities, but blanket consent is obtained by including it among other personal information items
- A case where, at sign-up, an alien registration number is collected only from foreigners, but blanket consent is obtained by including it among other personal information items
- A case where separate consent for the collection of sensitive information or unique identifying information is obtained but some of the four matters to be notified are omitted or notified with incorrect content (such as failing to notify of the right to refuse consent and the disadvantages resulting from such refusal)
