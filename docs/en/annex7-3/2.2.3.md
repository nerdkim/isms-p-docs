# 2.2.3 Awareness Raising and Education/Training  _( Annex 7-3 )_

## Certification criterion

Annual awareness raising activities and education/training plans shall be established/operated so that employees and relevant external parties can understand the organization's management system and policies and acquire job-specific expertise, and their effectiveness shall be evaluated based on the results and reflected in the next plan.

## Key checkpoints

1. Is an annual education plan that includes the timing, duration, targets, content, methods, etc. of information security and personal information protection education established and approved by management?
2. Is education conducted regularly at least once a year in accordance with the annual education plan for all employees and external parties within the scope of the management system, and is additional education conducted when there are significant changes to related laws and regulations?
3. Is information security and personal information protection education conducted before work begins when employees are hired and when new contracts are made with external parties?
4. Do employees within the IT, information security, and personal information protection organizations receive separate education to enhance job-specific expertise related to information security and personal information protection?
5. Are records of education implementation kept, and are the effectiveness and appropriateness of education evaluated and reflected in the next education plan?

## Detailed explanation

> ※ This item is a **relaxed certification criterion (Annex 7-3)** under the special exception for information security management system certification. The certification criterion/key checkpoints above are the relaxed/adjusted official text, while the detailed explanation/related laws/evidence/nonconformity examples below are from the "ISMS-P Certification Criteria Guide" for Annex 7 [2.2.4 Awareness Raising and Education/Training], which is the same control area (under the relaxed criterion, it applies within the scope of the certification criterion/checkpoints above).

The annual information security and personal information protection education plan shall be established to specifically include the timing, duration, targets, content, methods, etc. of the education as follows, and shall be approved by management.

- Education types : employee awareness raising education, education for key job holders and personal information handlers, education for entrusted parties (trustees), specialized education, etc.
- Education plan : education purpose, education targets, education content, education methods, education schedule, education hours, etc. (differentiated according to business scale, amount of personal information held, nature of work, education targets, education type, etc.)
- Education approval : reviewing and approving the education plan, and supporting budget allocation, etc. so that it can be implemented in accordance with the plan

Education shall be conducted regularly at least once a year in accordance with the annual education plan for all employees and external parties within the scope of the management system, and additional education shall be conducted when there are significant changes to related laws and regulations.

- Include all personnel who access information assets directly/indirectly, such as employees, temporary staff, and outsourced service provider staff
- In the case of trustees and dispatched staff, provide relevant materials so that the relevant company can conduct education, and manage/supervise whether it is implemented
- Conduct education at least once a year (in particular, for personal information handlers, personal information protection education is required at least once a year in accordance with legal requirements)
- The education content shall include all content necessary so that employees and relevant external parties can understand and comply with the organization's management system and policies

  ※ Content to be included in information security and personal information protection education (examples)
  - Basic overview of information security and personal information protection, management system establishment and methods, related laws
  - Internal regulations related to information security and personal information protection, administrative/technical/physical measures
  - Cases of infringement (leakage) incidents involving important information and personal information and response measures, legal liability for violation of regulations, etc.

- Prepare and implement education methods for personnel who could not attend education due to business trips, vacations, work, etc. (additional education for absentees, relay education, online education, etc.)
- Conduct additional education when significant changes to internal regulations and procedures, internal/external infringement incidents within the organization, changes to related laws, etc. occur (however, when the matter is not important, it may be replaced by bulletin board notice, email guidance, booklet distribution, etc.)

Information security and personal information protection education shall be conducted before work begins when employees are hired and when new contracts are made with external parties.

- Conduct information security and personal information protection education at the time new personnel arise or before work is performed, so that they become familiar with the organization's policies, precautions, legal liability for violation of regulations, etc.

Employees within the IT, information security, and personal information protection organizations shall be able to receive separate education to enhance job-specific expertise related to information security and personal information protection.

- Relevant job holders : IT job holders, Chief Information Security Officer, Chief Privacy Officer (personal information protection officer), personal information handlers, information security job holders, etc.
- Education courses : participation in information security and personal information protection related conferences/seminars/workshops, education entrusted to specialized education institutions, internal education through invitation of external experts, etc.

Records of education implementation shall be kept, and the effectiveness and appropriateness of education shall be evaluated and reflected in the next education plan.

- After conducting education, keep records such as education notices, education materials, attendance sheets, etc., and evaluate the appropriateness and effectiveness of the education content through surveys or tests, etc. in accordance with pre-established evaluation criteria
- Prepare measures for improvement points derived from the education evaluation results and reflect them when establishing the next education plan

## Related laws

- Personal Information Protection Act Article 26 (Restriction on Processing of Personal Information Following Entrustment of Work), Article 28 (Supervision of Personal Information Handlers), Article 29 (Duty of Safety Measures)
- Standards for Measures to Ensure the Safety of Personal Information Article 4 (Establishment/Implementation and Inspection of Internal Management Plan)

## Evidence (examples)

- Information security and personal information protection education plan
- Education result report
- Common and job-specific education materials
- List of education attendees

## Nonconformity examples

- Where an annual information security and personal information protection education plan was established and implemented in the previous year, but no annual information security and personal information protection education plan was established in the current year without a valid reason
- Where the annual information security and personal information protection education plan specifies the education cycle and targets, but does not include content such as the implementation schedule, content, and methods
- Where the annual information security and personal information protection education plan schedules a certain number of hours for personal information protection awareness education for all employees, but does not include a personal information protection education plan needed by job type, such as for the Chief Privacy Officer (personal information protection officer) and personal information staff
- Where, upon reviewing the information security and personal information protection education plan and result report, outsourced service provider staff (cleaning staff entering the computer room, security guards, external developers, etc.) who access information assets and facilities within the certification scope were omitted from the education targets
- Where information security and personal information protection education was conducted in the current year, but some records of education implementation and evaluation (education materials, attendance sheets, evaluation questionnaires, result reports, etc.) were not kept
- Where those who did not complete information security and personal information protection education are not identified, or additional education methods (relay education, additional education, online education, etc.) for such non-completers are not established/implemented
