## Certification criterion

The organization shall periodically identify the legal requirements related to information security and personal information protection that it must comply with, reflect them in its regulations, and continuously review compliance with them.

## Key checkpoints

1. Does the organization identify the legal requirements related to information security and personal information protection that it must comply with, and keep them up to date?
2. Is compliance with legal requirements reviewed regularly, at least once a year?

## Detailed explanation

> ※ This item is a **relaxed certification criterion (Annex 7-3)** under the special provisions for information security management system certification. The certification criterion / key checkpoints above are the officially relaxed/adjusted original text, while the detailed explanation / related laws / evidence / nonconformity examples below are the content of the "ISMS-P Certification Criteria Guide" for the same control area, Annex 7 [1.4.1 Review of compliance with legal requirements] (under the relaxed criteria, they apply within the scope of the certification criterion / checkpoints above).

The organization shall identify the legal requirements related to information security and personal information protection that it must comply with, and keep them up to date.

- Identify the laws related to information security and personal information protection that the organization must comply with

  ※ Laws related to information security and personal information protection (examples)
  - Act on Promotion of Information and Communications Network Utilization and Information Protection (Network Act)
  - Personal Information Protection Act
  - Credit Information Use and Protection Act
  - Act on the Protection, Use, etc. of Location Information
  - Electronic Financial Transactions Act
  - Act on Consumer Protection in Electronic Commerce, etc.
  - Copyright Act
  - Act on the Protection of Information and Communications Infrastructure
  - Digital Signature Act
  - Act on Prevention of Divulgence and Protection of Industrial Technology
  - Unfair Competition Prevention and Trade Secret Protection Act
  - Act on the Promotion of Information Security Industry
  - Act on the Development of Cloud Computing and Protection of Its Users
  - Electronic Government Act
  - Software Promotion Act
  - Protection of Communications Secrets Act
  - Telecommunications Business Act
  - Act on Reporting and Using Specified Financial Transaction Information
  - Act on the Protection of Virtual Asset Users, etc.

- Continuously monitor the enactment/amendment status of related laws, and when an enactment/amendment occurs, analyze its impact on the organization and, if necessary, reflect it in internal policies/guidelines and checklists, etc. to maintain currency

- [Reference] Information Security Disclosure System
  - Basis: Article 13 (Information Security Disclosure) of the Information Security Industry Act and Article 8 of its Enforcement Decree
  - Disclosure content: complete the information security status form with ▲information security investment status, ▲information security personnel status, ▲matters concerning information-security-related certification/evaluation/inspection, etc., and ▲information security activities
  - Disclosure deadline: submit the information security status by June 30 each year (voluntary/mandatory disclosure)
  - Criteria for entities obligated to make information security disclosure (however, public institutions, small enterprises, financial companies, and certain electronic financial businesses are exempt)

    | Field | Target | Basis |
    | --- | --- | --- |
    | Business area | Common telecommunications provider holding line facilities (ISP) | ※ Article 6(1) of the Telecommunications Business Act |
    | Business area | Integrated information and communications facility provider (IDC) | ※ Article 46 of the Network Act |
    | Business area | Tertiary (higher-level) general hospital | ※ Article 3-4 of the Medical Service Act |
    | Business area | Cloud computing service provider | ※ Article 3(1) of the Enforcement Decree of the Cloud Computing Act |
    | Revenue | Listed corporations that have designated/reported a Chief Information Security Officer and have revenue of KRW 300 billion or more | |
    | Number of users | Daily average number of users of information and communications services of 1 million or more (for the three months immediately preceding the end of the previous year) | |

Compliance with legal requirements shall be reviewed regularly, at least once a year.

- Establish and carry out a procedure to regularly review compliance with legal requirements (review cycle, target, person in charge, method, etc.)
- Promptly take corrective action on problems found as a result of the review of compliance with legal requirements

## Related laws

- Personal Information Protection Act Article 29 (Duty of Safety Measures)
- Standards for Measures to Ensure the Safety of Personal Information Article 4 (Establishment/Implementation and Inspection of Internal Management Plan)

## Evidence (examples)

- Records of legal compliance review
- Review and revision history of information security and personal information protection policies/guidelines
- Old-vs-new comparison table of policies/guidelines
- Materials sharing law amendments internally
- Materials proving coverage for liability for personal information damages (cyber insurance agreements, etc.)
- Records of information security disclosure

## Nonconformity examples

- The Network Act and the Personal Information Protection Act were recently amended, but the impact of the amendments on the organization was not reviewed, and the changes were not reflected in policy documents/implementation documents and the legal compliance checklist, etc., so the content of the policy documents/implementation documents and the legal compliance checklist, etc. does not match the content of the laws
- A law that the organization must comply with was amended, but the compliance review for that law was not performed for a long period
- The review of compliance with legal conformity was not conducted appropriately, so numerous violations of laws such as the Personal Information Protection Act were found
- The organization became subject to the personal information damage liability coverage system under the Personal Information Protection Act, but did not recognize this and therefore did not purchase insurance or set aside reserves; or it purchased insurance but failed to comply with the minimum coverage amount criteria based on the number of users and revenue
- The organization is a business obligated to make information security disclosure, but the information security disclosure was not carried out within the time specified by law
- The organization receives users' personal location information from a location information provider through a mobile app and uses it in its service, but did not file a report for the location-based service business
- The organization is a personal information controller with no address or place of business in Korea, and the daily average number of domestic data subjects whose personal information is stored/managed for the three months immediately preceding the end of the previous year is 1 million or more, so it is subject to the obligation to designate a domestic agent, yet it did not designate a domestic agent in writing
