## Certification criterion

A cooperation system with external agencies and others shall be established so that intrusion incidents and personal information leaks can be prevented and, when an incident occurs, responded to promptly.

## Key checkpoints

1. When an intrusion incident response system is established/operated through an external agency such as a security control service, are the details of the intrusion incident response procedures reflected in the contract?
2. Is a cooperation system established with external experts, specialized firms, specialized agencies, and others for the monitoring, response, and handling of intrusion incidents?

## Detailed explanation

> ※ This item is a **relaxed certification criterion (Annex 7-3)** under the special case of information security management system certification. The certification criterion/key checkpoints above are the officially relaxed/adjusted text, while the detailed explanation/related laws/evidence/nonconformity examples below are the content of the "ISMS-P Certification Criteria Guide" for Annex 7 [2.11.1 Establishment of Incident Prevention and Response System], which belongs to the same control area (under the relaxed criteria, they apply within the scope of the certification criterion/checkpoints above).

A system and procedures to prevent intrusion incidents and personal information leak incidents and to respond promptly and effectively when an incident occurs shall be prepared, including the following contents.

- Definition and scope of intrusion incidents (personal information leak incidents, hacking incidents, denial-of-service attacks, etc.)
- Types and severity of intrusion incidents
- Procedures and methods for declaring an intrusion incident
- Communication systems such as emergency contact networks
- Intrusion incident detection system
- Recording and reporting procedures when an intrusion incident occurs
- Intrusion incident notification and reporting procedures (relevant agencies, data subjects and users, etc.)
- Preparation of intrusion incident reports
- Response and recovery procedures according to the severity and type of intrusion incident
- Composition, responsibilities, and roles of the intrusion incident recovery organization
- Intrusion incident recovery equipment and resource procurement
- Intrusion incident response and recovery drills, drill scenarios
- Plans for utilizing external experts and specialized agencies
- Other matters necessary for the prevention and recovery of security incidents, etc.

When an intrusion incident response system is established/operated through an external agency such as a security control service, the details of the intrusion incident response procedures shall be reflected in the contract (SLA, etc.).

- Scope of the security control service
- Reporting and response procedures upon discovery of signs of intrusion
- Reporting and response procedures when an intrusion incident occurs
- Matters concerning responsibilities and roles arising from the occurrence of an intrusion incident, etc.

A cooperation system shall be established with external experts, specialized firms, specialized agencies, and others for the monitoring, response, and handling of intrusion incidents.

&lt;Source&gt; Guide on Intrusion Incident Response in the Private Sector (Ministry of Science and ICT, KISA)

## Related laws

- Personal Information Protection Act Article 34 (Notification/Reporting of Leakage of Personal Information, etc.)
- Network Act Article 48-3 (Reporting of Intrusion Incidents, etc.), Article 48-4 (Analysis of Causes of Intrusion Incidents, etc.)

## Evidence (examples)

- Intrusion incident response guidelines/procedures/manuals
- Intrusion incident response organization chart and emergency contact network
- Security control service contract (SLA, etc.)

## Nonconformity examples

- Where the intrusion incident response organization and response procedures prepared for intrusion incidents are not clearly defined
- Where the internal guidelines and procedures establish and specify stage-by-stage (pre-incident, recognition, handling, recovery, reporting, etc.) response procedures for intrusion incidents, but some or all of the reporting/notification procedures and the response and recovery procedures according to the type and severity of the intrusion incident are not established
- Where the intrusion incident response organization chart, emergency contact network, and the like are not kept up to date, or the roles and responsibilities of each person in charge are not clearly defined
- Where the contact information for external agencies for intrusion incident reporting/notification and response cooperation has incorrectly specified agency names, websites, contact details, etc., or where information related to some agencies is missing or not kept up to date
- Where the detection and response of intrusion incidents is outsourced to and operated by a related agency such as an external security control specialized firm, but the mutual roles and scope of responsibility for intrusion incident response are not clearly defined in the contract or SLA
- Where intrusion incident response procedures have been established, but the criteria and timing for reporting personal information breaches do not comply with legal requirements
