# 2.10.3 Removable Storage Media Management  _( Annex 7-3 )_

| Field | Value |

## Certification criterion

Management procedures shall be established and implemented so that leakage of personal information or important information does not occur through removable storage media and so that malicious code does not infect them, and removable storage media containing personal information or important information shall be kept in a secure location.

## Key checkpoints

1. Are policies and procedures for the handling (use), storage, disposal, and reuse of removable storage media such as external hard drives, USB memory, and CDs established and implemented?
2. Is the holding status, use, and management condition of removable storage media inspected periodically?
3. Is the use of removable storage media restricted in controlled areas, important restricted areas, and similar zones where major information systems are located?
4. Are measures in place to prevent malicious code infection and leakage of important information through removable storage media?
5. Are removable storage media containing personal information or important information kept in a secure location equipped with a locking device?

## Detailed explanation

> ※ This item is a **relaxed certification criterion (Annex 7-3)** under the special provisions for information security management system certification. The certification criterion and key checkpoints above are the relaxed/adjusted official text, while the detailed explanation / related laws / evidence / nonconformity examples below are the content of the "ISMS-P Certification Criteria Guide" for the same control area, Annex 7 [2.10.7 Removable storage media management] (under the relaxed criteria they apply within the scope of the certification criterion and checkpoints above).

Policies and procedures for the handling (use), storage, disposal, and reuse of removable storage media such as external hard drives, USB memory, and CDs shall be established and implemented.

- Method for managing the holding status of removable storage media: removable storage media management ledger, etc.
- Procedures for approving and registering the use of removable storage media
- Procedures for managing the taking in/out of removable storage media
- Procedures for the disposal and reuse of removable storage media
- Scope of use of removable storage media: use policies and procedures for each protected area, such as controlled areas and restricted areas
- Protective measures for removable storage media, etc.

The holding status, use, and management condition of removable storage media shall be inspected periodically.

- Inspect the management condition, including evidence of use approval for removable storage media, holding status, management ledger, and use history verification.

The use of removable storage media shall be restricted in controlled areas, important restricted areas, and similar zones where major information systems are located.

- When use is unavoidable, use shall follow lawful procedures after going through the approval procedure of the responsible person.
- Conduct a periodic review of the status of removable storage media use within controlled areas and important restricted areas.

Measures to prevent malicious code infection and leakage of important information through removable storage media shall be prepared.

- Establish and implement protective measures such as preventing auto-run of removable storage media and using them only after an antivirus program scan.

Removable storage media containing personal information or important information shall be kept in a secure location equipped with a locking device.

- Removable storage media containing personal information or important information (portable hard disks, USB memory, SSDs, etc.) shall be kept in a safe, a secure location equipped with a locking device.

## Related laws

- Personal Information Protection Act Article 29 (Duty of Safety Measures)
- Standards for Measures to Ensure the Safety of Personal Information Article 10 (Physical Safety Measures)

## Evidence (examples)

- Removable storage media (USB, CD, etc.) blocking policy
- Removable storage media management ledger
- Records of removable storage media condition inspections

## Nonconformity examples

- A policy restricting the use of removable storage media in the server room, a controlled area, was established and operated, but multiple instances were confirmed of removable storage media being used without complying with the exception approval procedure, and because periodic inspection of the removable storage media management condition was not carried out, the removable storage media management ledger was not adequately kept up to date.
- Removable storage media containing personal information were left in office drawers and similar places rather than being kept in a secure location equipped with a locking device.
- A removable storage media control solution was introduced and operated, but for some users, writing and similar actions were permitted through exception handling without an appropriate approval procedure.
- On some shared PCs and computing equipment located in the computer room, writing to ordinary USB memory was possible, but controls such as restricting media entry and use and recording and reviewing use history were not applied.
