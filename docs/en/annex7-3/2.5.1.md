## Certification criterion

To control unauthorized access to information systems, personal information, and important information, and to grant access rights to the minimum extent according to work purposes, procedures for user registration/termination and for granting/changing/revoking access rights must be established and implemented. At the time of user registration and rights granting, it must be stipulated and made known to users that they bear security responsibility.

## Key checkpoints

1. Are formal procedures for registering/changing/deleting user accounts and access rights that can access information systems, personal information, and important information established and implemented?
2. When creating/registering/changing user accounts and access rights that can access information systems, personal information, and important information, are only the minimum rights necessary for the work granted in accordance with a job-based access rights classification scheme?
3. When granting an account and access rights to a user, is it clearly made known that the security responsibility for that account rests with the user?

## Detailed explanation

> ※ This item is a **relaxed certification criterion (Annex 7-3)** under the special case for information security management system certification. The certification criterion/key checkpoints above are the official text as relaxed/adjusted, while the detailed explanation/related laws/evidence/nonconformity examples below are the content of the "ISMS-P Certification Criteria Guide" for the same control area, Annex 7 [2.5.1 User account management] (under the relaxed criteria, they apply within the scope of the certification criterion/checkpoints above).

To control unauthorized access to information systems, personal information, and important information, formal procedures for registering/changing/deleting/terminating user accounts and access rights must be established and implemented with the following points taken into account.

- Issuing a unique user account per user and per personal information handler, and prohibiting sharing
- Reviewing appropriateness through an approval procedure, etc., when issuing accounts and granting/changing access rights for users and personal information handlers
- Changing or revoking access rights (including account deletion or deactivation) without delay when personnel changes such as transfers or resignations occur
- After installing an information system, removing default accounts, test accounts, etc., of the manufacturer or vendor, or changing them to accounts that are hard to guess
- Maintaining/managing records related to the registration/change/deletion/termination of user accounts and access rights, etc.

When creating/registering/changing user accounts and access rights that can access information systems, personal information, and important information, only the minimum rights necessary for the work must be granted in accordance with a job-based access rights classification scheme.

- Access rights to information systems and personal information processing systems are granted to work personnel on a differentiated basis within the minimum scope according to the work performance purpose
- Access rights to important information and personal information are granted only within the scope strictly necessary for work, in accordance with the principles of need-to-know and need-to-do
- Rights are subdivided so that unnecessary or excessive access to important information or personal information is not possible
- Reviewing appropriateness through an approval procedure, etc., when granting or changing rights

When granting an account and access rights to a user, it must be clearly made known that the security responsibility for that account rests with the user.

- Specifying account responsibilities and obligations in the information security and personal information protection policy, pledge, etc. (prohibition of sharing/lending one's own account and password to others, precautions when logging in at public places, etc.)
- Using various methods such as pledges, email, system notices, and training

## Related laws

- Personal Information Protection Act Article 29 (Duty of Safety Measures)
- Standards for Measures to Ensure the Safety of Personal Information Article 5 (Management of Access Rights)

## Evidence (examples)

- User account and rights application form
- User account and rights management ledger or screen
- Access rights classification table for each information system and personal information processing system
- List of users, administrators, and personal information handlers for each information system and personal information processing system

## Nonconformity examples

- Where accounts/rights for users and personal information handlers are handled through verbal requests, email, etc., without a user registration, termination, and approval procedure, so that the approval and processing history cannot be confirmed
- Where a personal information handler informs a person not designated as a personal information handler of the personal information handler account without going through a formal procedure, citing work backup due to leave, business trip, official leave, etc., as the reason
- Where excessive rights beyond what is necessary are granted to a user of an information system or personal information processing system, making access to information or personal information unnecessary for work possible
