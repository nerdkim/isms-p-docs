## Certification criterion

Protection measures for the work environment, such as a clean desk policy and periodic inspections, shall be established and implemented so that personal information and important information are not exposed to or leaked to unauthorized persons through shared office equipment (document storage rooms, shared PCs, multifunction devices, file servers, etc.) or through individual work environments (work PCs, desks, etc.).

## Key checkpoints

1. Are protection measures established and implemented for shared facilities and office equipment, such as document storage rooms, shared PCs, multifunction devices, and file servers?
2. Are protection measures established and implemented to prevent the leakage/exposure of personal information and important information through individual work environments, such as work PCs, desks, and drawers?
3. Are the protective measures necessary to safely manage printouts/copies of personal information, such as paper printouts containing personal information, in place?
4. Is compliance with information protection in individual and shared work environments reviewed periodically?

## Detailed explanation

> Note: This item is a **relaxed certification criterion (Annex 7-3)** under the special case for information security management system certification. The certification criterion/key checkpoints above are the relaxed/adjusted official text, while the detailed explanation/related laws/evidence/nonconformity examples below are the content of the "ISMS-P Certification Criteria Guide" for the same control area, Annex 7 [2.4.7 Work Environment Security] (under the relaxed criteria, they apply within the scope of the certification criterion/checkpoints above).

Protection measures shall be established and implemented for shared facilities and office equipment, such as document storage rooms, shared PCs, multifunction devices, and file servers.

- Document storage rooms: minimize the number of persons with entry access, grant entry/access rights by department/task, manage entry history
- Shared PCs: designate a person in charge, set a screen saver, set a login password, change passwords periodically, restrict storage of important information, install anti-virus software, apply security updates, etc.
- Shared office equipment: do not leave important documents unattended around shared office equipment such as fax machines, copiers, and printers
- File servers: grant access rights by department/task, minimize unnecessary disclosure of information, issue accounts per user
- Shared offices: do not leave documents containing important information (personal information) unattended in shared offices such as meeting rooms and project rooms
- Establish protection measures for other shared work environments

Protection measures shall be established and implemented to prevent the leakage/exposure of personal information and important information through individual work environments, such as work PCs, desks, and drawers.

- Protective measures when away from the desk: do not leave personal information, documents containing personal information, or auxiliary storage media unattended (clean desk), set a screen saver and a password, etc.
- Do not expose login information (passwords, etc.) on monitors, desks, and the like
- Store documents and auxiliary storage media containing personal information and important information in a safe place with a locking device
- Shred documents containing personal information and important information using a shredder or similar so that they cannot be recovered
- Take measures to prevent unauthorized persons from accessing and arbitrarily operating administrative terminals, etc.

Protective measures necessary to prevent the loss/theft/leakage of personal information through printouts/copies of personal information, such as paper printouts containing personal information, and to safely manage printouts/copies shall be taken.

Note: Protective measures for printouts/copies (examples)
- Establish policies, regulations, and guidelines for the protection and management of printouts/copies
- Prepare and record a production/management ledger for printouts/copies
- Designate and operate a department responsible for operating/managing printouts/copies
- Control/report/restrict the removal of printouts/copies off-site and their reproduction
- Store/manage records of printouts/copies, such as the person who printed and the print date/time
- Establish a destruction plan that includes destruction procedures for paper printouts and confirmation of destruction, and perform periodic inspections
- Apply security technologies for printouts/copies, such as multifunction device security and watermarks on printouts, etc.

Compliance with information protection in individual and shared work environments shall be reviewed periodically.

- Persons who fail to comply with security regulations for individual and shared work environments are managed in accordance with reward and penalty regulations

Note: Office security inspection methods (examples)
- Individual work environment: self-diagnose compliance with information protection, and have the managing department periodically inspect compliance with information protection
- Shared work environment: periodically inspect compliance with protection measures for shared work, and provide notices or training on items of non-compliance

## Related laws

- Personal Information Protection Act Article 29 (Duty of Safety Measures)
- Standards for Measures to Secure the Safety of Personal Information Article 10 (Physical Safety Measures), Article 12 (Safety Measures for Printing/Copying)

## Evidence (examples)

- Office and shared space security inspection reports
- Office and shared space security inspection checklists
- Actions taken against non-compliant persons (training, rewards and penalties, etc.)
- Status of protective measures for printouts/copies

## Nonconformity examples

- A case where the internal personal information management plan specifies that daily security inspections for personal information protection (such as operating a clean desk policy) are to be performed periodically, but this is not carried out
- A case where documents containing personal information, such as membership application forms, are stored in an office document cabinet without a locking device
- A case where employees' computers do not have a screen saver or password set, and important documents are left unattended on the desk of an employee on leave for a long period
- A case where no protection measures are established for shared PCs installed in shared office spaces such as meeting rooms, so that files containing personal information are stored without encryption, or the PCs are kept in a vulnerable state such as with security updates not applied or anti-virus software not installed
