## Certification criterion

An organization must form a team of personnel with secured independence and expertise to review, at least once a year, whether the management system is operated effectively in accordance with internal policies and legal requirements, and must report any identified problems to management.

## Key checkpoints

1. Has a management system review plan been established that includes the review criteria, scope, cycle, and qualification requirements for review personnel, in order to check whether the information security and personal information protection management system is operated effectively in accordance with legal requirements and established policies?
2. Is a team of personnel with secured independence, objectivity, and expertise formed in accordance with the management system review plan to conduct a review at least once a year, and are identified problems reported to management?

## Detailed explanation

> ※ This item is a **relaxed certification criterion (Annex 7-3)** under the special provisions for information security management system certification. The certification criterion / key checkpoints above are the relaxed/adjusted official text, while the detailed explanation / related laws / evidence / nonconformity examples below are the content of the "ISMS-P Certification Criteria Guide" for the same control area, Annex 7 [1.4.2 Management System Review] (under the relaxed criteria, they apply within the scope of the certification criterion / checkpoints above).

An organization must establish a management system review plan that includes the review criteria, scope, cycle, and qualification requirements for review personnel, in order to check whether the information security and personal information protection management system is operated effectively in accordance with legal requirements and established policies, and must report it to management.

- Review criteria : include the information security and personal information protection management system certification criteria
- Review scope : include the entire organization or the certification scope
- Review cycle : must be conducted at least once a year
- Qualification requirements for review personnel : define qualification requirements so that the objectivity, independence, and expertise of the review can be secured

An organization must form a team of personnel with secured independence, objectivity, and expertise in accordance with the management system review plan, conduct a review at least once a year, and report any identified problems to management, such as the Chief Information Security Officer and the Chief Privacy Officer.

- Organize the review team so that the objectivity, independence, and expertise of the review can be secured
- Conduct the review at least once a year in accordance with the review plan
- Prepare a review results report and report it to management, such as the Chief Information Security Officer and the Chief Privacy Officer

## Related laws

- Personal Information Protection Act Article 29 (Duty of Safety Measures)
- Standards for Measures to Ensure the Safety of Personal Information Article 4 (Establishment/Implementation and Review of an Internal Management Plan)

## Evidence (examples)

- Management system review plan (internal review plan, internal audit plan)
- Management system review results report
- Minutes of the information security and personal information protection committee

## Nonconformity examples

- A case in which the independence of the review is compromised because an IT team member who was identified as a review target is included in the management system review personnel and is involved in reviewing areas managed by the IT team
- A case in which the current year's management system review was conducted, but the review scope was limited to some areas and therefore did not cover the scope of the information security and personal information protection management system
- A case in which the management system review team is composed only of internal staff and external consultants who participated in the process of building the management system, such as risk assessment or vulnerability review, so the independence of the review cannot be regarded as secured
