## Certification criterion

The matters that must be notified to data subjects, such as the details of use/provision of personal information, shall be identified, and their content shall be notified periodically.

## Key checkpoints

1. Where the organization falls under the parties subject to a legal obligation, does it periodically notify data subjects of the details of use/provision of personal information, or of the method to access the information system where such details can be checked?
2. Do the notified items of use/provision of personal information include all legally required items?

## Detailed explanation

> * This item is a **relaxed certification criterion (Annex 7-3)** under the special provisions for Information Security Management System certification. The certification criterion/key checkpoints above are the relaxed/adjusted official text, while the detailed explanation/related laws/evidence/nonconformity examples below are the content of the "ISMS-P Certification Criteria Guide" for Annex 7 [3.5.3 Notification to Data Subjects], which belongs to the same control area (under the relaxed criteria, it applies within the scope of the certification criterion/checkpoints above).

Where the organization falls under the parties subject to a legal obligation, it shall periodically notify data subjects of the details of use/provision of personal information, or of the method to access the information system where such details can be checked.

- Legal requirements related to the notification of the details of use/provision of personal information

| Field | Value |
| --- | --- |
| Parties subject to the notification obligation | 1. A party that processes sensitive information or unique identifying information concerning 50,000 or more data subjects<br>2. A party that processes personal information concerning 1,000,000 or more data subjects<br>* The number of data subjects is calculated based on the daily average over the immediately preceding 3 months as of the end of the previous year (however, effective from January 1, 2024) |
| Method of notification | 1. A method by which the data subject can easily check the content of the notification, such as in writing/by email/by telephone/by text message<br>2. A method of informing the data subject through a notice window so that the data subject can easily become aware of it in the course of providing goods and services (limited to cases where notification is made of the method to access the information system where the details of use/provision of personal information under Article 20-2(1) of the Act can be checked) |
| Frequency of notification | At least once per year |
| Exceptions to notification | 1. A data subject who has expressed an intention to refuse notification<br>2. Where a personal information controller has processed the personal information of its own executives and employees for the performance of its work, the relevant data subject<br>3. Where a personal information controller has processed personal information such as the contact details of executives, employees, or individuals of another public institution, corporation, or organization for the performance of its work, the relevant data subject<br>4. A data subject whose personal information was used/provided pursuant to special provisions of law or to comply with a statutory obligation<br>5. A data subject whose personal information was used/provided by a public institution for the performance of its duties prescribed by statutes, etc.<br>* Where personal information that could be used to notify the data subject, such as contact details, has not been collected/retained |

The notified items of use/provision of personal information shall include all legally required items.

- Items to be notified regarding the use/provision of personal information
  1. The purpose of collection/use of personal information and the items of personal information collected
  2. The party to whom personal information was provided, the purpose of such provision, and the items of personal information provided (however, information provided pursuant to Articles 13, 13-2, and 13-4 of the "Protection of Communications Secrets Act" and Article 83(3) of the "Telecommunications Business Act" is excluded)

## Related laws

- Personal Information Protection Act Article 20-2 (Notification of the Details of Use/Provision of Personal Information)

## Evidence (examples)

- Records of notification of the details of use/provision of personal information
- Forms and wording for notification of the details of use/provision of personal information

## Nonconformity examples

- A case where, despite falling under the parties subject to the obligation to notify the details of use/provision of personal information by storing/managing personal information of 1,000,000 or more persons on a daily average over the immediately preceding 3 months as of the end of the previous year, the organization did not notify the details of use/provision of personal information in the current year
- A case where, instead of directly notifying each individual data subject of the details of use/provision of personal information, the organization merely provided guidance through a simple pop-up window or a separate notice on its website
