## Certification criterion

To maintain the availability of information systems and the integrity of data, procedures for backup targets, cycles, methods, storage locations, retention periods, off-site storage, and the like shall be established and implemented. In addition, they shall be managed so that timely recovery is possible when an incident occurs.

## Key checkpoints

1. Are backup and recovery procedures that include backup targets, cycles, methods, and procedures established and implemented?
2. Are recovery tests conducted periodically to confirm the completeness and accuracy of the backed-up information and the adequacy of the recovery procedures?
3. In the case of backup media on which important information is stored, are the backup media dispersed to a physically separated location so as to cope with disasters/calamities?

## Detailed explanation

> Note: This item is a **relaxed certification criterion (Annex 7-3)** under the special case for Information Security Management System certification. The certification criterion/key checkpoints above are the relaxed/adjusted official text, while the detailed explanation/related laws/evidence/nonconformity examples below are the content of the "ISMS-P Certification Criteria Guide" for the same control area, Annex 7 [2.9.3 Backup and Recovery Management] (under the relaxed criteria, they apply within the scope of the certification criterion/checkpoints above).

Backup and recovery procedures that include backup targets, cycles, methods, procedures, and the like shall be established and implemented so that timely recovery is possible when an information system is damaged by disasters/calamities, failures, breach incidents, and so forth.

- Establishment of criteria for selecting backup targets
- Designation of backup personnel and responsible officers
- Definition of backup cycles and retention periods for each backup target
- Backup methods and procedures : use of a backup system, manual method, and so on
- Management of backup media (e.g. : labeling, storage location, access control, etc.)
- Backup recovery procedures : for major information systems, periodic recovery tests need to be performed to inspect the completeness and accuracy of the backup information from the perspective of IT disaster recovery
- Management of the backup management ledger, and so on

Note: Major backup targets (examples) (selected in consideration of the importance of the target information and information systems)
- Important information (personal information, confidential information, etc.)
- Important databases
- Various logs (information system audit logs, event logs, security system event logs, etc.)
- Configuration files, and so on

Recovery tests shall be conducted periodically to confirm the completeness and accuracy of the backed-up information and the adequacy of the recovery procedures.

- Recovery test plan (recovery test cycle and timing, person in charge, method, etc.)
- Establishment of recovery test scenarios
- Conduct of recovery tests and reporting of results
- Establishment and implementation of improvement plans when problems are found in recovery test results

In the case of backup media on which important information is stored, the backup media shall be dispersed to a physically separated location so as to cope with disasters/calamities.

- Backup media on which important information is stored shall be dispersed and stored at a location physically distant from the place where the operating information system or the backup system is located, and the off-site storage history shall be managed with a management ledger
  - Off-site storage date (removal, return, etc.)
  - Contents of the dispersed backup media and backup information
- Periodically inspect whether off-site storage is being carried out appropriately
- Prepare the following security measures for the off-site storage location
  - Measures against natural disasters such as fire and flood (e.g. : fire-resistant safe, flame-retardant treatment, etc.)
  - Access control for the off-site storage location and media, and so on

## Related laws

- Personal Information Protection Act Article 29 (Duty of Safety Measures)
- Standards for Measures to Secure the Safety of Personal Information Article 11 (Safety Measures for Disaster/Calamity Preparedness)

## Evidence (examples)

- Backup and recovery procedures
- Recovery test results
- Off-site backup status

## Nonconformity examples

- A case where backup and recovery procedures that include backup targets, cycles, methods, procedures, and the like have not been established
- A case where a backup policy has been established, but backup target information that must be retained for a long period (6 months, 3 years, 5 years, etc.) in accordance with legal requirements is not being retained in accordance with the backup policy
- A case where backup is not being carried out for some systems (security system policies and logs, etc.) that are specified to be backed up and managed separately in accordance with higher-level guidelines or internal guidelines
- A case where higher-level guidelines or internal guidelines require recovery tests on backup media to be performed periodically, but recovery tests have not been conducted for a long period
