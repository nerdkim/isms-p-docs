# 2.1.3 Information Asset Management  _( Annex 7-3 )_

## Certification criterion

Handling procedures and protection measures shall be established and implemented according to the purpose and importance of information assets, and responsibilities for each asset shall be clearly defined and managed.

## Key checkpoints

1. Are handling procedures (creation/acquisition, storage, use, disposal) and protection measures defined and implemented according to the security grade of information assets?
2. Are owners and managers assigned for the identified information assets?

## Detailed explanation

> Note: This item is a **relaxed certification criterion (Annex 7-3)** under the special provisions for Information Security Management System certification. The certification criterion / key checkpoints above are the official relaxed/adjusted text, while the detailed explanation / related laws / evidence / nonconformity examples below are the content of the "ISMS-P Certification Criteria Guide" for the same control domain, Annex 7 [2.1.3 Information Asset Management] (under the relaxed criteria, they apply within the scope of the certification criterion / checkpoints above).

Handling procedures (creation/acquisition, storage, use, disposal, etc.) shall be defined according to the security grade of information assets, and appropriate protection measures such as encryption and access control shall be defined and implemented accordingly.

- Mark assets so that employees can identify the security grade of each information asset (confidential, restricted, general, etc.)
  - (Electronic) documents: mark via a document cover page or watermarking, etc.
  - Hardware assets such as servers: verify security grade via asset numbers or barcode labels, etc.
- Establish and implement handling procedures (creation/acquisition, storage, use, disposal, etc.) and security control standards for each security grade of information assets

For identified information assets, an owner who can be responsible for asset acquisition, change, disposal, transfer in/out, and security management, together with an owner and manager (or person in charge) who actually manages/operates the asset, shall be assigned so that responsibilities are made clear.

- Assign an owner and manager for each information asset and record them in the asset inventory
- When personnel changes such as retirement or transfer occur, or when the status of information assets changes due to acquisition/change/disposal of information assets, identify the owner and person in charge for each information asset and reflect this in the asset inventory

## Related laws

_No individual legal provision directly corresponding to this item is specified._

## Evidence (examples)

- Information asset inventory (with owners and persons in charge assigned)
- Information asset handling procedures (documents, information systems, etc.)
- Information asset management system screens
- Records of information asset security grade markings

## Nonconformity examples

- The internal guidelines require security grades to be marked on documents, but this marking is not applied.
- The person in charge and owner for each information asset were not identified, or the asset inventory was not kept up to date, so that even though the person in charge and owner of key information assets changed due to personnel changes such as retirement or transfer, this was not identified.
- Importance assessments of identified information assets are conducted to assign security grades and these are recorded in the information asset inventory, but handling procedures according to the security grades are not defined.
