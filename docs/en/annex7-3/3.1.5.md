## Certification criterion

When personal information is collected from a source other than the data subject or is provided by a third party, the organization must collect or receive only the minimum personal information necessary for its work, and where there is a legal basis or a request from the data subject, it must inform the data subject of the collection source of the personal information, the purpose of processing, and the right to request suspension of processing.

## Key checkpoints

1. When personal information is provided by a third party other than the data subject, does the contract specify that the responsibility for obtaining consent to the collection of personal information lies with the party providing the personal information?
2. When personal information is collected from publicly available media and places, is it collected/used only within the scope of the data subject's purpose/scope of disclosure and the scope in which consent is recognized to exist under generally accepted social norms?
3. Where necessary to perform a service contract, is the minimum collection principle applied even to personal information collected/generated by automatic collection devices, etc. during the process of providing the service?
4. For personal information collected from a source other than the data subject, when there is a request from the data subject, are the necessary matters immediately notified to the data subject?
5. When processing personal information collected from a source other than the data subject, where the type/scale, etc. of the personal information meets the legal requirements, are the necessary matters notified to the data subject?
6. Is the record of notifying the data subject of the collection source retained/managed until the relevant personal information is destroyed?

## Detailed explanation

> ※ This item is a **relaxed certification criterion (Annex 7-3)** under the special provisions for information security management system certification. The certification criterion/key checkpoints above are the officially relaxed/adjusted text, while the detailed explanation/related laws/evidence/nonconformity examples below are drawn from the "ISMS-P Certification Criteria Guide" content for the same control area, Annex 7 [3.1.5 Indirect Collection of Personal Information] (under the relaxed criteria, they apply within the scope of the certification criterion/checkpoints above).

- When personal information is provided by a third party other than the data subject, the organization must verify whether the information is being collected/provided in accordance with lawful procedures, and must specify concretely through a contract that the responsibility for obtaining consent to the collection of personal information lies with the party providing the personal information.

- When personal information is collected from publicly available media or places such as SNS or Internet websites, it may be used only within the scope where the data subject's intent to consent is clearly indicated, or where consent is recognized to exist under generally accepted social norms in light of the content displayed on the Internet website, etc. (Article 6(4) of the Standard Personal Information Protection Guidelines).

- Where necessary to perform a service contract, even for personal information collected/generated by automatic collection devices, etc. during the process of providing the service (call records, access logs, payment records, usage details, etc.), only the minimum personal information necessary to perform and provide the relevant service contract must be collected.
  - However, where such information is collected for use for purposes unrelated to the performance of the service provision contract, it must be classified as an optional consent item and separate prior consent must be obtained (for example, where behavioral information collected through cookies is analyzed and used for individually customized advertising, etc.).

- For personal information collected from a source other than the data subject, when there is a request from the data subject, the necessary matters must be notified to the data subject immediately.

- Matters to be notified when there is a request from the data subject
  1. The collection source of the personal information
  2. The purpose of processing the personal information
  3. The fact that there is a right to request suspension of the processing of personal information or to withdraw consent

- Unless there is a justifiable reason, the notification must be made within 3 days from the date the data subject makes the request (Article 9(1) of the Standard Personal Information Protection Guidelines).

- Where the data subject's request is refused because the notification is likely to harm the life/body of another person, etc., the grounds and reasons for such refusal must, unless there is a justifiable reason, be notified within 3 days from the date the data subject makes the request (Article 9(2) of the Standard Personal Information Protection Guidelines).

  ★ Grounds for refusing notification of the personal information collection source (Article 20(4) of the Personal Information Protection Act)
  1. Where the personal information subject to the notification request is included in a personal information file falling under any of the subparagraphs of Article 32(2) of the Personal Information Protection Act (a personal information file exempt from registration and disclosure, such as those concerning national security, diplomatic secrets, criminal investigation, or classified as secret under other laws)
  2. Where the notification is likely to harm the life/body of another person or to unjustly infringe on the property or other interests of another person

- When processing personal information collected from a source other than the data subject, where the type/scale, etc. of the personal information meets the legal requirements, the necessary matters must be notified to the data subject.

- Requirements and methods for the notification obligation

| Field | Value |
| --- | --- |
| Conditions under which the notification obligation is imposed | Where personal information is provided by another personal information controller based on the data subject's consent to third-party provision of personal information pursuant to Article 17(1)1 of the Personal Information Protection Act |
| Requirements for personal information controllers on whom the notification obligation is imposed | A controller processing sensitive information or unique identification information concerning 50,000 or more data subjects<br>A controller processing personal information concerning 1 million or more data subjects<br>※ (Basis for calculating the number of data subjects) Based on the daily average over the immediately preceding 3 months as of the end of the previous year |
| Matters to be notified | The collection source of the personal information<br>The purpose of processing the personal information<br>The fact that there is a right to request suspension of the processing of personal information or to withdraw consent |
| Timing of notification | Within 3 months from the date the personal information is provided<br>However, where personal information is provided and processed periodically two or more times per year, within the scope for which the data subject's consent was obtained pursuant to Article 17(1)1 regarding the matters under Article 17(2)1 through 4, the data subject must be notified within 3 months from the date the personal information is provided, or must be notified one or more times per year counting from the date such consent was obtained |
| Method of notification | A method by which the data subject can easily confirm the content of the notification, such as in writing/email/telephone/text message, etc.<br>A method of notifying through a notification window so that the data subject can easily be made aware of it during the process of providing goods and services |
| Exceptions to notification | Where the personal information subject to the notification request is included in a personal information file falling under any of the subparagraphs of Article 32(2)<br>Where the notification is likely to harm the life/body of another person or to unjustly infringe on the property or other interests of another person<br>※ However, limited to cases where this clearly takes precedence over the rights of the data subject under this Act |
| Other | Pursuant to Article 20(2) of the Act, notification of matters concerning the collection source of personal information, etc. may be provided together with the notification of use/provision details under Article 20-2(1) of the Act<br>The record of notifying the data subject of the collection source must be retained/managed until the relevant personal information is destroyed (the fact of notification to the data subject, the timing of notification, the method of notification) |

- This obligation to notify the personal information collection source applies only to personal information collected from a personal information controller that provided the personal information with the data subject's consent pursuant to Article 17(1)1 of the Personal Information Protection Act; therefore, it does not apply to personal information collected from a party that provided personal information with consent under the Credit Information Act, or to personal information provided under laws (Explanatory Guide to the Personal Information Protection Laws and Guidelines/Notices).

- Where the information collected by the personal information controller does not include personal information that can be used to notify the data subject, such as contact details, notification is not required.

- The record of notifying the data subject of the collection source must be retained/managed until the relevant personal information is destroyed.

- Information to be retained/managed regarding the collection source notification (Article 15-2(4) of the Enforcement Decree of the Personal Information Protection Act)
  1. The fact of notification to the data subject
  2. The timing of notification
  3. The method of notification

## Related laws

- Personal Information Protection Act Article 16 (Limitation on Collection of Personal Information), Article 19 (Limitation on Use/Provision by the Party Provided with Personal Information), Article 20 (Notification of the Collection Source, etc. of Personal Information Collected from a Source Other than the Data Subject)

## Evidence (examples)

- Contract related to the provision of personal information (contractual terms with the providing party)
- Records of notification to the data subject regarding the personal information collection source
- Personal information processing policy

## Nonconformity examples

- A case where personal information disclosed on Internet websites or SNS is being collected, but no procedure exists for handling the data subject's request regarding the collection source
- A case where personal information was provided by another business operator based on consent to the provision of personal information pursuant to Article 17(1)1 of the Personal Information Protection Act, but the relevant data subject was not notified within 3 months (only where the party provided with the information processes sensitive information or unique identification information of 50,000 or more data subjects, or processes personal information of 1 million or more data subjects)
- A case where, being subject to the legal obligation, the organization notified the data subject of the personal information collection source but omitted some mandatory notification matters, such as the purpose of processing the personal information or the fact that there is a right to withdraw consent
- A case where, being subject to the legal obligation, the organization notified the data subject of the personal information collection source but did not retain the record of the collection source notification until the relevant personal information was destroyed
