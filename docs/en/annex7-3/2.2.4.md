## Certification criterion

Upon retirement or job change, procedures such as asset return, recovery/adjustment of accounts and access rights, and verification of results, which must be carried out by each relevant department (HR / information security / personal information protection / IT, etc.), shall be established and managed.

## Key checkpoints

1. Are HR change details arising from retirement, job change, department transfer, leave of absence, etc. shared among the HR department, the information security and personal information protection department, and the department operating information systems and personal information processing systems?
2. Upon retirement or job change of personnel within the organization (executives and employees, temporary staff, outsourced service staff, etc.), are procedures such as prompt return of information assets, recovery/adjustment of access rights, and verification of results established and implemented?

## Detailed explanation

> Note: This item is a relaxed certification criterion (Annex 7-3) under the special provisions for certification of the information security management system. The certification criterion / key checkpoints above are the officially relaxed/adjusted text, while the detailed explanation / related laws / evidence / nonconformity examples below are drawn from the "ISMS-P Certification Criteria Guide" content for Annex 7 [2.2.5 Management of Retirement and Job Change], which is the same control area (under the relaxed criterion, these apply within the scope of the certification criterion / checkpoints above).

HR change details arising from retirement, job change, department transfer, leave of absence, etc. must be promptly shared among the relevant departments such as the HR department, the information security department, the personal information protection department, and the system operations department.

- Establish/implement procedures so that HR change details can be promptly shared among the relevant organizations and systems

  Note: Procedures for prompt sharing of HR change details (examples)
  - Link the information processing system with the HR system to synchronize account information in real time or as a daily batch
  - Build an integrated account registration/management system for partner company personnel and synchronize accounts with individual systems
  - Include in the retirement process a procedure that shares information about retiring personnel with the relevant departments, etc.

Upon retirement or job change of personnel within the organization (executives and employees, temporary staff, outsourced service staff, etc.), procedures such as prompt return of information assets, recovery/adjustment of access rights, and verification of results shall be established and implemented.

- Establish/implement procedures such as return of access badges and assets, deletion or locking of accounts, recovery/adjustment of access rights, and security inspection upon retirement or job change
- If an account was unavoidably being shared, change the password of that account immediately
- Retain relevant records and periodically review compliance with the retirement procedures, etc.

## Related laws

- Personal Information Protection Act Article 29 (Duty of Safety Measures)
- Standards for Measures to Ensure the Safety of Personal Information Article 5 (Management of Access Rights)

## Evidence (examples)

- Retirement and job change procedure document
- Asset (account) return management ledger at retirement
- Retiree security inspection checklist and inspection records

## Nonconformity examples

- A case where the account and rights of personnel excluded from personal information handlers due to a job change remain intact in the personal information processing system
- A case where, for key job personnel and personal information handlers who recently retired, records of implementing retirement procedures such as asset return and rights recovery cannot be confirmed
- A case where asset return management is well implemented upon retirement of executives and employees, but the retiree security inspection and retirement confirmation form specified in the HR regulations are not prepared
- A case where, upon retirement of a personal information handler, access rights to the personal information processing system were promptly recovered, but access rights to some systems such as the access control system and VPN were not recovered
