## Certification criterion

When processing pseudonymized information, the organization shall comply with legal requirements such as purpose limitation, combination limitation, safety measures, and prohibition obligations, and shall establish/implement pseudonymization procedures that can ensure an adequate level of pseudonymization.

## Key checkpoints

1. When processing pseudonymized information, has the organization established procedures for processing pseudonymized information appropriately, including purpose limitation, pseudonymization methods and standards, adequacy review, prohibition of re-identification, and measures in the event of re-identification?
2. When using/providing personal information after pseudonymization, is the pseudonymization performed at an adequate level so that individuals cannot be identified without the use/combination of additional information?
3. When combining pseudonymized information with another personal information controller, is the combination performed through a specialized combination institution or a specialized data institution?
4. When processing pseudonymized information, does the organization take the technical/managerial and physical measures necessary to ensure safety, such as deleting or separately storing/managing additional information and preparing/retaining related records?
5. Is the processing period of pseudonymized information set to an adequate period considering the purpose of processing the pseudonymized information, and once that period has elapsed, is the information destroyed without delay?
6. When anonymizing personal information, is the anonymization performed at an adequate level so that a specific individual can no longer be identified even when other information is used, considering time/cost/technology reasonably?

## Detailed explanation

> * This item is a **relaxed certification criterion (Annex 7-3)** under the special provisions for information security management system certification. The certification criterion/key checkpoints above are the relaxed/adjusted official original text, while the detailed explanation/related laws/evidence/nonconformity examples below are the content of the "ISMS-P Certification Criteria Guide" for the same control area, Annex 7 [3.2.5 Processing of Pseudonymized Information] (under the relaxed criteria, they apply within the scope of the certification criterion/checkpoints above).

When processing pseudonymized information, the organization shall establish/implement procedures for processing pseudonymized information appropriately, including purpose limitation, pseudonymization methods and standards, adequacy review, prohibition of re-identification, and measures in the event of re-identification.

- "Pseudonymization" means processing personal information by deleting part of it or replacing part or all of it, etc., so that a specific individual cannot be identified without additional information
- "Pseudonymized information" means information that cannot identify a specific individual without the use/combination of additional information for restoring it to its original state after the personal information has been pseudonymized, and is included within the category of personal information
- Under the special provisions on the processing of pseudonymized information, pseudonymized information may be processed, such as used/provided, without the consent of the data subject for 3 purposes: statistical compilation, scientific research, and preservation of public interest records
- Since pseudonymized information that may be processed without the consent of the data subject under the special provisions on the processing of pseudonymized information is limited to purposes such as statistical compilation, scientific research, and preservation of public interest records, pseudonymizing and storing personal information held without a processing purpose being set cannot be regarded as processing based on the special provisions on the processing of pseudonymized information
- Establishing and implementing pseudonymization procedures to ensure pseudonymization at an adequate level, considering the processing purpose, use environment, data characteristics, etc.

- Example of pseudonymization procedures (Pseudonymized Information Processing Guidelines)

| Step | Category | Description |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | Advance preparation (setting the purpose, etc.) | / Set the purpose of processing pseudonymized information specifically and clearly from among the 3 purposes stipulated in the Personal Information Protection Act (statistical compilation, scientific research, preservation of public interest records, etc.)<br>/ Select the pseudonymization targets by clarifying the type and scope of information needed to achieve the processing purpose<br>/ Review the suitability of the processing purpose<br>/ Implement safety measures for processing pseudonymized information (establishing an internal management plan for processing pseudonymized information, etc.)<br>/ Prepare necessary documents, etc. (preparing a consignment contract when entrusting the processing of pseudonymized information, etc.) |
| 2 | Review of the risk of the processing target | / Select the personal information files and personal information items subject to pseudonymization<br>/ Review the risk of the data subject to pseudonymization<br>　(1) Identification risk of the data itself: identifying information, identifiable information, unique information, impact upon re-identification, etc.<br>　(2) Identification risk of the processing environment: form of use (internal use, external provision, external combination, etc.), place of processing, method of processing |
| 3 | Pseudonymization | / Based on the results of the identification risk review, set the pseudonymization plan for each item by determining the pseudonymization methods and levels needed to achieve the purpose of using the pseudonymized information<br>/ Perform pseudonymization based on the pseudonymization plan for each item<br>/ Additional information generated during the pseudonymization process is, in principle, destroyed and, where necessary, stored separately from the pseudonymized information |
| 4 | Adequacy review | / Conduct a final review of the adequacy of the pseudonymization results<br>/ The adequacy review of pseudonymization may be conducted internally using internal personnel or through external experts (however, forming a review committee of at least 3 members is recommended)<br>/ If the adequacy review determines the result to be inadequate, perform additional pseudonymization and then conduct the adequacy review again |
| 5 | Safe management | / Implement the obligation to take safety measures for pseudonymized information in accordance with the internal management plan established in the advance preparation stage (separate storage or deletion of additional information, separation of access rights, etc.)<br>/ Prohibit re-identification and monitor the possibility of re-identification<br>/ If information that can identify a specific individual is generated during the processing of pseudonymized information, immediately stop processing the relevant information and, without delay, recover/destroy it<br>/ Prepare and retain records related to the processing of pseudonymized information<br>/ Disclose matters concerning the processing of pseudonymized information in the personal information processing policy, etc. |

- Where pseudonymized information processing guidelines exist separately for the relevant field, apply the content of those guidelines first
  - Guidelines on the Use of Health and Medical Data (Ministry of Health and Welfare), Guidelines on the Processing of Pseudonymized/Anonymized Information in the Education Field (Ministry of Education), Practical Guide on the Provision of Pseudonymized Information in the Public Sector (Ministry of the Interior and Safety), Guide on Pseudonymization/Anonymization in the Financial Sector (Financial Services Commission), etc.

When using/providing personal information after pseudonymization, the organization shall perform pseudonymization by an appropriate method so that individuals cannot be identified without the use/combination of additional information.

- Personal identifying information shall be deleted, or, where necessary for combination, etc., replaced with values that cannot identify a specific individual but can distinguish them, through random value generation, hash value generation, etc.
- Personal identifiable information shall be deleted where it is not strictly necessary for the purpose of processing the pseudonymized information, and the remaining personal identifiable information shall be pseudonymized by an appropriate method and at an appropriate level considering the processing purpose and identification risk, etc.

  * Examples of pseudonymization/anonymization methods (Pseudonymized Information Processing Guidelines)
  / (Deletion techniques) deletion, partial deletion, row/item deletion, local deletion, masking
  / (Statistical tools) aggregation, partial aggregation
  / (Generalization techniques) general rounding, random rounding, controlled rounding, top/bottom coding, local generalization, range method, categorization of character data
  / (Encryption) two-way encryption, one-way encryption/cryptographic hash function, order-preserving encryption, format-preserving encryption, homomorphic encryption, polymorphic encryption
  / (Randomization techniques) noise addition, permutation (substitution), tokenization, (pseudo) random number generator
  / (Other techniques) sampling, anatomization, synthetic data, homomorphic secret sharing, differential privacy, etc.

When combining pseudonymized information with another personal information controller, the organization shall perform the combination through a specialized combination institution or a specialized data institution.

- When intending to combine pseudonymized information held by different personal information controllers and use it for purposes such as statistical compilation, scientific research, and preservation of public interest records, the combination shall be performed through a specialized combination institution designated by the Personal Information Protection Commission or the head of the relevant central administrative agency
  - Status of specialized combination institutions: refer to the Pseudonymized Information Combination Comprehensive Support System (link.privacy.go.kr)
  - When combining with a dataset held by a financial company, the combination shall be performed through a specialized data institution under the Credit Information Act
- Pseudonymized information combination procedures (Pseudonymized Information Processing Guidelines)

| Step | Category | Description |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | Combination application | / The combination applicant confirms the advance preparation matters necessary for combining pseudonymized information, such as consultation on matters necessary for the combination application among applicants and preparation of the combination application form, and applies to the specialized combination institution for combination<br>/ Simulated combination, combination rate confirmation, pseudonymized information extraction, etc. are selectable |
| 2 | Combination and additional processing | / The combination applicant providing the pseudonymized information receives the information used to generate the combination key (Salt value) from the combination key management institution, generates the combination key, and once the simulated combination, combination rate confirmation, pseudonymized information extraction, etc. selected at the time of the combination application are completed, transmits the information necessary for combination to each institution |
| 3 | Export and use | / When intending to export combined information or analysis results, etc., apply to the specialized combination institution for export |
| 4 | Safe management | / The combination applicant using the combined information processes the exported combined information (hereinafter "exported information") in accordance with the purpose stated in the original combination application form and export application form, and complies with the obligation to take safety measures, etc. |

When processing pseudonymized information, the organization shall delete or separately store/manage additional information and prepare/retain related records, and shall take the technical/managerial/physical measures necessary to ensure safety.

- (Managerial protective measures) Measures such as establishing/implementing an internal management plan to safely manage pseudonymized information and additional information, managing/supervising the trustee entrusted with pseudonymized information processing tasks, including provisions such as prohibition of re-identification in the contract when entrusting pseudonymized information processing tasks and providing to a third party, establishing and disclosing a personal information processing policy related to the processing of pseudonymized information, and providing education on the protection of pseudonymized information

  * Matters to be included in a pseudonymized information processing consignment contract (examples)
  1. Prohibition of processing beyond the purpose of performing the entrusted tasks
  2. Safety measures for pseudonymized information
  3. Purpose and scope of the entrusted tasks
  4. Restriction on re-entrustment
  5. Matters concerning management/supervision
  6. Matters concerning liability such as compensation for damages in the event of a breach of obligations the trustee must observe
  7. Prohibition of re-identification
  8. Notification in the event of a re-identification risk

- (Technical protective measures) Measures such as separate storage or deletion of additional information, separation of access rights to pseudonymized information and additional information, and preparation/retention of records related to the processing of pseudonymized information
  - Matters to be included in records related to the processing of pseudonymized information (Article 29-5 (2) of the Enforcement Decree of the Personal Information Protection Act)
  1. Purpose of processing the pseudonymized information
  2. Items of personal information that were pseudonymized
  3. History of the use of pseudonymized information
  4. Recipient in the case of provision to a third party
  5. Processing period of the pseudonymized information (limited to cases where the processing period is set separately in accordance with Article 28-4 (2) of the Act)
  6. Other matters that the Protection Commission recognizes as necessary and announces to manage the details of processing pseudonymized information
  * Records related to the processing of pseudonymized information shall be retained for at least 3 years from the date of destruction of the pseudonymized information

- (Physical protective measures) When storing pseudonymized information or additional information in a computer room or data storage room, establishing/implementing procedures such as access control to protect it from access by unauthorized persons, etc.
- (Other protective measures) Since pseudonymized information also constitutes personal information, implementing the standards for measures to ensure the safety of personal information under Article 29 of the Personal Information Protection Act, prohibiting pseudonymization for the purpose of identifying a specific individual, etc.
- (Re-identification monitoring, etc.) If information that can identify a specific individual is generated during the processing of pseudonymized information, immediately stopping the processing of the relevant information and, without delay, taking recovery/destruction measures, etc.

Considering the purpose of processing pseudonymized information, etc., the organization shall set the processing period of pseudonymized information to an adequate period, and once that period has elapsed, shall destroy it without delay.

- The processing period of pseudonymized information shall be set adequately as the period necessary to achieve the purpose of processing the pseudonymized information
- Once the processing period of pseudonymized information has elapsed, it shall be destroyed without delay

When anonymizing personal information, the organization shall perform anonymization at an adequate level so that a specific individual can no longer be identified even when other information is used, considering time/cost/technology reasonably.

- "Anonymized information" means information for which a specific individual can no longer be identified even when other information is used, considering time/cost/technology reasonably
  - [Reference] Article 58-2 of the Personal Information Protection Act (exceptions to application)
  / This Act shall not apply to information for which a specific individual can no longer be identified even when other information is used, considering time/cost/technology reasonably.
- When anonymizing, personal identifying information shall be deleted, and personal identifiable information shall be anonymized at an adequate level by using anonymization methods in combination
- To ensure the adequacy of anonymization, the organization may establish and implement an anonymization adequacy review procedure, such as forming a review committee composed of internal/external experts

## Related laws

- Article 2 of the Personal Information Protection Act (definitions)
- Article 28-2 of the Personal Information Protection Act (processing of pseudonymized information, etc.)
- Article 28-3 of the Personal Information Protection Act (restriction on the combination of pseudonymized information)
- Article 28-4 of the Personal Information Protection Act (obligation to take safety measures for pseudonymized information, etc.)
- Article 28-5 of the Personal Information Protection Act (prohibition obligations when processing pseudonymized information, etc.)
- Article 28-7 of the Personal Information Protection Act (scope of application)
- Article 58-2 of the Personal Information Protection Act (exclusion from application)

## Evidence (examples)

- Pseudonymization/anonymization adequacy assessment procedures and results
- Records of the processing of pseudonymized information
- Personal information processing policy (matters concerning the use/provision of pseudonymized information), etc.

## Nonconformity examples

- A case where, while processing pseudonymized information without the consent of the data subject for statistical compilation and scientific research, the organization does not keep records related to the processing of pseudonymized information, or does not disclose the related matters in the personal information processing policy
- A case where additional information is stored without being separated within the same database as the pseudonymized information, or where access rights to the pseudonymized information and additional information are not appropriately separated
- A case where personal information is pseudonymized and used, but because pseudonymization was not performed at an adequate level, there is a possibility of identifying a specific individual through combination with other information, etc., even without the use of additional information
- A case where personal information was anonymized for purposes such as generating test data or external disclosure, but it is difficult to regard the anonymization as having been performed adequately, as there is a possibility of identifying a specific individual due to outliers, etc.
