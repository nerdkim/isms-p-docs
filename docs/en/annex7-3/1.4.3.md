## Certification criterion

The root causes of the problems in the management system identified through the review of compliance with legal requirements and the inspection of the management system shall be analyzed, and measures to prevent recurrence shall be established and implemented; management shall confirm the accuracy and effectiveness of the improvement results.

## Key checkpoints

1. Are the root causes of the problems in the management system identified through the review of compliance with legal requirements and the inspection of the management system analyzed, and are measures for preventing recurrence and for improvement established and implemented?
2. Are criteria and procedures established for confirming the accuracy and effectiveness of the recurrence-prevention and improvement results?

## Detailed explanation

> ※ This item is a **relaxed certification criterion (Annex 7-3)** under the special provisions for information protection management system certification. The certification criterion / key checkpoints above are the relaxed/adjusted official original text, while the detailed explanation / related laws / evidence / nonconformity examples below are the content of the "ISMS-P Certification Criteria Guide" for Annex 7 [1.4.3 Improvement of the Management System], which is the same control area (under the relaxed criterion, these apply within the scope of the certification criterion / checkpoints above).

The root causes of the problems in the management system identified through the review of compliance with legal requirements and the inspection of the management system shall be analyzed, and measures for preventing recurrence and for improvement shall be established and implemented.

- For problems found through inspection, an action plan shall be established and implemented, and completion of the actions shall be additionally confirmed
- Analyze the root causes of the identified problems and deficiencies in the management system
- Based on the root cause analysis results, establish and implement measures to prevent recurrence of the problems found and to improve them

  ※ Recurrence-prevention measures (examples)
  / Revising information protection and personal information protection policies/guidelines/procedures
  / Strengthening or improving education for employees and external parties
  / Strengthening monitoring of anomalous behavior, etc.
  / Automating information protection and personal information protection operations (account management, etc.)
  / Improving review/approval procedures related to information protection and personal information protection
  / Improving the internal inspection checklist or method, etc.

- Share and provide education on the established recurrence-prevention measures to the relevant personnel

Criteria and procedures shall be established for confirming the accuracy and effectiveness of the recurrence-prevention and improvement results.

- To measure the accuracy and effectiveness of the recurrence-prevention and improvement actions, derive key performance indicators (security performance indicators) from a management system perspective

  ※ Security performance indicators related to recurrence-prevention and improvement actions (examples)
  / Security policy/guideline violation rate (external transmission rule violation rate, security bypass attempt rate, etc.)
  / Number of security exception approvals
  / Security program installation rate
  / Malicious program infection rate
  / Self-inspection execution rate, etc.

- Establish and implement procedures for measuring and monitoring the key performance indicators (security performance indicators)
- The confirmation and measurement results regarding the accuracy/effectiveness of the recurrence-prevention and improvement actions shall be reported to management

## Related laws

- Personal Information Protection Act, Article 29 (Duty of Safety Measures)
- Standards for Measures to Ensure the Safety of Personal Information, Article 4 (Establishment/Implementation and Inspection of the Internal Management Plan)

## Evidence (examples)

- Management system inspection results report
- Management system inspection action plan / action results report
- Recurrence-prevention measures
- Effectiveness measurement indicators and measurement results (including reporting to management)

## Nonconformity examples

- Where problems in the operation of the information protection and personal information protection management system found through internal inspection recur identically each time
- Where the internal regulations require that, for problems found during internal inspection, a root cause analysis and recurrence-prevention measures be established, but in the most recently conducted internal inspection no root cause analysis or recurrence-prevention measures were established for the problems found
- Where recurrence-prevention measures for problems in the management system have been established and key performance indicators have been prepared and are measured periodically, but the results have not been reported to management for a long period
- Where no action plan was established for problems found during the management system inspection, or where completion of the actions was not confirmed
