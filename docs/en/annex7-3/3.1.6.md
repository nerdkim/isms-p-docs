## Certification criterion

Where fixed video information processing devices are installed/operated in a publicly accessible place, or mobile video information processing devices are operated for business purposes in a publicly accessible place, legal requirements must be complied with according to the installation purpose and location, and appropriate protective measures must be established/implemented.

## Key checkpoints

1. When installing/operating fixed video information processing devices in a publicly accessible place, is it being reviewed whether it falls under the legally permitted requirements?
2. When a public institution or the like intends to install/operate fixed video information processing devices in a publicly accessible place, are opinions of relevant experts and interested parties being gathered by going through procedures under the law, such as holding public hearings/briefing sessions?
3. When installing/operating fixed video information processing devices, are necessary measures such as installing a notice board being taken so that data subjects can easily recognize them?
4. When operating mobile video information processing devices for business purposes in a publicly accessible place, is it being reviewed whether it falls under the legally permitted requirements?
5. When filming images of persons or objects related to such persons with mobile video information processing devices for business purposes in a publicly accessible place, is the fact of filming being indicated and notified by means such as light, sound, or a notice board?
6. Has a video information processing device operation/management policy for the safe management of video information processing devices and video information been established and implemented?
7. Has a retention period for video information been set, and is it being destroyed without delay upon expiration of the retention period?
8. When entrusting the affairs concerning the installation/operation of video information processing devices, is the relevant content being reflected in the contract in accordance with the applicable procedures and requirements?

## Detailed explanation

> * This item is a **relaxed certification criterion (Annex 7-3)** under the special provisions for information security management system certification. The certification criterion/key checkpoints above are the officially relaxed/adjusted text, and the detailed explanation/related laws/evidence/nonconformity examples below are the content of the "ISMS-P Certification Criteria Guide" for the same control area, Annex 7 [3.1.6 Installation/Operation of Video Information Processing Devices] (under the relaxed criterion, it applies within the scope of the certification criterion/checkpoints above).

- When installing/operating fixed video information processing devices in a publicly accessible place, it must be reviewed whether it falls under the legally permitted requirements.
  - **Definition of a fixed video information processing device** : A device that is installed in a fixed space and continuously or periodically films images of persons or objects, or transmits such images through a wired/wireless network, referring to closed-circuit television and network cameras (Article 2(7) of the Personal Information Protection Act and Article 3(1) of its Enforcement Decree)
    - Scope of fixed video information processing devices (Article 3(1) of the Enforcement Decree of the Personal Information Protection Act)
      1. Closed-circuit television: A device that falls under any one of the following items
         - A. A device that, through a camera installed in a fixed space, continuously or periodically films images and the like, or transmits filmed video information to a specific place through a wired/wireless closed-circuit transmission line
         - B. A device that enables the recording/logging of the video information filmed or transmitted under item A
      2. Network camera: A device that, through equipment installed in a fixed space, enables the person who installs/manages that equipment to process, such as collect/store, from anywhere via wired/wireless internet the video information continuously or periodically filmed
  - Cases in which fixed video information processing devices may be installed/operated in a publicly accessible place
    1. Where specifically permitted by law
    2. Where necessary for the prevention and investigation of crimes
    3. Where installed/operated by a person with legitimate authority for the safety and management of facilities and fire prevention
    4. Where installed/operated by a person with legitimate authority for traffic enforcement
    5. Where installed/operated by a person with legitimate authority for the collection/analysis and provision of traffic information
    6. Where filmed video information is not stored, being any one of the following cases and where the filmed images are not separately stored
       - Where necessary to produce statistical values such as the number of visitors
       - Where necessary to derive statistical characteristic values such as gender and age group
       - In other cases equivalent to the above two, where deliberation/resolution of the Personal Information Protection Commission has been undergone
  - It is prohibited to install/operate video information processing devices so as to be able to view the interior of places where individual privacy may be significantly infringed, such as public bathrooms, restrooms, sweat rooms (saunas), and changing rooms used by an unspecified number of people. However, this is exceptionally permitted for facilities that confine or protect persons based on law, such as prisons and mental health facilities, as prescribed by Presidential Decree (correctional facilities, mental medical institutions with confinement facilities, mental care facilities, and mental rehabilitation facilities).

- When a public institution or the like intends to install/operate fixed video information processing devices in a publicly accessible place, it must gather opinions of relevant experts and interested parties by going through legal procedures such as public hearings/briefing sessions.
  - Those who must go through the opinion-gathering procedure
    1. The head of a public institution that intends to install/operate fixed video information processing devices in a publicly accessible place
    2. Correctional facilities, mental medical institutions, mental care facilities, and mental rehabilitation facilities that intend to install/operate fixed video information processing devices so as to be able to view the interior of places where individual privacy may be significantly infringed
  - Opinion-gathering procedure
    1. Conducting an administrative notice or hearing opinions under the "Administrative Procedures Act"
    2. A briefing session/questionnaire survey or public opinion poll targeting local residents and others directly affected by the installation of the relevant video information processing devices
  - Subjects of opinion gathering
    1. Relevant experts
    2. Interested parties such as persons working at the relevant facility, persons confined or protected at the relevant facility, or their guardians

- When installing/operating fixed video information processing devices, a notice board must be installed so that data subjects can easily recognize them.
  - Matters that must be included on the notice board
    1. Installation purpose and location
    2. Filming scope and time
    3. Contact information of the manager
    4. Name and contact information of the entrusted party (when the affairs of installing/operating video information processing devices are entrusted)
  - Exceptions to notice board installation
    1. Military facilities
    2. National key facilities
    3. National security facilities
  - Considerations when installing a notice board
    - Install it in a position where data subjects can easily recognize it
    - Where multiple fixed video information processing devices are installed within a building, a notice board indicating that the entire facility or place is a fixed video information processing device installation area may be installed in a conspicuous place such as an entrance
    - Where a public institution installs fixed video information processing devices for purposes such as long-distance filming, speeding/signal-violation enforcement, or traffic flow surveys and the risk of personal information infringement is low, or where fixed video information processing devices are installed for forest fire monitoring, and where installing a notice board is impossible due to the characteristics of the location or where data subjects cannot easily recognize the notice board even if installed, the relevant matters may be posted on an internet website

- When operating mobile video information processing devices for business purposes in a publicly accessible place, it must be reviewed whether it falls under the legally permitted requirements and the corresponding measures must be implemented.
  - **Definition of a mobile video information processing device** : A device that is worn on or carried on the body, or attached to or mounted on a movable object, to film images of persons or objects, or to transmit such images through a wired/wireless network, being a device prescribed by Presidential Decree (Article 2(7-2) of the Personal Information Protection Act and Article 3(2) of its Enforcement Decree)
    - Scope of mobile video information processing devices (Article 3(2) of the Enforcement Decree of the Personal Information Protection Act)
      1. Wearable device: A device such as glasses or a watch that is worn on a person's body or clothing to film images and the like, or to collect/store or transmit filmed video information
      2. Portable device: A device such as a mobile communications terminal or a digital camera that a person carries to film images and the like, or to collect/store or transmit filmed video information
      3. Attached/mounted device: A device that is attached to or mounted on a movable object such as a vehicle or a drone to film images and the like, or to collect/store or transmit filmed video information
  - Cases in which mobile video information processing devices may be operated for business purposes in a publicly accessible place
    1. Where it falls under any one of the subparagraphs of Article 15(1) of the Personal Information Protection Act (consent of the data subject, etc.)
    2. Where, despite clearly indicating the fact of filming so that the data subject can be aware of it, the data subject has not expressed an intention to refuse filming. In this case, this is limited to cases where there is no risk of unfairly infringing the rights of the data subject and where it does not exceed a reasonable scope
    3. In other cases equivalent to subparagraphs 1 and 2, as prescribed by Presidential Decree
  - No one may film images of persons or objects related to such persons with mobile video information processing devices in a place from which the interior of places where individual privacy may be significantly infringed, such as public bathrooms, restrooms, sweat rooms, and changing rooms used by an unspecified number of people, can be viewed. However, where filming is necessary for the rescue/first aid of human life and the like in situations such as crimes, disasters, fires, or situations equivalent thereto, filming is exceptionally permitted.

- When filming images of persons or objects related to such persons with mobile video information processing devices for business purposes in a publicly accessible place, the fact of filming must be indicated and notified by means such as light, sound, or a notice board so that data subjects can easily be aware of the fact of filming.
  - Methods of indicating the fact of filming : light, sound, notice board, written notice, public announcement, or other equivalent means
  - Exception to indicating the fact of filming : Where, due to the characteristics of the filming method such as aerial filming by drone, it is difficult to indicate and notify data subjects so that they can easily be aware of the fact of filming, matters such as the fact and purpose of filming and the date/time and location of filming are announced through the website established/operated by the Personal Information Protection Commission to support the indication of the fact of filming by mobile video information processing devices (Article 39-2(2) of the Standard Personal Information Protection Guidelines)

- The operator of video information processing devices must establish and implement a video information processing device operation/management policy for the safe management of video information processing devices and video information.
  - In the case of an operator of fixed video information processing devices, establish a fixed video information processing device operation/management policy, and in the case of an operator of mobile video information processing devices, establish a mobile video information processing device operation/management policy
  - Matters that must be included in the video information processing device operation/management policy
    1. The installation basis and installation purpose of the video information processing devices
    2. The number of installed video information processing devices, installation locations, and filming scope
    3. The manager, the department in charge, and persons who have access rights to the video information
    4. The filming time, retention period, storage location, and processing method of the video information
    5. The method and location by which the operator of video information processing devices checks the video information
    6. Measures in response to a data subject's request such as to view the video information
    7. Technical/managerial and physical measures for the protection of the video information
    8. Other matters necessary for the installation/operation and management of the video information processing devices
  - Where matters concerning the operation/management of video information processing devices are included when establishing the personal information processing policy, the video information processing device operation/management policy need not be established
  - Include in the rules and carry out management/supervision so that fixed video information processing devices are not arbitrarily manipulated for a purpose different from the purpose for which they were installed, or made to face another direction
  - Take measures so that the recording function of fixed video information processing devices cannot be used

- The retention period of video information must be set, and it must be destroyed without delay upon expiration of the retention period.
  - Determine the retention period as the minimum period for achieving the purpose of retaining the video information
  - However, where there is a special provision in another law regarding the retention period of video information, it shall be retained in accordance with that provision
  - Where the operator of video information processing devices, due to its circumstances, finds it difficult to calculate the minimum period for achieving the retention purpose, the retention period shall be within 30 days after the collection of personal video information (Article 41(2) of the Standard Personal Information Protection Guidelines)

- When entrusting the affairs concerning the installation/operation of video information processing devices, the necessary content must be reflected in the contract in accordance with the applicable procedures and requirements.
  - Content that must be included in a contract for entrusting the affairs of installing/operating video information processing devices of a public institution (Article 26(1) of the Enforcement Decree of the Personal Information Protection Act)
    1. The purpose and scope of the entrusted affairs
    2. Matters concerning restrictions on re-entrustment
    3. Matters concerning measures to secure safety, such as restricting access to the video information
    4. Matters concerning the inspection of the management status of the video information
    5. Matters concerning liability, such as damages, in the event that the entrusted party violates the obligations it must comply with

## Related laws

- Personal Information Protection Act Article 25 (Restrictions on the Installation/Operation of Fixed Video Information Processing Devices), Article 25-2 (Restrictions on the Operation of Mobile Video Information Processing Devices)

## Evidence (examples)

- Video information processing device operation status
- Video information processing device notice board
- Video information processing device operation/management policy
- Video information processing device management screen (account/authority details, video information retention period, etc.)
- Contract with the entrusted party operating video information processing devices and inspection history

## Nonconformity examples

- Where the notice board of video information processing devices is being operated with some of the required notice wording omitted, or where a video information processing device operation/management policy is not established/operated
- Where a video information processing device operation/management policy is established and operated, but management is inadequate, such as being operated without complying with the retention period contrary to the policy content, or where matters described in the policy such as access control and logging for the protection of video information are not complied with
- Where the affairs of installing/operating video information processing devices are entrusted to an external company, but content required by law, such as matters concerning the inspection of the management status of video information and matters concerning liability for damages, is not specified in the video information processing device work entrustment contract
- Where the affairs of installing/operating video information processing devices are entrusted to an external company, but the name and contact information of the entrusted party are omitted from the notice board of the video information processing devices
