## Certification criterion

When personal information is collected/used for marketing purposes such as promotion of goods or services, solicitation of sales, or transmission of advertising information, the purpose must be disclosed so that the data subject can clearly recognize it, and consent must be obtained.

## Key checkpoints

1. When obtaining consent for the processing of personal information in order to promote goods or services to the data subject or to solicit sales, is the data subject notified so that they can clearly recognize this, and is separate consent obtained?
2. When transmitting advertising information for commercial purposes using electronic transmission media, is the recipient's explicit prior consent obtained, and is the recipient's consent status confirmed periodically every 2 years?
3. When a recipient expresses an intention to refuse receipt of, or withdraws prior consent to, advertising information for commercial purposes transmitted via electronic transmission media, is the transmission of advertising information for commercial purposes suspended?
4. When transmitting advertising information for commercial purposes, are the name of the sender, the method of refusing receipt, etc. specifically disclosed, and is transmission avoided during nighttime hours?

## Detailed explanation

> ※ This item is a **relaxed certification criterion (Annex 7-3)** under the special provisions of information security management system certification. The certification criterion/key checkpoints above are the relaxed/adjusted official text, while the detailed explanation/related laws/evidence/nonconformity examples below are the content of the "ISMS-P Certification Criteria Guide" for Annex 7 [3.1.7 Collection/Use of Personal Information for Marketing Purposes] within the same control area (under the relaxed criteria, they apply within the scope of the certification criterion/checkpoints above).

When intending to process personal information in order to promote goods or services to the data subject or to solicit sales, the data subject must be notified so that they can clearly recognize this, and separate consent must be obtained.

- It is prohibited to collect personal information for the purpose of "promotion and marketing" while describing the purpose as "provision of supplementary services," "provision of affiliated services," etc.
- Personal information collected for the purpose of product promotion or marketing must be clearly distinguished from information collected for other purposes, and collected after obtaining consent
- Implement the consent form so that it is easy to recognize, clearly indicating the fact that the relevant personal information may be used to contact the data subject for the promotion or solicitation of sales of goods or services (clearly indicate the content through font size, color, thickness or underlining, etc.)

When transmitting advertising information for commercial purposes using electronic transmission media, the recipient's explicit prior consent must be obtained, and the recipient's consent status must be confirmed periodically every 2 years.

- "Electronic transmission media" refers to mobile phones, landline phones, fax, messengers, email, bulletin boards, etc.
- To transmit advertising information for commercial purposes, the recipient's explicit consent to receive must be obtained by document (including electronic document) or verbal method

※ Concept of advertising information for commercial purposes
- Advertising information for commercial purposes refers to (1) information about the sender and (2) the content of goods or services that the sender will provide, transmitted by the sender for the purpose of gaining economic benefit. A person who causes the transmission is also included in the sender.
- In principle, all information sent by a person conducting business to a customer constitutes advertising information
- Because the purpose of a for-profit corporation's existence is the pursuit of profit, in principle all information transmitted to customers constitutes commercial advertising information; for a non-profit corporation, whether information is commercial advertising is judged according to the nature of the transmitted information
- Even if it is not the promotion of specific goods or services, if the information sent to the recipient constitutes promotion of the sender's image, it may be regarded as advertising information
- Even if the main information is not advertising information, if advertising information is incidentally included, the entirety constitutes advertising information

※ Exceptions to advertising information for commercial purposes
- Information whose purpose is to facilitate, complete, or confirm a transaction previously concluded with the recipient
- Explanation, warranty, product recall, safety or security related information about goods or services used or purchased by the recipient
- One-time information sent at the customer's request (quotations, etc.)
- Information for which the recipient has paid a monetary consideration and applied (newsletters, stock information, livestock product transaction information, etc.)
- Guidance and confirmation information related to the recipient's purchase or use of goods or services provided by the sender, etc.

- Consent for the purpose of promoting goods or services / soliciting sales under Article 22(1)(7) of the Personal Information Protection Act corresponds to consent for the sender to collect/use the recipient's personal information in order to transmit advertising information, and consent under Article 50(1) of the Network Act corresponds to consent to receive the advertising information sent by the sender; the two consents must be distinguished and obtained separately
- Advertising information (app push advertising) must not be transmitted merely because a smartphone app (application) has been downloaded and simply installed; when the app is first executed, whether the recipient consents to receive advertising information must be confirmed, and advertising may be transmitted only after obtaining consent
- However, in cases falling under the exceptions arising from a transactional relationship, advertising information may be transmitted without obtaining consent to receive

※ Exceptions to consent to receive advertising information transmission arising from a transactional relationship
- Where a person who directly collected contact details from a recipient through a transactional relationship for goods, etc. intends to transmit, within 6 months from the date of the transaction, advertising information for commercial purposes on goods, etc. of the same kind as those they process and that the recipient transacted
- Where a telemarketing seller under the "Act on Door-to-Door Sales, Etc." verbally notifies the recipient of the source of collection of personal information and conducts telephone solicitation

- A person who transmits advertising information after obtaining the recipient's consent to receive must confirm the consent status every 2 years from the date the consent to receive was obtained
  - Since this imposes a duty to inform the consenting party of the fact that they had consented to receive, there is no need to obtain re-consent
  - If the recipient does not express any intention, the intention to consent to receive is deemed to be maintained as is
  - Matters to be notified to the recipient when confirming the receipt status
    1. Name of the sender
    2. Date of consent to receive and the fact of having consented to receive
    3. Method of expressing the intention to maintain or withdraw the consent to receive

When a recipient expresses an intention to refuse receipt of, or withdraws prior consent to, the transmission of advertising information for commercial purposes via electronic transmission media, the transmission of advertising information for commercial purposes must be suspended.

- Even if there is a transactional relationship, if the recipient expresses an intention to refuse receipt, transmission of advertising information is prohibited
- Withdrawing membership may also be regarded as an expression of intention to refuse receipt, so advertising information must not be transmitted to a recipient who has withdrawn membership
- Unless the recipient has expressly limited the scope of the withdrawal of consent to receive and the expression of intention to refuse receipt, its effect applies not only to that particular advertisement but to all advertisements sent by that sender

When transmitting advertising information for commercial purposes, the name of the sender, the method of refusing receipt, etc. must be specifically disclosed.

- Matters that must also be notified when transmitting advertising information for commercial purposes using electronic transmission media
  1. Name and contact details of the sender
  2. Matters concerning measures and methods that make it easy to express the intention to refuse receipt or to withdraw consent to receive
  - ※ Comply with Annex 6 of the Enforcement Decree of the Network Act (Matters to be specified and methods of specification for advertising information for commercial purposes)
- During nighttime hours (from 9:00 p.m. to 8:00 a.m. the following day), the transmission of advertising information for commercial purposes using electronic transmission media is prohibited
  - To transmit advertising information during nighttime hours, separate consent to receive is required
  - However, in the case of email, nighttime transmission is possible even without separate consent
  - ※ For detailed content, refer to the "Guide to the Network Act for the Prevention of Illegal Spam"

## Related laws

- Personal Information Protection Act Article 22 (Methods of Obtaining Consent)
- Network Act Article 50 (Restrictions on Transmission of Advertising Information)

## Evidence (examples)

- Online personal information collection forms (website membership registration screen, mobile app membership registration screen, event participation, etc.)
- Offline personal information collection forms (membership application forms, etc.)
- Marketing consent records
- Records of consent to receive advertising information transmission and records of confirmation of consent-to-receive status
- Administrator screen of the advertising information sending system (email, SMS, app push, etc.)
- Wording used to send advertising information
- Personal information processing policy

## Nonconformity examples

- Where personal information is collected for the purpose of "promotion and marketing" while the purpose is vaguely described as "provision of supplementary services," "provision of affiliated services," etc., or where blanket consent is obtained without distinguishing it from personal information collected for other purposes
- Where, in a mobile app, an intention to refuse the transmission of advertising information (app push) was expressed, but advertising app push is carried out due to reasons such as program errors
- Where, on the online membership registration screen, the transmission of advertising information by text message or email is checked by default
- Where the status of consent to receive advertising information is not confirmed every 2 years
- Where advertising information for commercial purposes is transmitted by email without indicating "(Advertisement)" at the beginning of the subject line
