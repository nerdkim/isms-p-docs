## Certification criterion

When a sign or occurrence of a security incident or personal information breach is recognized, the legal obligations of notification and reporting must be complied with, response and recovery must be carried out promptly in accordance with procedures, and after incident analysis, recurrence prevention measures must be established and reflected in the response system.

## Key checkpoints

1. When a sign or occurrence of a security incident or personal information breach is recognized, are response and reporting carried out promptly in accordance with the defined incident response procedures?
2. When a personal information breach occurs, are the procedures for notifying data subjects (users) and reporting to relevant agencies carried out in accordance with the applicable laws?
3. After a security incident is closed, is the cause of the incident analyzed, the results reported, and the results shared with the relevant organizations and personnel?
4. Using the information obtained through incident analysis, are measures established to prevent similar incidents from recurring, and, where necessary, are the incident response procedures and the like modified?

## Detailed explanation

> ※ This item is a **relaxed certification criterion (Annex 7-3)** under the special provisions for information security management system certification. The certification criterion / key checkpoints above are the officially relaxed/adjusted original text; the detailed explanation / related laws / evidence / nonconformity examples below are the content of the "ISMS-P Certification Criteria Guide" for Annex 7 [2.11.5 Incident Response and Recovery] in the same control domain (under the relaxed criterion, they apply within the scope of the certification criterion / checkpoints above).

When a sign or occurrence of a security incident or personal information breach is recognized, response and reporting must be carried out promptly in accordance with the defined incident response procedures.

- Initial incident response and evidence preservation measures
  - Deleting/changing access rights of, or blocking access to, information systems suspected of being compromised
  - Security inspection of internal and external systems such as networks and firewalls, and remediation of vulnerabilities
  - Preserving evidence such as external access logs needed for incident investigation
  - Confirming whether personal information and important information were leaked through log analysis and the like
- Preparing an incident report including the following items and reporting it internally
  - Date and time the incident occurred
  - Reporter and date and time of reporting
  - Incident content (findings, damage details, etc.)
  - Progress of incident response
  - Time taken until incident response, etc.
- If the impact of the incident on the organization is severe, reporting promptly up to top management in accordance with the reporting procedure

When a personal information breach occurs, the procedures for notifying data subjects and reporting to relevant agencies must be carried out in accordance with the applicable laws.

After a security incident is closed, the cause of the incident must be analyzed, the results reported, and shared with the relevant organizations and personnel.

- After a security incident is handled and closed, performing an analysis of the cause of the incident, preparing a results report, and reporting it to the person in charge
- Sharing incident information and the discovered vulnerabilities, causes, and remediation measures with the relevant organizations and personnel

Using the information obtained through incident analysis, measures must be established to prevent similar incidents from recurring, and, where necessary, the incident response procedures and the like must be modified.

- Establishing recurrence prevention measures that use the information obtained through incident analysis to prevent similar incidents from repeating
- According to the analyzed results, where necessary, making changes to the incident response system, such as the incident response procedures and the information protection policies and procedures

## Related laws

- Personal Information Protection Act Article 34 (Notification/Reporting of Leakage etc. of Personal Information)
- Network Act Article 48-3 (Reporting of Security Incidents, etc.), Article 48-4 (Cause Analysis of Security Incidents, etc.)

## Evidence (examples)

- Incident response procedures
- Incident response report
- Incident management ledger
- Personal information leakage report
- Emergency contact list

## Nonconformity examples

- The internal incident response guidelines specify that when a security incident occurs it must be reported to the internal information protection committee and interested departments, but when an incident occurred, the responsible department carried out response measures on its own and did not report to the information protection committee and interested departments
- There was a case where part of a service was suspended due to a security incident suspected to be a recent DDoS attack, but no cause analysis or recurrence prevention measures were established for it
- A personal information leakage incident occurred due to external hacking, but notification and reporting were not carried out within 72 hours on the grounds that the number of leaked personal information records was small
- A leakage of personal information of 1,000 or more data subjects occurred through an internet website bulletin board due to a staff member's mistake, but leakage notification to the relevant data subjects was not carried out
