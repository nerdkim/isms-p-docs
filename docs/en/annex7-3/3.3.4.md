## Certification criterion

When transferring personal information overseas, appropriate protective measures such as consent to the cross-border transfer and disclosure of related matters must be established and implemented.

## Key checkpoints

1. When transferring personal information overseas, does the organization inform the data subject of all the notification matters concerning the cross-border transfer and obtain separate consent, or comply with lawful requirements such as certification or recognition?
2. When informing the data subject about the overseas entrustment of processing/storage of personal information for the conclusion and performance of a contract with the data subject, does the organization inform them by an appropriate method, including all necessary matters?
3. Does the organization conclude a contract concerning the cross-border transfer that includes matters on compliance with personal information protection laws and on the protection of personal information?
4. When transferring personal information overseas, does the organization take the measures necessary for the protection of personal information?

## Detailed explanation

> ※ This item is a **relaxed certification criterion (Annex 7-3)** under the special provisions for information security management system certification. The certification criterion / key checkpoints above are the relaxed/adjusted official original text, while the detailed explanation / related laws / evidence / nonconformity examples below are the content of the "ISMS-P Certification Criteria Guide" for Annex 7 [3.3.4 Cross-Border Transfer of Personal Information], which is the same control domain (under the relaxed criteria, they apply within the scope of the certification criterion / checkpoints above).

- When providing (including cases where it is made available for lookup) / entrusting the processing of / storing (hereinafter referred to as "transfer") personal information to a third party overseas, the organization must inform the data subject of all the notification matters concerning the cross-border transfer and obtain separate consent, or comply with lawful requirements such as certification or recognition.

- Cases where cross-border transfer of personal information is permitted (Article 28-8(1) of the Personal Information Protection Act)

| No | Field | Description |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | Separate consent | Where separate consent to the cross-border transfer has been obtained from the data subject |
| 2 | Basis in law, treaty, etc. | Where there is a special provision on the cross-border transfer of personal information in a statute, a treaty to which the Republic of Korea is a party, or other international agreement |
| 3 | Disclosure in the privacy policy, etc. (entrustment of processing/storage) | Where entrustment of processing / storage of personal information is necessary for the conclusion and performance of a contract with the data subject, and the related matters are disclosed in the privacy policy or notified to the data subject by means such as email |
| 4 | Certification | Where the organization has obtained certification designated and publicly notified by the Protection Commission, such as personal information protection certification (ISMS-P certification), and has taken all of the following measures<br>a. Safety measures necessary for the protection of personal information and measures necessary to guarantee the rights of the data subject<br>b. Measures necessary to implement the certified matters in the country to which the personal information is transferred |
| 5 | Recognition of the destination country, etc. | Where the Protection Commission recognizes that the personal information protection system, the scope of guarantee of data subject rights, the remedy procedures, etc. of the destination country or international organization have a level substantially equivalent to the level of personal information protection under this Act |

- When obtaining consent for the cross-border transfer of personal information, the organization must inform the data subject of and obtain consent on all of the following 5 matters
  - Notification matters when obtaining consent for the cross-border transfer of personal information (Article 28-8(2) of the Personal Information Protection Act)
    1. The items of personal information to be transferred
    2. The country, timing, and method by/at which the personal information is transferred
    3. The name of the person receiving the transferred personal information (in the case of a corporation, its name and contact information)
    4. The purpose of use and the retention/use period of the personal information by the person receiving the transfer
    5. The method, procedure, and effect of refusing the transfer of personal information

- When informing the data subject about the overseas entrustment of processing / storage of personal information for the conclusion and performance of a contract with the data subject, the organization must inform them by an appropriate method, including all necessary matters.

- Methods of informing the data subject
  1. Disclosure in the privacy policy
  2. Methods such as writing (in writing, by email, fax, telephone, text message, or an equivalent method)

- Matters to be informed to the data subject
  1. The items of personal information to be transferred
  2. The country, timing, and method by/at which the personal information is transferred
  3. The name of the person receiving the transferred personal information (in the case of a corporation, its name and contact information)
  4. The purpose of use and the retention/use period of the personal information by the person receiving the transfer
  5. The method, procedure, and effect of refusing the transfer of personal information

- The organization must conclude a contract concerning the cross-border transfer that includes matters on compliance with personal information protection laws and on the protection of personal information.

- Consult in advance with the person receiving the transfer on the following matters and reflect them in the terms of the contract, etc.
  1. Measures to ensure safety for the protection of personal information under Article 30(1) of the Enforcement Decree of the Personal Information Protection Act
  2. Measures concerning grievance handling and dispute resolution regarding personal information infringements
  3. Other measures necessary for the protection of the data subject's personal information

- When transferring the data subject's personal information overseas, the organization must take the measures necessary for the protection of personal information.

- Protective measures to be implemented when transferring personal information overseas
  1. Compliance with the provisions of the Personal Information Protection Act concerning the cross-border transfer of personal information
  2. Compliance with Articles 17 through 19 of the Personal Information Protection Act
  3. Compliance with Chapter 5 (Guarantee of the Rights of Data Subjects) of the Personal Information Protection Act
  4. Measures to ensure safety for the protection of personal information under Article 30(1) of the Enforcement Decree of the Personal Information Protection Act
  5. Measures concerning grievance handling and dispute resolution regarding personal information infringements
  6. Other measures necessary for the protection of the data subject's personal information

## Related laws

- Personal Information Protection Act Article 28-8 (Cross-Border Transfer of Personal Information), Article 28-9 (Order to Suspend Cross-Border Transfer of Personal Information), Article 28-10 (Reciprocity), Article 28-11 (Provisions Applied Mutatis Mutandis)
- Regulations on the Operation of Cross-Border Transfer of Personal Information, etc.

## Evidence (examples)

- Consent form related to the cross-border transfer of personal information
- Contract related to the cross-border transfer of personal information
- Privacy policy
- Notification or disclosure records related to the overseas entrustment of processing / storage of personal information

## Nonconformity examples

- Where, in the course of processing personal information, third-party provision of personal information to an overseas operator occurred, but separate consent to the cross-border transfer of personal information was not obtained, even though the case did not fall under the consent exception requirements such as certification or recognition of the destination country
- Where personal information was entrusted for processing and stored using an overseas cloud service (an overseas region), but related matters such as the country to which it is transferred and the method of transfer were not disclosed in the privacy policy or notified to the data subject
- Where, in obtaining consent for the cross-border transfer of personal information, only the name (company name) of the person receiving the transfer was disclosed, and matters such as the country to which the information is transferred were not informed
