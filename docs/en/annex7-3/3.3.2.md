## Certification criterion

When personal information processing tasks are outsourced to a third party, the content of the outsourced tasks and related matters such as the trustee must be disclosed. In addition, when tasks of promoting goods or services or soliciting sales are outsourced, the content of the outsourced tasks and the trustee must be notified to the data subject.

## Key checkpoints

1. When personal information processing tasks are outsourced to a third party (including re-outsourcing), are the content of the outsourced tasks and the trustee disclosed in an up-to-date manner on the internet website, etc.?
2. When tasks of promoting goods or services or soliciting sales are outsourced, are the content of the outsourced tasks and the trustee notified to the data subject by methods such as written document, email, or text message?

## Detailed explanation

> Note: This item is a relaxed certification criterion (Annex 7-3) under the special provisions of information security management system certification. The certification criterion / key checkpoints above are the relaxed/adjusted official text, while the detailed explanation / related laws / evidence / nonconformity examples below are the content of the "ISMS-P Certification Criteria Guide" for the same control area, Annex 7 [3.3.2 Outsourcing of Personal Information Processing Tasks] (under the relaxed criterion, they apply within the scope of the certification criterion / checkpoints above).

When personal information processing tasks are outsourced to a third party (including re-outsourcing), the content of the outsourced tasks and the trustee must be kept up to date and continuously disclosed on the internet website, etc., so that the data subject can easily check them at any time.

- Matters to be notified to the data subject
  1. The content of the outsourced tasks
  2. The party who receives and processes the personal information processing tasks (trustee)
     - Note: The "trustee" includes a third party (re-trustee) who is again entrusted with the tasks that were entrusted to the party receiving and processing the personal information processing tasks

- Methods of disclosing personal information processing outsourcing matters (Article 28(2) and (3) of the Enforcement Decree of the Personal Information Protection Act)
  - Method of continuously posting the content of the outsourced tasks and the trustee on the outsourcer's internet website
  - Methods of disclosure when it cannot be posted on the internet website (use one or more of the following methods)
    1. Method of posting at a conspicuous location such as the outsourcer's place of business
    2. Method of publishing in the Official Gazette (only where the outsourcer is a public institution) or in a general daily newspaper, general weekly newspaper, or internet newspaper under Article 2, subparagraph 1, items (a)/(c) and subparagraph 2 of the same Article of the "Act on the Promotion of Newspapers, etc." whose main circulation area is the region at or above the city/province (Si/Do) level where the outsourcer's place of business, etc. is located
    3. Method of continuously publishing in a periodical/newsletter/promotional material or invoice, etc. that is issued twice or more per year under the same title and distributed to data subjects
    4. Method of including it in a contract, etc. prepared by the outsourcer and the data subject to provide goods or services and issuing it to the data subject

- Points to note
  - Even if there are many trustees, all such trustee names must be listed and disclosed
  - If re-outsourcing exists, matters regarding the re-outsourcing must also be disclosed together
  - If the content of the outsourced tasks or the trustee is changed, the changed content must be reflected without delay and disclosed through the internet website, etc.

When tasks of promoting goods or services or soliciting sales are outsourced, the content of the outsourced tasks and the trustee must be notified to the data subject by methods such as written document, email, or text message.

- Notification method : written document, etc. (written document, email, fax, telephone, text message, or an equivalent method)
- Matters to be notified : the content of the outsourced tasks, the trustee

Note: For matters regarding outsourcing contracts related to the outsourcing of personal information processing tasks, the outsourcer's consent upon re-outsourcing, and the management/supervision of trustees, refer to the certification criteria of area 2.3 (Security of External Parties).

## Related laws

- Personal Information Protection Act Article 26 (Restrictions on Processing of Personal Information Following Outsourcing of Tasks)

## Evidence (examples)

- Personal information processing policy (disclosure details related to outsourcing of personal information processing tasks)
- Personal information collection form
- Personal information processing outsourcing contract
- Records of notification to data subjects related to the outsourcing of tasks for promoting goods or services or soliciting sales

## Nonconformity examples

- The case where personal information processing outsourcing matters are disclosed in the personal information processing policy on the website, but some trustees and the content of the outsourced tasks are omitted
- The case where, while outsourcing tasks of promoting goods or services or soliciting sales, the content of the outsourced tasks and the trustee are not notified to the data subject by methods such as written document, but this is substituted by disclosure in the personal information processing policy
- The case where the personal information processing trustee has changed due to the termination of the contract with the existing personal information processing trustee, but this is not reflected in the personal information processing policy without delay
- The case where the party entrusted with personal information processing tasks re-outsources the relevant tasks to a third party, but the matters regarding the re-outsourcing are not disclosed on the internet website, etc.
