## Certification criterion

To verify whether vulnerabilities of information systems are exposed, vulnerability inspections shall be performed regularly, and discovered vulnerabilities shall be remediated promptly. In addition, the occurrence of the latest security vulnerabilities shall be continuously identified, and their impact on information systems shall be analyzed and addressed.

## Key checkpoints

1. Has a procedure for inspecting information system vulnerabilities been established, and are inspections performed regularly?
2. Are actions taken on discovered vulnerabilities, and are the results reported to the responsible manager?
3. Is the occurrence of the latest security vulnerabilities continuously identified, and is their impact on information systems analyzed and addressed?
4. Is the history of vulnerability inspections recorded and managed so that protective measures are prepared for problems such as the recurrence of vulnerabilities identified in the previous year?

## Detailed explanation

> ※ This item is a **relaxed certification criterion (Annex 7-3)** under the exception for information security management system certification. The certification criterion / key checkpoints above are the relaxed/adjusted official original text, while the detailed explanation / related laws / evidence / nonconformity examples below are content from the 「ISMS-P Certification Criteria Guide」 for the same control area, Annex 7 [2.11.2 Vulnerability Inspection and Remediation] (under the relaxed criterion, they apply within the scope of the certification criterion / checkpoints above).

### A procedure for inspecting information system vulnerabilities shall be established, and inspections shall be performed regularly.

- Matters to be included in the vulnerability inspection procedure
  - Targets of vulnerability inspection (e.g., servers, network equipment, etc.)
  - Vulnerability inspection cycle (considering legal requirements, importance, etc.)
  - Designation of persons in charge and responsible managers for vulnerability inspection
  - Vulnerability inspection procedures and methods, etc.
  - Remediation criteria according to importance
  - Procedure for reporting vulnerability inspection results
  - Security reviews of unremediated vulnerabilities, etc.
  - Other matters necessary for the prevention of and recovery from security incidents, etc.
- Targets of vulnerability inspection
  - Configuration and setting vulnerabilities of network systems such as routers and switches
  - Server OS security setting vulnerabilities
  - Vulnerabilities of security systems such as firewalls
  - Application vulnerabilities
  - Web service vulnerabilities
  - Vulnerabilities of smart devices and mobile services (mobile apps, etc.), etc.
- When conducting vulnerability inspections, consider performing penetration testing according to the size of the company and the importance of the information held
- Personal information controllers must establish/implement matters concerning vulnerability inspection for preventing the leakage and theft of personal information by including them in the internal management plan (Standards for Measures to Ensure the Safety of Personal Information, Article 4(1)(10))

### Actions shall be taken on discovered vulnerabilities, and the results shall be reported to the responsible manager.

- Prepare a report that includes the inspection date/time, inspection targets, inspection methods, inspection details and results, findings, and actions taken, so that a history can be managed for vulnerability inspections
- After completing response actions for each vulnerability, confirm completion through follow-up inspections, etc.
- For vulnerabilities that unavoidably cannot be remediated, clearly identify the reasons and report the associated risks, supplementary measures, etc. to the responsible manager

### The occurrence of the latest security vulnerabilities shall be continuously identified, and their impact on information systems shall be analyzed and addressed.

- In addition to regular security vulnerability inspections, continuously identify the latest security vulnerabilities
- When the latest security vulnerabilities are discovered, analyze their impact on information systems and take response actions as necessary

### The history of vulnerability inspections shall be recorded and managed to prepare protective measures for problems such as the recurrence of vulnerabilities identified in the previous year.

- Record management of vulnerability inspection history
- When conducting vulnerability inspections, compare and analyze against previous inspection results to check whether vulnerabilities have recurred
- If similar vulnerabilities are recurring, conduct root cause analysis and prepare measures to prevent recurrence

## Related laws

- Personal Information Protection Act, Article 29 (Duty of Safety Measures)
- Standards for Measures to Ensure the Safety of Personal Information, Article 4 (Establishment/Implementation and Inspection of the Internal Management Plan), Article 6 (Access Control)

## Evidence (examples)

- Vulnerability inspection plan
- Vulnerability inspection result report (web, mobile app, server, network system, security system, DBMS, etc.)
- Vulnerability inspection history
- Vulnerability remediation plan
- Vulnerability remediation completion report
- Penetration testing plan / result report

## Nonconformity examples

- The case where internal regulations require technical vulnerability inspection of major systems at least once a year, but some of the major systems are omitted from the scope of vulnerability inspection
- The case where supplementary measures for vulnerabilities discovered in vulnerability inspections have not been implemented, or where there is no record of feasibility review and approval for vulnerabilities that cannot be remediated within a short period
