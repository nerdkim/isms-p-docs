## Certification criterion

Personal information shall be used or provided only within the purpose notified to and consented by the data subject at the time of collection or within the scope grounded in statute. When intending to use or provide it beyond such scope, the organization shall obtain additional consent from the data subject or confirm whether it is a lawful case under relevant statutes, and shall establish and implement appropriate protective measures.

## Key checkpoints

1. Is personal information used or provided only within the purpose consented to by the data subject at the time of initial collection or within the scope grounded in statute?
2. When personal information has been provided by a personal information controller, is it used or provided only within the scope of the purpose for which it was provided?
3. When personal information is used or provided beyond the scope of the collection purpose or the purpose for which it was provided by a personal information controller, is this limited to cases where separate consent is obtained from the data subject or where there is a legal basis?
4. When personal information is provided to a third party for a purpose other than the intended one, is the recipient requested to restrict the purpose and method of use, or to arrange the measures necessary to secure safety?
5. When a public institution uses personal information for a purpose other than the intended one or provides it to a third party, are the necessary matters concerning the legal basis, purpose, and scope of such use or provision posted in the Official Gazette or on the internet homepage, etc.?
6. When a public institution, etc. uses personal information for a purpose other than the intended one or provides it to a third party, is a procedure in place such as recording and managing it in the register of use and third-party provision beyond the purpose?

## Detailed explanation

> * This item is a **relaxed certification criterion (Annex 7-3)** under the special provisions for information security management system certification. The certification criterion and key checkpoints above are the relaxed/adjusted official original text, while the detailed explanation, related laws, evidence, and nonconformity examples below are the content of the "ISMS-P Certification Criteria Guide" for the same control domain, Annex 7 [3.2.4 Use and Provision of Personal Information Beyond the Purpose] (under the relaxed criteria, they apply within the scope of the certification criterion and checkpoints above).

- Personal information shall be used or provided only within the purpose consented to by the data subject at the time of initial collection or within the scope grounded in statute.
  - Using or providing personal information beyond the scope for which the purpose of use/provision was notified to the data subject and consent was obtained, or beyond the scope permitted for use/provision by the Personal Information Protection Act or other statutes, is prohibited.
    - * Examples of use/provision of personal information beyond the purpose
      - Using personal information entered to participate in a customer satisfaction survey, promotional event, or prize event to send advertising materials for the company's own discount sale event without obtaining prior consent
      - A home shopping company using customer information collected to deliver ordered products, without the data subject's consent, by providing it to an affiliated condominium company for sending condominium sales promotional materials

- When personal information has been provided by a personal information controller, it shall be used or provided only within the scope of the purpose for which it was provided.
  - A person who has been provided with personal information is prohibited from using it for a purpose other than the purpose for which it was provided or from providing it to a third party.

- When personal information is used or provided beyond the scope of the collection purpose or the purpose for which it was provided by a personal information controller, this shall be limited to cases where separate consent is obtained from the data subject or where there is a legal basis.
  - Cases where personal information may be used/provided for a purpose other than the intended one (however, excluding cases where there is a concern that it may unfairly infringe upon the interests of the data subject or a third party)

| No. | Cases where use/provision beyond the purpose is permitted | Public institution | Other than public institution |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | Where separate consent has been obtained from the data subject | O | O |
| 2 | Where there are special provisions in other statutes | O | O |
| 3 | Where it is deemed manifestly necessary for the urgent life, body, or property interests of the data subject or a third party | O | O |
| 4 | Where the affairs under the jurisdiction prescribed by other statutes cannot be performed unless personal information is used for a purpose other than the intended one or provided to a third party, and it has undergone deliberation/resolution by the Personal Information Protection Commission | O | - |
| 5 | Where it is necessary to provide it to a foreign government or international organization for the implementation of a treaty or other international agreement | O | - |
| 6 | Where it is necessary for the investigation of a crime and the institution and maintenance of a prosecution | O | - |
| 7 | Where it is necessary for the performance of the trial affairs of a court | O | - |
| 8 | Where it is necessary for the execution of a punishment, custody, or protective disposition | O | - |
| 9 | Where it is urgently necessary for public safety and security, such as public health | O | O |

  - Matters to be notified when consent is obtained in order to use/provide personal information for a purpose other than the intended one
    1. The recipient of the personal information
    2. The purpose of use of the personal information (in the case of provision, the recipient's purpose of use)
    3. The items of personal information to be used or provided
    4. The retention and use period of the personal information (in the case of provision, the recipient's retention and use period)
    5. The fact that the data subject has the right to refuse consent, and where there is any disadvantage resulting from the refusal of consent, the content of such disadvantage
  - A person who has been provided with personal information from another personal information controller shall not use it for a purpose other than the purpose for which it was provided or provide it to a third party, except in any of the following cases:
    1. Where separate consent has been obtained from the data subject
    2. Where there are special provisions in other statutes

- When personal information is provided to a third party for a purpose other than the intended one, the recipient shall be requested to restrict the purpose and method of use, etc., or to arrange the measures necessary to secure safety.
  - Restricting the purpose of use, method of use, period of use, form of use, etc. for the recipient of the personal information
  - Requesting, in a document (including an electronic document), that specific measures necessary to secure the safety of the personal information be arranged
  - Clarifying the responsibility relationship regarding the safety measures for personal information with the recipient of the relevant personal information

- When a public institution uses personal information for a purpose other than the intended one or provides it to a third party, the necessary matters concerning the legal basis, purpose, and scope of such use or provision shall be posted in the Official Gazette or on the internet homepage, etc.
  - Cases where posting in the Official Gazette or on the internet homepage is not required for use/provision beyond the purpose (exceptions)
    1. When using/providing beyond the purpose on the basis of the data subject's consent
    2. When using/providing beyond the purpose for the investigation of a crime and the institution and maintenance of a prosecution
  - Matters to be posted in the Official Gazette or on the internet homepage
    1. The date of the use, etc. beyond the purpose
    2. The legal basis for the use, etc. beyond the purpose
    3. The purpose of the use, etc. beyond the purpose
    4. The items of personal information used, etc. beyond the purpose
  - The timing and period of posting in the Official Gazette or on the internet homepage
    1. Timing of posting: within 30 days from the date of use/provision beyond the purpose
    2. Period of posting: at least 10 days when posted on the internet homepage

- When a public institution, etc. uses personal information for a purpose other than the intended one or provides it to a third party, it shall record and manage it in the register of use and third-party provision beyond the purpose.
  - In the case of a public institution, when personal information is used for a purpose other than the intended one or provided to a third party, it must be recorded and managed in the 'register of use and third-party provision of personal information beyond the purpose'.
    - * Matters to be recorded/managed in the 'register of use and third-party provision of personal information beyond the purpose' (Form No. 1 of the "Notice on the Method of Processing Personal Information")
      1. The name of the personal information or personal information file used or provided
      2. The name of the using institution or the recipient institution
      3. The purpose of use or the purpose for which it is provided
      4. The legal basis for the use or provision
      5. The items of personal information used or provided
      6. The date, cycle, or period of the use or provision
      7. The form of the use or provision
      8. Where a request has been made to impose restrictions or to arrange necessary measures for the protection of personal information, the content thereof
  - Establishing procedures to systematically respond to requests for the provision of data such as personal information from judicial or government agencies (warrants, orders, requests for submission of data, etc.)
    - * Considerations when establishing procedures for responding to requests for the provision of data such as personal information from judicial or government agencies
      - Establishing/implementing response procedures such as the legal basis for provision, the request intake channel (including designation of the responsible organization and person in charge), requests via official documents (warrants, etc.), verification of the requesting agency's person in charge, the principle of minimum information provision, internal reporting and approval procedures, and preservation of related records
      - Using safe procedures and methods so that personal information is not leaked/exposed during the provision process, and recording the details of personal information provision and retaining them for a certain period, etc.

## Related laws

- Personal Information Protection Act Article 18 (Restrictions on Use and Provision of Personal Information Beyond the Purpose)
- Personal Information Protection Act Article 19 (Restrictions on Use and Provision by a Person Who Has Been Provided with Personal Information)

## Evidence (examples)

- Details of use and third-party provision of personal information beyond the purpose (including related evidence such as request forms)
- Register of use and third-party provision of personal information beyond the purpose (in the case of a public institution)
- Details of posting on the homepage or in the Official Gazette (in the case of a public institution)
- Guidelines for responding to requests for the provision of data
- Official documents requesting the provision of data, details of personal information provision, register, etc.

## Nonconformity examples

- A case where personal information collected for the purpose of product delivery was used for mail-order sales advertising of the company's own products without prior consent
- A case where personal information collected to participate in a customer satisfaction survey or prize event was used to send advertisements for the company's own discount sale event
- A case where a public institution, while providing a civil petitioner's personal information to another institution beyond the purpose on the basis of another statute, did not post the related matters in the Official Gazette or on the internet homepage
- A case where a public institution, while providing personal information to a police station for the purpose of criminal investigation, did not record the related matters in the 'register of use and third-party provision of personal information beyond the purpose'
