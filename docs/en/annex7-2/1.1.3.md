## Certification criterion

The top management shall establish and operate a working-level organization for the effective implementation of information protection.

## Key checkpoints

1. To systematically carry out the organization's information protection activities, is a working-level organization with expertise established and operated?

## Detailed explanation

> ※ This item is a **relaxed certification criterion (Annex 7-2)** under the special provisions for information protection management system certification. The certification criterion / key checkpoints above are the relaxed/adjusted official original text, while the detailed explanation / related laws / evidence / nonconformity examples below are the content of the "ISMS-P Certification Criteria Guide" for [1.1.3 Organization Composition] of Annex 7, which belongs to the same control domain (under the relaxed criteria, it applies within the scope of the certification criterion / checkpoints above).

Taking into account characteristics such as the size of the organization and the importance of its business, the basis for the organizational composition needed to build and continuously operate the information protection and personal information protection management system shall be specified in documents such as the information protection and personal information protection policy, and a working-level organization with expertise shall be established and operated.

- Matters concerning the composition/operation of the information protection and personal information protection organization, such as the Chief Information Security Officer, the Chief Privacy Officer, the personal information protection working-level organization, and committees, are specified in the policy documents, internal management plan, etc.
- The form of composition and size of the working-level organization are determined by considering the size of the company-wide organization, the business, the characteristics of the services, the importance and sensitivity of the information and personal information handled, laws and regulations, etc.
- The working-level organization may be composed as a dedicated organization or a concurrently-assigned organization, but even if it is composed as a concurrently-assigned organization, roles and responsibilities must be officially assigned so that it can carry out its role substantively.
- The members of the working-level organization are composed of staff with information protection and personal information protection expertise and with a high level of understanding and experience of various services (holding relevant degrees and certifications, having practical experience, having completed relevant training, etc.)

A committee that can review, approve, and make decisions on important information protection and personal information protection matters across the entire organization shall be established and operated.

- The committee is composed of executives, officers, the Chief Information Security Officer, the Chief Privacy Officer, and other executives and staff who have substantive authority to review and make decisions, so that it can represent the interests within the organization and make decisions with respect to information protection and personal information protection.
- The committee is convened regularly or as needed depending on the matter.
- The committee carries out review, approval, and decision-making on major matters across the entire organization.

※ Major matters requiring review and decision-making by the committee (examples)
- Establishment/revision of information protection and personal information protection policies/guidelines
- Risk assessment results
- Allocation of information protection and personal information protection budget and resources
- Measures against internal security incidents and major violations
- Internal audit results, etc.

For company-wide information protection and personal information protection activities, a working-level consultative body composed of information protection and personal information protection officers and officers of each department shall be established and operated.

- The members of the working-level consultative body, the organizational structure, etc. are determined according to the size of the organization and the importance of the services within the scope of the management system.
- The working-level consultative body shares/coordinates/reviews/improves matters related to information protection and personal information protection at the working level, and where decision-making and management support are needed, refers them to the committee for discussion.

## Related laws

- Personal Information Protection Act Article 29 (Duty of safety measures)
- Standards for Measures to Ensure the Safety of Personal Information Article 4 (Establishment/implementation and inspection of the internal management plan)

## Evidence (examples)

- Information protection and personal information protection committee regulations/minutes
- Information protection and personal information protection working-level consultative body regulations/minutes
- Information protection and personal information protection organization chart
- Internal management plan
- Job description

## Nonconformity examples

- A case where an information protection and personal information protection committee was established, but it does not include executives such as officers and is composed of heads of working-level departments, so it cannot decide on matters concerning the protection of the organization's important information and personal information.
- A case where an information protection and personal information protection working-level consultative body composed of heads (team-leader level) of important information processing departments and personal information processing departments was established under internal guidelines, but there has been no operating record for a long period.
- A case where an information protection and personal information protection committee was convened, but major information protection and personal information protection matters such as the annual information protection and personal information protection plan and training plan, budget, and personnel were not reviewed and decided.
- A case where an information protection committee was established and operated for deliberation/resolution on information protection and personal information protection matters, but only the operations and IT security organizations participate and the personal information protection organization does not participate, so matters concerning personal information protection cannot be decided.
