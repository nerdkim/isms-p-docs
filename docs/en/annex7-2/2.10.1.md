## Certification criterion

An organization shall establish a cooperation system with external agencies and others so as to prevent intrusion incidents and personal information breaches and to respond promptly when an incident occurs.

## Key checkpoints

1. When an incident response system is established/operated through an external agency such as a security control (monitoring) service, are the details of the incident response procedures reflected in the contract?
2. Is a cooperation system established with external experts, specialized companies, specialized agencies, and others for the monitoring, response, and handling of intrusion incidents?

## Detailed explanation

> Note: This item is a relaxed certification criterion (Annex 7-2) under the special provisions for information security management system certification. The certification criterion and key checkpoints above are the relaxed/adjusted official text, while the detailed explanation / related laws / evidence / nonconformity examples below are the content of the "ISMS-P Certification Criteria Guide" for Annex 7 [2.11.1 Establishment of an Incident Prevention and Response System], which is the same control area (under the relaxed criteria, these apply within the scope of the certification criterion and checkpoints above).

To prevent intrusion incidents and personal information breach incidents and to respond promptly and effectively when an incident occurs, a system and procedures shall be prepared that include the following content.

- Definition and scope of intrusion incidents (personal information breach incidents, hacking incidents, denial-of-service attacks, etc.)
- Types and severity of intrusion incidents
- Procedures and methods for declaring an intrusion incident
- Contact system such as an emergency contact network
- Intrusion incident detection system
- Recording and reporting procedures when an intrusion incident occurs
- Procedures for reporting and notifying intrusion incidents (relevant agencies, data subjects and users, etc.)
- Preparation of intrusion incident reports
- Response and recovery procedures according to the severity and type of the intrusion incident
- Composition, responsibilities, and roles of the intrusion incident recovery organization
- Intrusion incident recovery equipment and resource procurement
- Intrusion incident response and recovery drills, and drill scenarios
- Plans for utilizing external experts and specialized agencies
- Other matters necessary for the prevention and recovery of security incidents, etc.

When an incident response system is established/operated through an external agency such as a security control (monitoring) service, the details of the incident response procedures shall be reflected in the contract (SLA, etc.).

- Scope of the security control (monitoring) service
- Procedures for reporting and responding when signs of intrusion are discovered
- Procedures for reporting and responding when an intrusion incident occurs
- Matters concerning responsibilities and roles arising from the occurrence of an intrusion incident, etc.

A cooperation system shall be established with external experts, specialized companies, specialized agencies, and others for the monitoring, response, and handling of intrusion incidents.

&lt;Source&gt; Private Sector Intrusion Incident Response Guide (Ministry of Science and ICT, KISA)

## Related laws

- Personal Information Protection Act Article 34 (Notification/Reporting of Breach, etc. of Personal Information)
- Network Act Article 48-3 (Reporting of Intrusion Incidents, etc.), Article 48-4 (Analysis of Causes of Intrusion Incidents, etc.)

## Evidence (examples)

- Intrusion incident response guidelines/procedures/manuals
- Intrusion incident response organization chart and emergency contact network
- Security control (monitoring) service contract (SLA, etc.)

## Nonconformity examples

- Where the incident response organization and response procedures in preparation for intrusion incidents are not clearly defined
- Where the internal guidelines and procedures establish and specify response procedures for each stage of an intrusion incident (before the incident, recognition, handling, recovery, reporting, etc.), but some or all of the reporting/notification procedures and the response and recovery procedures according to the type and severity of the incident are not established when an intrusion incident occurs
- Where the intrusion incident response organization chart and emergency contact network are not kept up to date, or the roles and responsibilities of each person in charge are not clearly defined
- Where the agency names, websites, contact information, etc. in the list of external agency contacts for intrusion incident reporting/notification and response cooperation are stated incorrectly, or information related to some agencies is missing or not kept up to date
- Where intrusion incident detection and response are entrusted to and operated by a related organization such as an external specialized security control (monitoring) company, but the mutual roles and scope of responsibility for incident response are not clearly defined in the contract or SLA
- Where incident response procedures have been established, but the criteria and timing for reporting personal information breaches do not comply with legal requirements
