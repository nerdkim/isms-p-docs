## Certification criterion

The matters that must be notified to the data subject, such as the use/provision records of personal information, must be identified and their content notified periodically.

## Key checkpoints

1. Where the organization is subject to a legal obligation, does it periodically notify the data subject of the use/provision records of personal information, or of the method to access the information system where such records can be verified?
2. Do the notification items for the use/provision records of personal information include all of the legally required items?

## Detailed explanation

> ※ This item is a **relaxed certification criterion (Annex 7-2)** under the special provisions for information security management system certification. The certification criterion/key checkpoints above are the officially relaxed/adjusted original text, and the detailed explanation/related laws/evidence/nonconformity examples below are the content of the "ISMS-P Certification Criteria Guide" for Annex 7 [3.5.3 Notification to the data subject] in the same control domain (under the relaxed criteria, they apply within the scope of the certification criterion/checkpoints above).

Where the organization is subject to a legal obligation, it must periodically notify the data subject of the use/provision records of personal information, or of the method to access the information system where such records can be verified.

- Legal requirements related to notification of the use/provision records of personal information

| Field | Value |
| --- | --- |
| Parties subject to the notification obligation | 1. A party that processes sensitive information or unique identifying information concerning 50,000 or more data subjects<br>2. A party that processes personal information concerning 1,000,000 or more data subjects<br>※ The number of data subjects is calculated based on the daily average over the immediately preceding 3 months as of the end of the previous year (however, effective from January 1, 2024) |
| Notification method | 1. A method by which the data subject can easily verify the notified content, such as writing/email/telephone/text message<br>2. A method of informing through a notification window so that the data subject can easily become aware during the process of providing goods and services (limited to cases of notifying the method to access the information system where the use/provision records of personal information under Article 20-2(1) of the Act can be verified) |
| Notification cycle | At least once a year |
| Exceptions to notification | 1. A data subject who has expressed an intention to refuse the notification<br>2. Where the personal information controller has processed the personal information of its own officers and employees for the performance of its duties, the relevant data subject<br>3. Where the personal information controller has processed personal information such as the contact details of officers, employees, or individuals of another public institution, corporation, or organization for the performance of its duties, the relevant data subject<br>4. The data subject of personal information used/provided pursuant to special provisions in a law or to comply with a legal obligation<br>5. The data subject of personal information used/provided by a public institution for the performance of its duties under its jurisdiction as prescribed by statute, etc.<br>※ Where personal information that can be used to notify the data subject, such as contact details, has not been collected/retained |

The notification items for the use/provision records of personal information must include all of the legally required items.

- Notification items for the use/provision records of personal information
  1. The purpose of collection/use of personal information and the items of personal information collected
  2. The party to whom the personal information was provided, the purpose of provision, and the items of personal information provided (however, information provided pursuant to Articles 13, 13-2, and 13-4 of the "Protection of Communications Secrets Act" and Article 83(3) of the "Telecommunications Business Act" is excluded)

## Related laws

- Personal Information Protection Act Article 20-2 (Notification of the use/provision records of personal information)

## Evidence (examples)

- Records of notification of the use/provision records of personal information
- Form and wording for notification of the use/provision records of personal information

## Nonconformity examples

- A case where the personal information stored/managed at a daily average over the immediately preceding 3 months as of the end of the previous year is 1,000,000 or more, thereby falling under the parties subject to the obligation to notify the use/provision records of personal information, yet the use/provision records of personal information were not notified in the current year
- A case where, instead of directly notifying each individual data subject of the use/provision records of personal information, only a simple pop-up window or a separate notice on the website was used to provide the information
