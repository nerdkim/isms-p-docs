## Certification criterion

To protect personal information and critical information, the organization shall establish encryption targets, cryptographic strength, cryptographic usage policies, and cryptographic key management procedures that reflect legal requirements, and shall apply encryption when personal information and critical information are stored, transmitted, or delivered.

## Key checkpoints

1. To protect personal information and critical information, has a cryptographic policy that reflects legal requirements and includes encryption targets, cryptographic strength, cryptographic usage, etc. been established?
2. In accordance with the cryptographic policy, is encryption performed when personal information and critical information are stored, transmitted, or delivered?
3. Have procedures for the generation, use, storage, distribution, change, recovery, and destruction of cryptographic keys been established/implemented?
4. Are cryptographic keys kept in a separate secure location so that they can be recovered when necessary, and are access rights for the use of cryptographic keys minimized?

## Detailed explanation

> ※ This item is a **relaxed certification criterion (Annex 7-2)** under the special provisions for information security management system certification. The certification criterion/key checkpoints above are the officially relaxed/adjusted original text, and the detailed explanation/related laws/evidence/nonconformity examples below are the content from the "ISMS-P Certification Criteria Guide" for Annex 7 [2.7.1 Application of Cryptographic Policy], which belongs to the same control domain (under the relaxed criterion, it applies within the scope of the certification criterion/checkpoints above).

To protect personal information and critical information, a cryptographic policy that reflects legal requirements and includes encryption targets, cryptographic strength, cryptographic usage, etc. shall be established.

- Encryption targets: defined according to legal requirements and the sensitivity and importance of the information being processed

**Personal information subject to encryption under the Personal Information Protection Act**

| Field | | Personal information of data subjects who are not users | Personal information of users |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| During transmission/reception over an information and communications network | Information and communications network, internet network | - | Authentication information (passwords, biometric information, etc.) Personal information |
| When stored | Regardless of storage location | - | Authentication information (passwords, biometric information, etc.) ※ However, passwords shall use one-way encryption / Resident registration number ※ Encrypted in accordance with Article 24-2 (2) of the Act |
| When stored | Internet zone, DMZ | Unique identifying information | Resident registration number, passport number, driver's license number, alien registration number, credit card number, account number, biometric information ※ Regardless of storage location |
| When stored | Internal network | ※ However, when unique identifying information other than the resident registration number is stored on the internal network, whether and to what extent encryption is applied may be determined and implemented according to the results of a privacy impact assessment or a risk analysis | (same as above) |
| When stored on a personal information handler's computer, mobile device, auxiliary storage medium, etc. | | Unique identifying information, biometric information | Personal information |

※ However, with respect to authentication information (passwords, biometric information, etc.) during transmission/reception over an information and communications network, for those subject to the former Standards for Measures to Ensure the Safety of Personal Information, this takes effect on 2024.9.15.

- Encryption algorithm: select a safe encryption algorithm and security strength, taking into account legal requirements, etc.

※ Safe encryption algorithms (examples) (refer to the "Guide to Encryption Measures for Personal Information")

| Field | Algorithm name |
| --- | --- |
| Symmetric-key encryption algorithm | SEED, ARIA-128/192/256, AES-128/192/256, HIGHT, LEA, etc. |
| Public-key encryption algorithm | RSAES-OAEP, etc. |
| One-way encryption algorithm | SHA-256/384/512, etc. |

In accordance with the cryptographic policy, encryption shall be performed when personal information and critical information are stored, transmitted, or delivered.

- Select and apply an encryption method taking into account the encryption location, system characteristics, etc.

※ Encryption methods (examples)

| Field | Encryption method |
| --- | --- |
| During transmission/reception over an information and communications network | 1. Install an SSL (Secure Socket Layer) certificate on the web server to encrypt information for transmission and reception<br>2. Install an encryption application on the web server to encrypt information for transmission and reception<br>3. Other use of encryption technology: VPN, PGP, etc. |
| When stored in a personal information processing system, etc. | 1. Application-level encryption (API method)<br>2. Database server encryption (Plug-in method)<br>3. DBMS built-in encryption (TDE method)<br>4. Calling DBMS encryption functions<br>5. Operating system encryption (file encryption, etc.)<br>6. Other use of encryption technology |
| When stored on a work computer or mobile device | 1. Document-tool built-in encryption (using the password setting function provided by office software, etc.)<br>2. Encryption using an encryption utility<br>3. Application of DRM (Digital Right Management) technology, etc. |

## Related laws

- Personal Information Protection Act Article 24-2 (Restrictions on the Processing of Resident Registration Numbers), Article 29 (Duty of Safety Measures)
- Standards for Measures to Ensure the Safety of Personal Information Article 7 (Encryption of Personal Information)

## Evidence (examples)

- Cryptographic control policy (targets, methods, algorithms, etc.)
- Status of encryption application (when storing and transmitting)
- Risk analysis results (when encryption is not applied to unique identifying information other than the resident registration number on the internal network)
- Encryption solution management screen

## Nonconformity examples

- Cases where matters such as encryption targets reflecting legal requirements related to cryptographic control, cryptographic strength, encryption methods for storage and transmission, and the roles and responsibilities of encryption-related personnel are not appropriately specified in the internal policy/guidelines
- Cases where, in establishing the cryptographic policy, the laws applicable to the company are misapplied so that legal requirements related to encryption are not complied with (for example, storing a user's account number without applying encryption)
- Cases where one-way encryption was applied to the passwords of personal information handlers and data subjects, but the insecure MD5 algorithm was used
- Cases where a personal information controller applied a secure server to an internet shopping mall in accordance with relevant laws and internal regulations, but encryption measures were omitted in some sections where a user's personal information is transmitted, such as member information inquiry and modification, password recovery, and password change
- Cases where passwords for accessing information systems, authentication key values, etc. are stored in plaintext within system configuration files and source code
