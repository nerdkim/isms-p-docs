## Certification criterion

When processing pseudonymized information, the organization shall comply with legal requirements such as purpose limitation, combination restrictions, safety measures, and prohibition obligations, and shall establish/implement pseudonymization procedures to ensure pseudonymization at an appropriate level.

## Key checkpoints

1. When processing pseudonymized information, has a procedure been established for processing pseudonymized information appropriately, including purpose limitation, pseudonymization methods and criteria, adequacy review, prohibition of re-identification, and actions to be taken when re-identification occurs?
2. When personal information is pseudonymized for use/provision, is pseudonymization performed at an appropriate level so that a specific individual cannot be identified without the use/combination of additional information?
3. When pseudonymized information is combined with that of another personal information controller, is the combination carried out through a specialized combination institution or a data specialized institution?
4. When processing pseudonymized information, are the technical/administrative and physical measures necessary to secure safety being taken, such as deleting additional information or storing/managing it separately, and preparing/retaining related records?
5. Considering the purpose of processing pseudonymized information and other factors, is the processing period of pseudonymized information set to an appropriate period, and is the information destroyed without delay once that period has elapsed?
6. When personal information is anonymized, is it anonymized at an appropriate level so that a specific individual can no longer be identified even when using other information, given reasonable consideration of time/cost/technology, etc.?

## Detailed explanation

> * This item is a **relaxed certification criterion (Annex 7-2)** under the special provisions for information security management system certification. The certification criterion/key checkpoints above are the officially relaxed/adjusted text, and the detailed explanation/related laws/evidence/nonconformity examples below are drawn from the content of the "ISMS-P Certification Criteria Guidebook" for Annex 7 [3.2.5 Processing of Pseudonymized Information], which belongs to the same control area (under the relaxed criteria, they apply within the scope of the certification criterion/checkpoints above).

When processing pseudonymized information, a procedure shall be established/implemented to process pseudonymized information appropriately, including purpose limitation, pseudonymization methods and criteria, adequacy review, prohibition of re-identification, and actions to be taken when re-identification occurs.

- "Pseudonymization" means processing personal information such that a specific individual cannot be identified without additional information, by means such as deleting part of the personal information or replacing part or all of it
- "Pseudonymized information" means information that, having been created by pseudonymizing personal information, cannot identify a specific individual without the use/combination of additional information to restore it to its original state, and is included within the category of personal information
- Under the special provisions on the processing of pseudonymized information, pseudonymized information may be processed, including used/provided, without the consent of the data subject for three purposes: compilation of statistics, scientific research, preservation of records in the public interest, etc.
- Since the pseudonymized information that may be processed without the consent of the data subject under the special provisions on the processing of pseudonymized information is limited to purposes such as compilation of statistics, scientific research, and preservation of records in the public interest, pseudonymizing and retaining personal information held without an established processing purpose cannot be regarded as processing based on the special provisions on the processing of pseudonymized information
- Establishment and implementation of pseudonymization procedures to ensure pseudonymization at an appropriate level, considering the processing purpose, usage environment, data characteristics, etc.

- Example of pseudonymization procedure (Pseudonymized Information Processing Guidelines)

| Step | Category | Description |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | Advance preparation (setting the purpose, etc.) | / Set the purpose of processing pseudonymized information specifically and clearly among the three purposes prescribed by the Personal Information Protection Act (compilation of statistics, scientific research, preservation of records in the public interest, etc.)<br>/ Select the pseudonymization targets by clarifying the type and scope of information needed to achieve the processing purpose<br>/ Review the suitability of the processing purpose<br>/ Implement safety measures for processing pseudonymized information (establishing an internal management plan for processing pseudonymized information, etc.)<br>/ Prepare necessary documents, etc. (preparing a consignment contract when consigning the processing of pseudonymized information, etc.) |
| 2 | Review of the risk of the processing target | / Select the personal information files and personal information items subject to pseudonymization<br>/ Review the risk of the data subject to pseudonymization<br>　(1) Risk of identification from the data itself: identifying information, potentially identifying information, singular information, impact upon re-identification, etc.<br>　(2) Risk of identification from the processing environment: form of use (internal use, external provision, external combination, etc.), place of processing, method of processing |
| 3 | Pseudonymization | / Based on the results of the identification-risk review, set an item-by-item pseudonymization plan by determining the pseudonymization method and level necessary to achieve the intended use of the pseudonymized information<br>/ Perform pseudonymization based on the item-by-item pseudonymization plan<br>/ Additional information generated during the pseudonymization process is destroyed in principle and, where necessary, stored separately from the pseudonymized information |
| 4 | Adequacy review | / Conduct a final review of the adequacy of the pseudonymization results<br>/ The adequacy review of pseudonymization may be conducted internally using in-house personnel or through external experts (however, it is recommended to form a review committee of at least 3 members)<br>/ If the result of the adequacy review is judged inadequate, perform additional pseudonymization and then conduct the adequacy review again |
| 5 | Safe management | / Fulfill the obligation to take safety measures for pseudonymized information according to the internal management plan established in the advance preparation step (separate storage or deletion of additional information, separation of access rights, etc.)<br>/ Prohibit re-identification and monitor the possibility of re-identification<br>/ If information that can identify a specific individual is generated during the processing of pseudonymized information, immediately stop processing such information and recover/destroy it without delay<br>/ Prepare and retain records related to the processing of pseudonymized information<br>/ Disclose matters concerning the processing of pseudonymized information in the personal information processing policy, etc. |

- Where separate pseudonymized information processing guidelines exist for the relevant field, apply the content of those guidelines with priority
  - Guidelines on the Use of Health and Medical Data (Ministry of Health and Welfare), Guidelines on Processing Pseudonymized/Anonymized Information in the Education Sector (Ministry of Education), Practical Guide on Providing Pseudonymized Information in the Public Sector (Ministry of the Interior and Safety), Guide on Pseudonymization/Anonymization in the Financial Sector (Financial Services Commission), etc.

When personal information is pseudonymized for use/provision, pseudonymization shall be performed by an appropriate method so that an individual cannot be identified without the use/combination of additional information.

- Personal identifying information shall be deleted or, where necessary for combination, etc., replaced with a value that cannot identify a specific individual but can distinguish them, through methods such as generating random values or generating hash values
- Where personal-identity-inferable information is not strictly necessary for the purpose of processing the pseudonymized information, it shall be deleted, and the remaining personal-identity-inferable information shall be pseudonymized by an appropriate method and level considering the processing purpose, identification risk, etc.

  * Examples of pseudonymization/anonymization methods (Pseudonymized Information Processing Guidelines)
  / (Deletion techniques) Deletion, partial deletion, row-item deletion, local deletion, masking
  / (Statistical tools) Aggregation, partial aggregation
  / (Generalization techniques) General rounding, random rounding, controlled rounding, top/bottom coding, local generalization, range method, categorization of character data
  / (Encryption) Two-way encryption, one-way encryption/cryptographic hash functions, order-preserving encryption, format-preserving encryption, homomorphic encryption, polymorphic encryption
  / (Randomization techniques) Noise addition, permutation (substitution), tokenization, (pseudo) random number generators
  / (Other techniques) Sampling, anatomization, synthetic data, homomorphic secret sharing, differential privacy, etc.

When pseudonymized information is combined with that of another personal information controller, the combination shall be carried out through a specialized combination institution or a data specialized institution.

- Where pseudonymized information held by different personal information controllers is to be combined and used for purposes such as compilation of statistics, scientific research, and preservation of records in the public interest, the combination shall be performed through a specialized combination institution designated by the Personal Information Protection Commission or the head of the relevant central administrative agency
  - Status of specialized combination institutions: refer to the Pseudonymized Information Combination Integrated Support System (link.privacy.go.kr)
  - When combining with a data set held by a financial company, the combination shall be performed through a data specialized institution under the Credit Information Act
- Pseudonymized information combination procedure (Pseudonymized Information Processing Guidelines)

| Step | Category | Description |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | Combination application | / The combination applicant confirms the advance preparation matters necessary for combining pseudonymized information, such as consultation among applicants on matters required for the combination application and preparation of the combination application form, and applies to the specialized combination institution for the combination<br>/ Options such as mock combination, combination rate verification, and pseudonymized information extraction may be selected |
| 2 | Combination and additional processing | / The combination applicant providing the pseudonymized information receives the information used to generate the combination key (Salt value) from the combination key management institution, generates the combination key, and once the mock combination, combination rate verification, pseudonymized information extraction, etc. selected at the time of application are completed, transmits the information necessary for combination to each institution |
| 3 | Export and use | / When seeking to export the combined information or analysis results, etc., apply to the specialized combination institution for export |
| 4 | Safe management | / The combination applicant using the combined information processes the exported combined information (hereinafter "exported information") in accordance with the purpose stated in the original combination application form and export application form, and complies with the obligation to take safety measures, etc. |

When processing pseudonymized information, the organization shall delete additional information or store/manage it separately, and shall take the technical/administrative/physical measures necessary to secure safety, such as preparing/retaining related records.

- (Administrative protective measures) Measures such as establishing/implementing an internal management plan to safely manage pseudonymized information and additional information, managing/supervising consignees for pseudonymized information processing work, including clauses such as prohibition of re-identification in contracts when consigning pseudonymized information processing work and providing it to third parties, establishing and disclosing a personal information processing policy related to the processing of pseudonymized information, and providing education on the protection of pseudonymized information

  * Matters to be included in a consignment contract for pseudonymized information processing work (examples)
  1. Prohibition of processing beyond the purpose of performing the consigned work
  2. Matters concerning safety measures for pseudonymized information
  3. Purpose and scope of the consigned work
  4. Restriction on re-consignment
  5. Matters concerning management/supervision
  6. Matters concerning liability such as damages in the event that the consignee violates obligations it must comply with
  7. Prohibition of re-identification
  8. Notification when a risk of re-identification arises

- (Technical protective measures) Measures such as separate storage or deletion of additional information, separation of access rights to pseudonymized information and additional information, and preparation/retention of records related to the processing of pseudonymized information
  - Matters to be included in records related to the processing of pseudonymized information (Article 29-5(2) of the Enforcement Decree of the Personal Information Protection Act)
  1. Purpose of processing the pseudonymized information
  2. Items of personal information that were pseudonymized
  3. Details of the use of the pseudonymized information
  4. Recipients when provided to third parties
  5. Processing period of the pseudonymized information (only where a processing period is separately set under Article 28-4(2) of the Act)
  6. Other matters that the Protection Commission deems necessary and announces to manage the details of processing pseudonymized information
  * Records related to the processing of pseudonymized information shall be retained for at least 3 years from the date the pseudonymized information is destroyed

- (Physical protective measures) Where pseudonymized information or additional information is stored in a computer room or data storage room, establishing/implementing procedures such as access control to protect it from access by unauthorized persons, etc.
- (Other protective measures) Since pseudonymized information also constitutes personal information, implementing the standards for measures to secure the safety of personal information under Article 29 of the Personal Information Protection Act, prohibiting pseudonymization for the purpose of identifying a specific individual, etc.
- (Re-identification monitoring, etc.) Where information that can identify a specific individual is generated during the processing of pseudonymized information, immediately stopping the processing of such information and recovering/destroying it without delay, etc.

Considering the purpose of processing pseudonymized information and other factors, the processing period of pseudonymized information shall be set to an appropriate period, and once that period has elapsed, the information shall be destroyed without delay.

- The processing period of pseudonymized information shall be set appropriately as the period necessary to achieve the purpose of processing the pseudonymized information
- Once the processing period of pseudonymized information has elapsed, it shall be destroyed without delay

When personal information is anonymized, it shall be anonymized at an appropriate level so that a specific individual can no longer be identified even when using other information, given reasonable consideration of time/cost/technology, etc.

- "Anonymized information" means information that can no longer identify an individual even when using other information, given reasonable consideration of time/cost/technology, etc.
  - [Reference] Article 58-2 of the Personal Information Protection Act (Exception to Application)
  / This Act shall not apply to information that can no longer identify an individual even when using other information, given reasonable consideration of time/cost/technology, etc.
- When anonymizing, personal identifying information shall be deleted, and personal-identity-inferable information shall be anonymized at an appropriate level by combining anonymization methods
- To ensure the adequacy of anonymization, a procedure for reviewing the adequacy of anonymization, such as forming a review committee composed of internal/external experts, may be established and implemented

## Evidence (examples)

- Procedure and results of the adequacy assessment of pseudonymization/anonymization
- Records of the processing of pseudonymized information
- Personal information processing policy (matters concerning the use/provision of pseudonymized information), etc.

## Nonconformity examples

- A case where pseudonymized information is processed without the consent of the data subject for the compilation of statistics and scientific research, but no records related to the processing of pseudonymized information are kept, or the related matters are not disclosed in the personal information processing policy
- A case where additional information is stored without being separated within the same database as the pseudonymized information, or access rights to the pseudonymized information and the additional information are not properly separated
- A case where personal information is pseudonymized and used, but pseudonymization is not performed at an appropriate level, so that the possibility exists of identifying a specific individual through combination with other information, etc., even without the use of additional information
- A case where personal information was anonymized for purposes such as generating test data or external disclosure, but the possibility of identifying a specific individual exists due to outliers, etc., making it difficult to regard the anonymization as having been performed appropriately
