## Certification criterion

When personal information is collected from someone other than the data subject or is provided by a third party, only the minimum personal information necessary for the work shall be collected or received. When there is a legal basis or a request from the data subject, the data subject shall be informed of the source from which the personal information was collected, the purpose of processing, and the right to request suspension of processing.

## Key checkpoints

1. When personal information is provided by a third party other than the data subject, is it specified through a contract that the responsibility for obtaining consent to the collection of personal information rests with the party providing the personal information?
2. When personal information is collected from publicly available media or places, is it collected/used only within the scope of the data subject's purpose/scope of disclosure and the scope in which consent is deemed to exist according to social norms?
3. Where necessary for the performance of a service contract, is the minimum-collection principle also applied to personal information collected/generated by automatic collection devices, etc., during the course of providing the service?
4. When there is a request from the data subject regarding personal information collected from someone other than the data subject, are the necessary matters immediately provided to the data subject?
5. When processing personal information collected from someone other than the data subject, if the type/scale of the personal information falls under the legal requirements, are the necessary matters provided to the data subject?
6. Is the record of having informed the data subject of the collection source retained/managed until the relevant personal information is destroyed?

## Detailed explanation

> ※ This item is a **relaxed certification criterion (Annex 7-2)** established under the special cases for Information Security Management System certification. The above certification criterion/key checkpoints are the officially relaxed/adjusted original text. The detailed explanation/related laws/evidence/nonconformity examples below are the content of the "ISMS-P Certification Criteria Guide" for the same control area, Annex 7 [3.1.5 Indirect Collection of Personal Information] (under the relaxed criteria, they apply within the scope of the above certification criterion/checkpoints).

- When personal information is provided by a third party other than the data subject, it must be confirmed whether the information is collected/provided in accordance with lawful procedures, and it must be specifically stated through a contract that the responsibility for obtaining consent to the collection of personal information rests with the party providing the personal information.

- When personal information is collected from publicly available media or places such as SNS or internet homepages, it may be used only within the scope in which the data subject's intent to consent is clearly expressed, or in which consent is deemed to exist according to social norms in light of the content displayed on the internet homepage, etc. (Article 6(4) of the Standard Personal Information Protection Guidelines).

- Where necessary for the performance of a service contract, even for personal information collected/generated by automatic collection devices, etc., during the course of providing the service (call records, access logs, payment records, usage details, etc.), only the minimum personal information necessary for the performance and provision of the relevant service contract shall be collected.
  - However, when such information is collected for use for purposes unrelated to the performance of the service provision contract, it must be classified as an optional consent item and separate prior consent must be obtained (for example, when behavioral information collected through cookies is analyzed and used for individually tailored advertising, etc.).

- When there is a request from the data subject regarding personal information collected from someone other than the data subject, the necessary matters must be immediately provided to the data subject.

- Matters to be provided upon request from the data subject
  1. The source from which the personal information was collected
  2. The purpose of processing the personal information
  3. The fact that the data subject has the right to request suspension of the processing of personal information or to withdraw consent

- Unless there is a justifiable reason, the data subject must be informed within 3 days from the date the request was made (Article 9(1) of the Standard Personal Information Protection Guidelines).

- When refusing the data subject's request because the notification may harm the life/body of another person, etc., the grounds and reasons for the refusal must be provided within 3 days from the date the request was made, unless there is a justifiable reason (Article 9(2) of the Standard Personal Information Protection Guidelines).

  ★ Grounds for refusing notification of the personal information collection source (Article 20(4) of the Personal Information Protection Act)
  1. When the personal information subject to the notification request is included in a personal information file falling under any of the subparagraphs of Article 32(2) of the Personal Information Protection Act (personal information files excluded from registration and disclosure, such as those related to national security, diplomatic secrets, criminal investigations, or classified as secret under other statutes)
  2. When the notification may harm the life/body of another person, or may unfairly infringe on the property or other interests of another person

- When processing personal information collected from someone other than the data subject, if the type/scale of the personal information, etc., falls under the legal requirements, the necessary matters must be notified to the data subject.

- Requirements and methods for the notification obligation

| Field | Value |
| --- | --- |
| Conditions under which the notification obligation is imposed | When personal information is received from another personal information controller on the basis of the data subject's consent to third-party provision of personal information pursuant to Article 17(1)1 of the Personal Information Protection Act |
| Personal information controller requirements for which the notification obligation is imposed | A controller that processes sensitive information or unique identifying information of 50,000 or more data subjects<br>A controller that processes personal information of 1,000,000 or more data subjects<br>※ (Criteria for counting the number of data subjects) Based on the daily average over the immediately preceding 3 months as of the end of the previous year |
| Matters to be notified | The source from which the personal information was collected<br>The purpose of processing the personal information<br>The fact that the data subject has the right to request suspension of the processing of personal information or to withdraw consent |
| Timing of notification | Within 3 months from the date the personal information was received<br>However, where personal information is received and processed periodically two or more times a year within the scope for which the data subject's consent was obtained pursuant to Article 17(1)1 for the matters under Article 17(2)1 through 4, the data subject must be informed either within 3 months from the date the personal information was received, or at least once a year counted from the date the consent was obtained |
| Method of notification | A method by which the data subject can easily confirm the content of the notification, such as in writing/email/telephone/text message<br>A method of notifying through a pop-up window so that the data subject can easily become aware of it in the course of providing goods and services |
| Exceptions to notification | When the personal information subject to the notification request is included in a personal information file falling under any of the subparagraphs of Article 32(2)<br>When the notification may harm the life/body of another person, or may unfairly infringe on the property or other interests of another person<br>※ However, limited to cases where such interests clearly take precedence over the rights of the data subject under this Act |
| Other | Pursuant to Article 20(2) of the Act, the notification of matters such as the source from which personal information was collected may be provided together with the notification of use/provision details under Article 20-2(1) of the Act<br>The record of having informed the data subject of the collection source is retained/managed until the relevant personal information is destroyed (the fact of informing the data subject, the time of informing, and the method of informing) |

- This obligation to notify the source from which personal information was collected applies only to personal information collected from a personal information controller that provided the personal information with the data subject's consent pursuant to Article 17(1)1 of the Personal Information Protection Act. Therefore, it does not apply to personal information collected from a party that provided personal information with consent obtained under the Credit Information Use and Protection Act, or to personal information received pursuant to statutes (Commentary on Personal Information Protection Statutes and Guidelines/Notices).

- If the information collected by the personal information controller does not include personal information that can be used to inform the data subject, such as contact details, notification is not required.

- The record of having informed the data subject of the collection source must be retained/managed until the relevant personal information is destroyed.

- Information to be retained/managed regarding notification of the collection source (Article 15-2(4) of the Enforcement Decree of the Personal Information Protection Act)
  1. The fact of having informed the data subject
  2. The time of informing
  3. The method of informing

## Related laws

- Personal Information Protection Act Article 16 (Restrictions on the Collection of Personal Information), Article 19 (Restrictions on Use/Provision by the Recipient of Personal Information), Article 20 (Notification of the Collection Source, etc., of Personal Information Collected from Someone Other Than the Data Subject)

## Evidence (examples)

- Contract related to the provision of personal information (contractual matters with the providing party)
- Records of notifying data subjects of the personal information collection source
- Personal information processing policy

## Nonconformity examples

- Where personal information disclosed on an internet homepage or SNS is being collected, but no procedure exists for handling the data subject's request regarding the collection source
- Where personal information was received from another business on the basis of consent to the provision of personal information pursuant to Article 17(1)1 of the Personal Information Protection Act, but the relevant data subject was not notified within 3 months (however, this applies where the recipient processes sensitive information or unique identifying information of 50,000 or more data subjects, or processes personal information of 1,000,000 or more data subjects)
- Where, being subject to the legal obligation, the personal information collection source was notified to the data subject, but some mandatory notification matters were omitted, such as the purpose of processing the personal information or the fact that the data subject has the right to withdraw consent
- Where, being subject to the legal obligation, the personal information collection source was notified to the data subject, but the record concerning the notification of the collection source was not retained until the relevant personal information was destroyed
