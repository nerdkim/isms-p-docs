## Certification criterion

The chief executive officer shall designate a Chief Information Security Officer who oversees information security duties and a Chief Privacy Officer who oversees personal information protection duties, as executive-level officers capable of allocating resources such as budget and personnel.

## Key checkpoints

1. Has the chief executive officer formally designated a chief officer who will comprehensively be responsible for the duties related to information security and the handling of personal information?
2. Are the Chief Information Security Officer and the Chief Privacy Officer designated as executive-level officers capable of allocating resources such as budget and personnel, and do they meet the qualification requirements under the relevant laws?

## Detailed explanation

> ※ This item is a **relaxed certification criterion (Annex 7-2)** under the special provisions for information security management system certification. The certification criterion and key checkpoints above are the officially relaxed/adjusted text, while the detailed explanation, related laws, evidence, and nonconformity examples below are the content of the "ISMS-P Certification Criteria Guide" for the same control domain, Annex 7 [1.1.2 Designation of the chief officer] (under the relaxed criterion, these apply within the scope of the certification criterion/checkpoints above).

To effectively promote information security and personal information protection management activities within the organization, the chief executive officer shall formally designate, through procedures such as personnel appointment, a Chief Information Security Officer and a Chief Privacy Officer who can comprehensively be responsible for these activities.

- The Chief Information Security Officer and the Chief Privacy Officer shall be formally appointed through means such as a personnel appointment order; in the case of an ex officio position, the position shall be specified in the information security and personal information protection policy document.

The Chief Information Security Officer and the Chief Privacy Officer shall be designated as executive-level officers capable of allocating resources such as budget and personnel, and shall meet the qualification requirements under the relevant laws (※ refer to Article 36-7 of the Enforcement Decree of the Network Act).

- The Chief Information Security Officer and the Chief Privacy Officer shall be designated as executive-level officers who possess knowledge and competence related to information security and personal information protection so as to be able to substantially oversee the organization's information security and personal information protection duties, and who can allocate resources such as budget and personnel.

  ※ An information and communications service provider shall designate, as its Chief Information Security Officer, an executive or employee meeting the standards prescribed by Presidential Decree in order to ensure the security of information and communications systems, etc., and the safe management of information, and shall report this to the Minister of Science and ICT. However, where the case falls under the standards prescribed by Presidential Decree, an exemption from reporting applies.

- Compliance with the legal requirements for designating the Chief Information Security Officer is required (※ refer to Article 45-3 of the Network Act).

  The Chief Information Security Officer performs the following duties:

  1. The Chief Information Security Officer oversees the following duties:
     - a. Establishment/implementation and improvement of information security plans
     - b. Periodic auditing and improvement of information security conditions and practices
     - c. Identification and assessment of information security risks and preparation of information security measures
     - d. Establishment and implementation of information security education and simulation training plans
  2. The Chief Information Security Officer may concurrently perform the following duties:
     - a. Duties related to the disclosure of information security under Article 13 of the "Act on the Promotion of the Information Security Industry"
     - b. Duties of the information security officer under Article 5(5) of the "Act on the Protection of Information and Communications Infrastructure"
     - c. Duties of the Chief Information Security Officer under Article 21-2(4) of the "Electronic Financial Transactions Act"
     - d. Duties of the Chief Privacy Officer under Article 31(2) of the "Personal Information Protection Act"
     - e. Other implementation of measures necessary for information security under this Act or the relevant laws

  Requirements for designating the Chief Information Security Officer (※ Article 36-7(1) of the Enforcement Decree of the Network Act)

  | No | Category (information and communications service provider) | Requirements for designating the Chief Information Security Officer |
  | --- | --- | --- |
  | 1 | A person with capital of KRW 100 million or less / a small enterprise / a medium enterprise that is not a telecommunications business operator, not a person required to obtain information security management system certification, not a personal information controller required to disclose a personal information processing policy, and not a mail-order distributor | The business owner or representative |
  | 2 | A person whose total assets as of the end of the immediately preceding business year are KRW 5 trillion or more / among persons required to obtain information security management system certification under Article 47(2) of the Act, a person whose total assets as of the end of the immediately preceding business year are KRW 500 billion or more | A director (including a person under Article 401-2(1)3 of the "Commercial Act" and an executive officer under Article 408-2 of the same Act) ※ Compliance with concurrent-position restriction requirements is required |
  | 3 | A person not falling under Nos. 1 or 2 above | The business owner or representative / a director (including a person under Article 401-2(1)3 of the "Commercial Act" and an executive officer under Article 408-2 of the same Act) / the head of the department that oversees information security-related duties |

- Compliance with the legal requirements for designating the Chief Privacy Officer is required (※ refer to Article 32, etc., of the Enforcement Decree of the Personal Information Protection Act).

  The Chief Privacy Officer performs the following duties:

  1. Establishment and implementation of a personal information protection plan
  2. Periodic investigation and improvement of the status and practices of personal information processing
  3. Handling of complaints related to personal information processing and remedy for damages
  4. Establishment of an internal control system to prevent leakage, misuse, and abuse of personal information
  5. Establishment and implementation of a personal information protection education plan
  6. Protection, management, and supervision of personal information files
  7. Establishment/change and implementation of the personal information processing policy
  8. Management of personal information protection-related materials
  9. Destruction of personal information whose processing purpose has been achieved or whose retention period has elapsed

## Related laws

- Personal Information Protection Act Article 29 (Duty of safety measures), Article 31 (Designation of the Chief Privacy Officer)
- Network Act Article 45-3 (Designation of the Chief Information Security Officer, etc.)
- Standards for Measures to Ensure the Safety of Personal Information Article 4 (Establishment/implementation and inspection of the internal management plan)

## Evidence (examples)

- Materials related to the appointment of the Chief Information Security Officer and the Chief Privacy Officer (personnel appointment orders, personnel cards, etc.)
- Information security and personal information protection organization chart
- Information security and personal information protection policies/guidelines
- Job descriptions (matters concerning the roles and responsibilities of the Chief Information Security Officer and the Chief Privacy Officer)
- Records of the Chief Information Security Officer report
- Internal management plan (matters concerning the designation of the Chief Privacy Officer)

## Nonconformity examples

- Where a party is subject to the obligation to designate and report a Chief Information Security Officer under the Network Act, but has not designated and reported a Chief Information Security Officer
- Where a person who does not hold substantial authority and standing related to personal information protection is designated as the Chief Privacy Officer, making it difficult to regard that person as able to comprehensively be responsible for the duties related to personal information processing
- Where the Chief Information Security Officer and the Chief Privacy Officer are specified in the organization chart, but a formal designation procedure such as a personnel appointment order has not been carried out
- Where an information and communications service provider that is subject to mandatory ISMS certification and whose total assets as of the end of the previous year exceeded KRW 500 billion has its Chief Information Security Officer concurrently serving as the CIO
