## Certification criterion

When personal information is collected/used for marketing purposes such as promotion or sale solicitation of goods or services, or transmission of advertising information, the organization must notify the data subject of that purpose so that they can clearly recognize it and must obtain consent.

## Key checkpoints

1. When obtaining consent for the processing of personal information in order to promote goods or services to the data subject or to solicit sales, does the organization notify the data subject so that they can clearly recognize it and obtain separate consent?
2. When transmitting advertising information for commercial purposes using an electronic transmission medium, does the organization obtain the recipient's explicit prior consent, and does it regularly confirm the recipient's consent to receive every two years?
3. When a recipient expresses a refusal to receive, or withdraws prior consent to, the transmission of advertising information for commercial purposes using an electronic transmission medium, does the organization stop transmitting advertising information for commercial purposes?
4. When transmitting advertising information for commercial purposes, does the organization specifically state the name of the sender, the method of refusing receipt, etc., and refrain from transmission during nighttime hours?

## Detailed explanation

> Note: This item is a **relaxed certification criterion (Annex 7-2)** under the special provisions for information security management system certification. The certification criterion/key checkpoints above are the relaxed/adjusted official text, while the detailed explanation/related laws/evidence/nonconformity examples below are the content of the "ISMS-P Certification Criteria Guide" for Annex 7 [3.1.7 Collection/Use of Personal Information for Marketing Purposes] in the same control area (under the relaxed criteria, they apply within the scope of the certification criterion/checkpoints above).

When an organization intends to process personal information in order to promote goods or services to the data subject or to solicit sales, it must notify the data subject so that they can clearly recognize it and obtain separate consent.

- It is prohibited to state the purpose as "provision of supplementary services", "provision of affiliated services", etc. while collecting personal information for the purpose of "promotion and marketing"
- Personal information collected for the purpose of product promotion or marketing must be clearly distinguished from information collected for other purposes, and consent must be obtained before collection
- Implement the consent form so that the fact that the organization may contact the data subject using the relevant personal information for the promotion or sale solicitation of goods or services is clearly indicated and easy to recognize (clearly indicate the content through the size, color, boldness, or underlining of the text)

When transmitting advertising information for commercial purposes using an electronic transmission medium, the organization must obtain the recipient's explicit prior consent, and must regularly confirm the recipient's consent to receive every two years.

- An "electronic transmission medium" refers to a mobile phone, landline phone, fax, messenger, email, bulletin board, etc.
- To transmit advertising information for commercial purposes, the organization must obtain the recipient's explicit consent to receive by means of a document (including an electronic document) or verbally

Note: The concept of advertising information for commercial purposes
- Advertising information for commercial purposes refers to (1) information about the sender and (2) the content of goods or services that the sender will provide, transmitted by the sender for the purpose of gaining economic benefit. A person who causes the transmission is also included in the sender
- In principle, all information sent by a business operator to customers constitutes advertising information
- Since the reason for a for-profit corporation's existence is the pursuit of profit, in principle all information transmitted to customers constitutes advertising information for commercial purposes; for a non-profit corporation, whether the information is advertising for commercial purposes is judged according to the nature of the information transmitted
- Even if it is not the promotion of specific goods or services, if the information sent to the recipient constitutes promotion of the sender's image, it can be regarded as advertising information
- Even if the main information is not advertising information, if advertising information is incidentally included, the whole constitutes advertising information

Note: Exceptions to advertising information for commercial purposes
- Information whose purpose is to facilitate, complete, or confirm a transaction previously concluded with the recipient
- Explanation, warranty, product recall, safety, or security related information about goods or services used or purchased by the recipient
- One-time information sent at the customer's request (quotations, etc.)
- Information requested by the recipient for a monetary consideration (newsletters, stock information, livestock product trade information, etc.)
- Guidance and confirmation information related to the recipient's purchase or use of goods or services provided by the sender, etc.

- Consent for the purpose of promoting goods or services / soliciting sales under Article 22, Paragraph 1, Item 7 of the Personal Information Protection Act corresponds to consent for the sender to collect/use the recipient's personal information in order to transmit advertising information, whereas consent under Article 50, Paragraph 1 of the Network Act corresponds to consent to receive the advertising information the sender sends; therefore the two consents must be distinguished and obtained separately
- Advertising information (app push advertising) must not be transmitted in a state where a smartphone app (application) has merely been downloaded and installed; when the app is first launched, the organization must confirm whether the user consents to receiving advertising information, obtain consent, and only then transmit advertising
- However, where an exception based on a transactional relationship applies, advertising information may be transmitted without obtaining consent to receive

Note: Exceptions to consent to receive transmission of advertising information based on a transactional relationship
- Where a person who has directly collected contact information from the recipient through a transactional relationship in goods, etc. intends to transmit advertising information for commercial purposes about goods, etc. of the same kind as those they process and traded with the recipient, within 6 months from the date of the transaction
- Where a telemarketing seller under the "Act on Door-to-Door Sales, Etc." verbally notifies the recipient of the source of collection of the personal information and makes a telephone solicitation

- A person who transmits advertising information after obtaining the recipient's consent to receive must confirm whether the recipient still consents to receive every two years from the date on which consent to receive was obtained
  - Since this imposes a duty to notify the consenting recipient of the fact that they had consented to receive, it is not necessary to obtain re-consent
  - If the recipient does not express any intention, the consent to receive is deemed to be maintained as is
  - Matters to be notified to the recipient when confirming whether they still wish to receive
    1. The name of the sender
    2. The date of consent to receive and the fact of consent to receive
    3. The method of expressing the intention to maintain or withdraw consent to receive

When a recipient expresses a refusal to receive, or withdraws prior consent to, the transmission of advertising information for commercial purposes using an electronic transmission medium, the transmission of advertising information for commercial purposes must be stopped.

- Even where there is a transactional relationship, if the recipient expresses a refusal to receive, the transmission of advertising information is prohibited
- Withdrawing membership can also be regarded as an expression of refusal to receive, so advertising information must not be transmitted to a recipient who has withdrawn membership
- Unless the recipient has expressed the withdrawal of consent to receive and refusal to receive with a specifically defined scope, its effect applies not only to that particular advertisement but to all advertisements sent by that sender

When transmitting advertising information for commercial purposes, the name of the sender, the method of refusing receipt, etc. must be specifically stated.

- Matters that must also be notified when transmitting advertising information for commercial purposes using an electronic transmission medium
  1. The name and contact information of the sender
  2. Matters concerning measures and methods enabling the recipient to easily express refusal to receive or withdrawal of consent to receive
  - Note: Comply with Annex 6 of the Enforcement Decree of the Network Act (matters to be stated and methods of stating advertising information for commercial purposes)
- During nighttime hours (from 9:00 PM to 8:00 AM the following day), the transmission of advertising information for commercial purposes using an electronic transmission medium is prohibited
  - To transmit advertising information during nighttime hours, separate consent to receive is required
  - However, in the case of email, nighttime transmission is possible even without separate consent
  - Note: For detailed content, refer to the "Guide to the Network Act for Prevention of Illegal Spam"

## Related laws

- Personal Information Protection Act Article 22 (Methods of Obtaining Consent)
- Network Act Article 50 (Restrictions on Transmission of Advertising Information)

## Evidence (examples)

- Online personal information collection forms (website membership registration screen, mobile app membership registration screen, event participation, etc.)
- Offline personal information collection forms (membership registration application, etc.)
- Marketing consent records
- Records of consent to receive advertising information transmission and records of confirmation of intention to consent to receive
- Administrator screens of the advertising information sending system (email, SMS, app push, etc.)
- Advertising information sending text
- Personal information processing policy

## Nonconformity examples

- Where personal information is collected for the purpose of "promotion and marketing" but the purpose is vaguely stated as "provision of supplementary services", "provision of affiliated services", etc., or where comprehensive consent is obtained without distinguishing it from personal information collected for other purposes
- Where a recipient expressed refusal regarding the transmission of advertising information (app push) in a mobile app, but advertising app push occurs due to reasons such as a program error
- Where, on the online membership registration screen, transmission of advertising information by text message or email is checked by default
- Where consent to receive advertising information is not confirmed every two years
- Where advertising information for commercial purposes is transmitted by email without indicating "(Advertisement)" at the beginning of the subject line
