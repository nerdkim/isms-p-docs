## Certification criterion

For shared office equipment (document archives, shared PCs, multifunction printers, file servers, etc.) and individual work environments (business PCs, desks, etc.), the organization shall establish and implement work-environment protective measures such as a clean-desk policy and periodic inspections so that personal information and important information are not exposed or leaked to unauthorized persons.

## Key checkpoints

1. Are protective measures established and implemented for shared facilities and office equipment such as document archives, shared PCs, multifunction printers, and file servers?
2. Are protective measures established and implemented to prevent leakage/exposure of personal information and important information through individual work environments such as business PCs, desks, and drawers?
3. Are the protective measures necessary to safely manage printed/copied materials of personal information, such as paper printouts containing personal information, being taken?
4. Is compliance with information protection in individual and shared work environments reviewed periodically?

## Detailed explanation

> ※ This item is a **relaxed certification criterion (Annex 7-2)** under the special provisions for information security management system certification. The certification criterion and key checkpoints above are the official, relaxed/adjusted text, while the detailed explanation/related laws/evidence/nonconformity examples below are from the "ISMS-P Certification Criteria Guide" for Annex 7 [2.4.7 Work Environment Security], which is the same control domain (under the relaxed criterion, they apply within the scope of the certification criterion/checkpoints above).

Protective measures shall be established and implemented for shared facilities and office equipment such as document archives, shared PCs, multifunction printers, and file servers.

- Document archives: minimize the number of persons with access, grant access authorization by department/task, manage access records
- Shared PCs: assign a responsible person, set a screen saver, set a login password, change passwords periodically, restrict storage of important information, install antivirus, apply security updates, etc.
- Shared office equipment: prohibit leaving important documents unattended near shared office equipment such as fax machines, copiers, and printers
- File servers: grant access authorization by department/task, minimize unnecessary disclosure of information, issue accounts per user
- Shared offices: prohibit leaving documents containing important information (personal information) unattended in shared offices such as meeting rooms and project rooms
- Establish protective measures for other shared work environments

Protective measures shall be established and implemented to prevent leakage/exposure of personal information and important information through individual work environments such as business PCs, desks, and drawers.

- Protective measures when leaving the desk: prohibit leaving personal information and documents/auxiliary storage media containing personal information unattended (clean desk), set a screen saver and password, etc.
- Prohibit exposing login information (passwords, etc.) on monitors, desks, etc.
- Store documents and auxiliary storage media containing personal information and important information in a safe place with a locking device
- Shred documents containing personal information and important information using a shredder or the like so that they cannot be recovered
- Take measures so that unauthorized persons cannot access and arbitrarily operate management terminals, etc.

Necessary protective measures shall be taken to prevent loss/theft/leakage of personal information through printed/copied materials of personal information, such as paper printouts containing personal information, and to safely manage the printed/copied materials.

※ Protective measures for printed/copied materials (examples)
- Establish policies, regulations, guidelines, etc. for the protection and management of printed/copied materials
- Prepare and maintain a production/management ledger for printed/copied materials
- Designate and operate a department to operate/manage printed/copied materials
- Control/report/restrict external removal and reproduction of printed/copied materials
- Store/manage records of printed/copied materials such as the printer (person) and the print date/time
- Establish a destruction plan including destruction procedures for paper printouts and verification of destruction, and conduct periodic inspections
- Apply security technologies for printed/copied materials such as multifunction printer security and output watermarks, etc.

Compliance with information protection in individual and shared work environments shall be reviewed periodically.

- Persons who fail to comply with security regulations for individual and shared work environments are managed in accordance with the reward-and-penalty regulations

※ Office security inspection measures (examples)
- Individual work environment: self-diagnosis of compliance with information protection, and periodic inspection of compliance with information protection by the managing department
- Shared work environment: periodic inspection of compliance with shared-work protective measures, and notification or training for noncompliance items

## Related laws

- Personal Information Protection Act Article 29 (Duty of Safety Measures)
- Standards for Measures to Ensure the Safety of Personal Information Article 10 (Physical Safety Measures), Article 12 (Safety Measures for Printing/Copying)

## Evidence (examples)

- Office and shared-space security inspection report
- Office and shared-space security inspection checklist
- Actions taken against noncompliant persons (training, rewards/penalties, etc.)
- Status of protective measures for printed/copied materials

## Nonconformity examples

- The internal personal information management plan specifies that daily security inspections for personal information protection (clean-desk operation, etc.) be conducted periodically, but this is not implemented
- Documents containing personal information, such as membership application forms, are stored in an office document cabinet without a locking device
- Employees' computer screen savers and passwords are not set, and important documents are left unattended for a long time on the desk of an employee on leave
- Protective measures have not been established for shared PCs installed in shared office spaces such as meeting rooms, so files containing personal information are stored without encryption, or the PCs are kept in a vulnerable state with security updates not applied and antivirus not installed
