## Certification criterion

When personal information is transferred abroad, appropriate protective measures such as consent to the cross-border transfer and disclosure of related matters must be established and implemented.

## Key checkpoints

1. When personal information is transferred abroad, does the organization inform the data subject of all the notice items concerning the cross-border transfer and obtain separate consent, or comply with lawful requirements such as certification or recognition?
2. When informing the data subject about the cross-border entrustment of processing/storage of personal information for the conclusion and performance of a contract with the data subject, does the organization inform them by an appropriate method, including all necessary matters?
3. Is a contract concerning the cross-border transfer being concluded, including matters on compliance with personal information protection laws and on personal information protection?
4. When personal information is transferred abroad, are the measures necessary for personal information protection being taken?

## Detailed explanation

> ※ This item is a **relaxed certification criterion (Annex 7-2)** under the special provisions for information security management system certification. The certification criterion/key checkpoints above are the officially relaxed/adjusted original text, and the detailed explanation/related laws/evidence/nonconformity examples below are the content of the "ISMS-P Certification Criteria Guide" for Annex 7 [3.3.4 Cross-border transfer of personal information] in the same control domain (under the relaxed criteria, they apply within the scope of the certification criterion/checkpoints above).

- When personal information is provided (including cases where it can be accessed)/entrusted for processing/stored to a third party abroad (hereinafter referred to as "transfer"), the organization must inform the data subject of all the notice items concerning the cross-border transfer and obtain separate consent, or comply with lawful requirements such as certification or recognition.

- Cases in which cross-border transfer of personal information is permitted (Article 28-8(1) of the Personal Information Protection Act)

| No | Field | Description |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | Separate consent | Where separate consent to the cross-border transfer has been obtained from the data subject |
| 2 | Basis in a law, treaty, etc. | Where a law, a treaty to which the Republic of Korea is a party, or another international agreement has special provisions concerning the cross-border transfer of personal information |
| 3 | Disclosure in the privacy policy, etc. (entrustment of processing/storage) | Where entrustment of processing/storage of personal information is necessary for the conclusion and performance of a contract with the data subject, and the related matters have been disclosed in the privacy policy or communicated to the data subject by means such as email |
| 4 | Certification | Where certification designated and published by the Protection Commission, such as personal information protection certification (ISMS-P certification), has been obtained, and all of the following measures have been taken<br>a. Safety measures necessary for personal information protection and measures necessary to guarantee the rights of the data subject<br>b. Measures necessary to implement the certified matters in the country to which the personal information is transferred |
| 5 | Recognition of the recipient country, etc. | Where the Protection Commission recognizes that the personal information protection system, the scope of guaranteeing data subject rights, the remedy procedures, etc. of the recipient country or international organization have a level substantially equivalent to the level of personal information protection under this Act |

- When obtaining consent for the cross-border transfer of personal information, all of the following 5 matters must be notified and consent obtained
  - Notice items when obtaining consent for the cross-border transfer of personal information (Article 28-8(2) of the Personal Information Protection Act)
    1. The items of personal information to be transferred
    2. The country to which the personal information is transferred, and the timing and method of transfer
    3. The name of the recipient of the personal information (in the case of a corporation, its name and contact information)
    4. The purpose of use of the personal information by the recipient and the retention/use period
    5. The method, procedure, and effect of refusing the transfer of personal information

- When informing the data subject about the cross-border entrustment of processing/storage of personal information for the conclusion and performance of a contract with the data subject, the organization must inform them by an appropriate method, including all necessary matters.

- Methods of informing the data subject
  1. Disclosure in the privacy policy
  2. Means such as writing (writing, email, fax, telephone, text message, or an equivalent method)

- Matters to be notified to the data subject
  1. The items of personal information to be transferred
  2. The country to which the personal information is transferred, and the timing and method of transfer
  3. The name of the recipient of the personal information (in the case of a corporation, its name and contact information)
  4. The purpose of use of the personal information by the recipient and the retention/use period
  5. The method, procedure, and effect of refusing the transfer of personal information

- A contract concerning the cross-border transfer must be concluded, including matters on compliance with personal information protection laws and on personal information protection.

- The following matters must be agreed in advance with the recipient and reflected in the contract content, etc.
  1. Measures to ensure safety for personal information protection under Article 30(1) of the Enforcement Decree of the Personal Information Protection Act
  2. Measures concerning grievance handling and dispute resolution for personal information infringement
  3. Other measures necessary for the protection of the data subject's personal information

- When transferring a data subject's personal information abroad, the organization must take the measures necessary for personal information protection.

- Protective measures that must be implemented in cross-border transfer of personal information
  1. Compliance with the provisions of the Personal Information Protection Act concerning cross-border transfer of personal information
  2. Compliance with the provisions of Articles 17 through 19 of the Personal Information Protection Act
  3. Compliance with the provisions of Chapter 5 (Guarantee of the rights of data subjects) of the Personal Information Protection Act
  4. Measures to ensure safety for personal information protection under Article 30(1) of the Enforcement Decree of the Personal Information Protection Act
  5. Measures concerning grievance handling and dispute resolution for personal information infringement
  6. Other measures necessary for the protection of the data subject's personal information

## Related laws

- Personal Information Protection Act Article 28-8 (Cross-border transfer of personal information), Article 28-9 (Order to suspend cross-border transfer of personal information), Article 28-10 (Reciprocity), Article 28-11 (Provisions applied mutatis mutandis)
- Regulations on the Operation, etc. of Cross-border Transfer of Personal Information

## Evidence (examples)

- Consent form related to cross-border transfer of personal information
- Contract related to cross-border transfer of personal information
- Privacy policy
- Notification or disclosure records related to cross-border entrustment of processing/storage of personal information

## Nonconformity examples

- A case where, in the course of processing personal information, third-party provision of personal information to an overseas operator occurred, but separate consent to the cross-border transfer of personal information was not obtained even though it did not fall under an exception to consent such as certification or recognition of the recipient country
- A case where an overseas cloud service (overseas region) was used to entrust the processing and storage of personal information, but related matters such as the recipient country and the transfer method were not disclosed in the privacy policy or communicated to the data subject
- A case where, in obtaining consent for the cross-border transfer of personal information, only the name of the recipient (company name) was notified and the recipient country, etc. was not informed
