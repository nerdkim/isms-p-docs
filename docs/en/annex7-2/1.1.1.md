# 1.1.1 Participation of Management  _( Annex 7-2 )_

## Certification criterion

The chief executive officer shall establish and operate a reporting and decision-making system so that management can participate throughout the establishment and operation activities of the information security and personal information protection management system.

## Key checkpoints

1. Are the responsibilities and roles for reporting, decision-making, and so on documented so that management can participate throughout the establishment and operation activities of the information security and personal information protection management system?
2. Have reporting, review, and approval procedures been established and implemented so that management can actively participate in decision-making regarding information security and personal information protection activities?

## Detailed explanation

> Note: This item is a **relaxed certification criterion (Annex 7-2)** under the special case of information security management system certification. The certification criterion / key checkpoints above are the relaxed/adjusted official text, while the detailed explanation / related laws / evidence / nonconformity examples below are the content of the "ISMS-P Certification Criteria Guide" for Annex 7 [1.1.1 Participation of management], which is the same control domain (under the relaxed criteria, it applies within the scope of the certification criterion / checkpoints above).

The responsibilities and roles for reporting, decision-making, and so on shall be documented so that management with decision-making authority can participate throughout the establishment and operation activities of the information security and personal information protection management system.

- Specify the basis for activities in the information security and personal information protection policy or implementation documents so that management can participate in important matters of management system operation, such as the enactment/revision of information security and personal information protection policies, risk management, and internal audits

Reporting, review, and approval procedures that allow management to actively participate in decision-making regarding information security and personal information protection activities shall be established and implemented.

- Define important activities in which management participates within the information security and personal information protection management system, and establish a corresponding reporting system (regular/irregular reporting, committee participation, and so on)
- Determine the reporting and decision-making procedures, targets, cycles, and so on in accordance with the organization's size and characteristics so that management can effectively participate in the establishment/operation of the management system
- In accordance with the established internal procedures, management receives reports on and participates in decision-making regarding major matters within the information security and personal information protection management system

## Related laws

_No individual legal provision directly corresponding to this item is specified._

## Evidence (examples)

- Information security and personal information protection reporting system (communication plan, and so on)
- Minutes of the information security and personal information protection committee
- Information security and personal information protection policies/guidelines (including management approval records)
- Information security plan and internal management plan (including management approval records)
- Information security and personal information protection organization chart

## Nonconformity examples

- A case where the information security and personal information protection policy specifies that the information security and personal information protection status is to be reported to management on a quarterly basis, but the relevant reporting has not been carried out for a long period
- A case where, in carrying out important information security activities (risk assessment, determination of the risk acceptance level, review of information security measures and implementation plans, review of the results of implementing information security measures, security audits, and so on), management or a person delegated with management's authority did not participate in the related reporting, approval, and other decision-making, or the related evidence cannot be confirmed
