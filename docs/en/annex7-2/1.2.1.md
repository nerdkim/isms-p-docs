## Certification criterion

According to the organization's business characteristics, a classification standard for information assets shall be established, and all information assets within the scope of the management system shall be identified/classified, their importance rated, and the resulting list kept up to date.

## Key checkpoints

1. Has a classification standard for information assets been established, and are all assets within the scope of the information security and personal information protection management system identified and managed as a list?
2. For identified information assets, is importance determined and a security grade assigned by taking into account legal requirements and the impact on business, etc.?
3. Is the status of information assets surveyed periodically to keep the information asset list up to date?

## Detailed explanation

> ※ This item is a **relaxed certification criterion (Annex 7-2)** under the special provisions for information security management system certification. The certification criterion/key checkpoints above are the official relaxed/adjusted text, while the detailed explanation/related laws/evidence/nonconformity examples below are the content of the "ISMS-P Certification Criteria Guide" for the same control area, Annex 7 [1.2.1 Information Asset Identification] (under the relaxed criterion, they apply within the scope of the certification criterion/checkpoints above).

**A classification standard for information assets shall be established, and all assets within the scope of the information security and personal information protection management system shall be identified and managed as a list.**

- Establish a classification standard for information assets suited to the organization's characteristics, and identify all information assets without omission according to the classification standard

  ※ Information asset classification (example)
  - Classification by asset type: servers, data (DBMS), information systems (application programs), software, network equipment, security systems, PCs, information, facilities, premises, etc.
  - Items by asset type (example)
    - Server: host name, asset serial number, model name, purpose, IP address, managing department name, managing operator, managing manager, security grade, etc.
    - Data: database name, table name, (personal) information item names (e.g., name, gender, date of birth, mobile phone number, email, etc.), managing department name, managing operator, managing manager, storage system (host name), storage location (IP address), security grade, etc.
    - Information system: hardware and software needed for collecting, processing, storing, retrieving, and transmitting/receiving information, such as servers, terminals like PCs, auxiliary storage media, network equipment, and application programs
    - Security system: a system built to prevent damage, alteration, leakage, etc. of information, including intrusion prevention (firewall) systems, intrusion detection systems, intrusion protection systems, personal information leakage prevention systems, etc.
    - Information: covers both documentary information and electronic information (important information, personal information, etc.)

- Prepare a list by confirming asset information such as asset name, purpose, location, responsible person and manager, and managing department

- To manage information assets efficiently, manage them in various forms such as using an asset management system or documents (Excel)

- When using cloud services, prepare classification standards reflecting the characteristics of cloud services (for example, virtual servers, object storage, etc.) and identify/manage cloud assets accordingly

**For identified information assets, importance shall be determined and a security grade assigned by taking into account legal requirements and the impact on business, etc.**

- Determine security grade evaluation criteria suited to each asset's characteristics, such as legal requirements or the impact on business

  ※ Security grade rating criteria (example)
  - Importance evaluation according to confidentiality, integrity, availability, legal compliance, etc.
  - Also consider service impact, loss of profit, loss of customers, damage to external image, etc.

- Rate the security grade for each information asset according to the security grade evaluation criteria and manage it as a list

**The status of information assets shall be surveyed periodically to keep the information asset list up to date.**

- Prepare procedures to confirm the status of assets that are newly introduced, changed, or disposed of

- Periodically survey the status of information assets and keep the information asset list up to date

## Related laws

_No individual legal provision directly corresponding to this item is specified._

## Evidence (examples)

- Classification standard for information assets and personal information assets
- List of information assets and personal information assets (asset management system screen)
- Security grades of information assets and personal information
- Asset stocktaking records
- Risk analysis report (asset identification records)

## Nonconformity examples

- A case where, in the asset list within the scope of the information security and personal information protection management system, internal information leakage control systems used to control the PCs of important-information handlers and personal-information handlers, such as output (printout) security, document encryption, and USB media control, are omitted
- A case where there is personal information provided by a third party within the scope of the information security and personal information protection management system, but asset identification for that personal information has not been carried out
- A case where the classification standard for information asset and personal information security grades specified in the internal guidelines does not match the classification standard in the asset management ledger
- A case where on-premises assets have been identified, but asset identification for externally outsourced IT services (web hosting, server hosting, cloud, etc.) is omitted (however, within the certification scope)
- A case where the rationality and reliability of the information asset importance evaluation is inadequate, such as rating the confidentiality grade of a backup server storing personal information such as unique identification information as (Low)
