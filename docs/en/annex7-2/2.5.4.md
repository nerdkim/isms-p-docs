## Certification criterion

To prevent information leakage, malware infection, and internal network intrusion through the Internet, an internet access control policy shall be established and implemented, including restricting Internet access or services (P2P, web hard drives, messengers, etc.) for key information systems, terminals used for key duties and for handling personal information, etc.

## Key checkpoints

1. Is a control policy for Internet access by business PCs, such as terminals used for key duties and for handling personal information, established and implemented?
2. Is unnecessary external Internet access from key information systems (DB servers, etc.) controlled?
3. Where an obligation to block the Internet network is imposed under relevant laws, are the applicable persons identified and internet-network blocking measures applied in a secure manner?

## Detailed explanation

> Note: This item is a relaxed certification criterion (Annex 7-2) under the special case for information security management system certification. The certification criterion and key checkpoints above are the relaxed/adjusted official text, and the detailed explanation, related laws, evidence, and nonconformity examples below are the content of the "ISMS-P Certification Criteria Guide" for Annex 7 [2.6.7 Internet Access Control] in the same control domain (under the relaxed criterion, they apply within the scope of the certification criterion and checkpoints above).

To reduce the risks of information leakage, malware infection, and internal network intrusion through the Internet to an appropriate level, a control policy for Internet access by business PCs, such as terminals used for key duties and for handling personal information, shall be established and implemented.

- Network configuration policy for Internet connection
- User access policy for using external email, accessing Internet sites, downloading and transmitting software, etc.
- Policy for blocking access to harmful sites (adult, entertainment, etc.)
- Policy for blocking access to sites that may enable information leakage (web hard drives, P2P, remote access, etc.)
- Policy related to network separation or internet-network blocking measures (whether network separation is applied, persons subject to network separation, method of network separation, procedures for transferring data between networks, etc.)
- Policy for reviewing (monitoring) Internet access records, etc.

Unnecessary external Internet access from key information systems (database servers, etc.) shall be controlled.

- Restrict external Internet access from internal servers (DB servers, file servers, etc.) so that malware inflow, information leakage, reverse connections, etc. are blocked
- Where there is an unavoidable reason, prepare protective measures through risk analysis and allow it after approval by the responsible person

Where an obligation to block the Internet network is imposed under relevant laws, persons subject to internet-network blocking shall be identified and internet-network blocking measures applied in a secure manner.

- Scope of application for internet-network blocking measures under the Personal Information Protection Act
  - (Personal information controllers subject to the obligation) Personal information controllers for whom the daily average number of users whose personal information is stored/managed during the three months immediately before the end of the previous year is at least 1 million
  - (Computers, etc. subject to the obligation) Computers, etc. of personal information handlers who can download or destroy personal information from the personal information processing system, or set access privileges
  - (Measures when using external cloud services) Where a personal information processing system is configured/operated using a cloud computing service under Article 2, subparagraph 3 of the "Act on the Development of Cloud Computing and Protection of Users," apply a measure that blocks the Internet other than access to the relevant service
- Apply internet-network blocking measures in a secure manner, taking the following into account
  - Review whether subject to the obligation of internet-network blocking measures, and if subject, identify the persons subject to internet-network blocking measures
  - If not subject to the obligation of internet-network blocking measures, decide whether to apply internet-network blocking measures based on risk analysis results, etc.
  - Apply internet-network blocking measures physically (e.g., configuring two PCs with separated networks) or logically (e.g., using virtualization technology such as VDI)
  - Identify bypass paths around internet-network blocking measures and apply control measures
  - Prepare control measures for the secure transfer of data on computers to which internet-network blocking measures are applied
  - Perform periodic inspection of the adequacy of the internet-network blocking environment and the existence of vulnerabilities, etc.

## Related laws

- Personal Information Protection Act, Article 29 (Obligation to Take Safety Measures)
- Standards for Measures to Ensure the Safety of Personal Information, Article 6 (Access Control)

## Evidence (examples)

- Policy for blocking non-business sites (P2P, etc.) (management screen of the non-business site blocking system, etc.)
- History of Internet access monitoring
- List of persons subject to internet-network blocking measures
- Procedures and processing records for data transfer between networks (application/approval records, etc.)
- Network configuration diagram

## Nonconformity examples

- Where internet-network blocking measures were applied under the Personal Information Protection Act, but the application of internet-network blocking measures was omitted for some persons subject to the obligation, such as those able to set access privileges to the personal information processing system
- Where internet-network blocking measures were applied as a person subject to the obligation under the Personal Information Protection Act, but bypass access via another server was possible, allowing connection to the personal information processing system and downloading or destroying personal information in an environment where internet-network blocking measures were not applied
- Where some servers located in the DMZ and internal network can unnecessarily connect directly to the Internet
- Where internet-network blocking measures were applied by physically separating the networks of Internet PCs and internal business PCs and a system for transferring data between networks was built/operated, but there is no approval procedure for data transfer and periodic review of data transfer records is not conducted
- Where internal regulations require that a personal information handler's access to P2P and web hard drive sites be allowed only for a specific period after approval by the responsible person, but there are many cases where exceptional access was allowed without going through the approval procedure
