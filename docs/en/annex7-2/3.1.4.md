## Certification criterion

To process sensitive information and unique identifying information (excluding resident registration numbers), the separate consent of the data subject must be obtained, except in cases where the law specifically requires or permits such processing.

## Key checkpoints

1. Is sensitive information processed only when separate consent has been obtained from the data subject or when there is a basis in the relevant laws?
2. Is unique identifying information (excluding resident registration numbers) processed only when separate consent has been obtained from the data subject or when there is a specific basis in the relevant laws?
3. When it is judged that there is a risk of infringing privacy because the information disclosed in the course of providing goods or services includes the data subject's sensitive information, is the data subject informed, in an easily understandable manner and before the provision of the goods or services, of the possibility that the sensitive information may be disclosed and of the method for choosing non-disclosure?

## Detailed explanation

> Note: This item is a relaxed certification criterion (Annex 7-2) under the special cases of information security management system certification. The certification criterion and key checkpoints above are the official text as relaxed/adjusted, while the detailed explanation, related laws, evidence, and nonconformity examples below are content from the "ISMS-P Certification Criteria Guide" for Annex 7 [3.1.4 Restrictions on Processing Sensitive Information and Unique Identifying Information], which is the same control area (under the relaxed criterion, they apply within the scope of the certification criterion and checkpoints above).

The processing of sensitive information is prohibited in principle; however, it may be processed only in cases where separate consent has been obtained from the data subject or where there is a basis in the relevant laws.

- Scope of sensitive information
  1. Ideology/belief : various ideologies or ideological tendencies, religious beliefs, and the like
  2. Political opinion : information on one's position on political issues or whether one supports a particular political party
  3. Joining/withdrawing from a labor union/political party : information on joining or withdrawing from a labor union or political party
  4. Information on health and sex life : information on an individual's past and present medical history, physical/mental disability (such as whether a disability grade exists), sexual orientation, and the like
  5. Personal information that carries the risk of significantly infringing privacy
     - Genetic information obtained as a result of genetic testing and the like, and information on criminal records
     - Information on criminal records such as the sentencing/exemption of a punishment heavier than a fine and the suspension of sentencing, protective custody, custody for treatment, protective observation, the invalidation of a suspended sentence, the revocation of a suspended execution, and the sentencing or disposition of confiscation, additional collection, community service order, or confinement order imposed together with a punishment heavier than a fine
     - Information generated through certain technical means for the purpose of identifying a specific individual, which is information on an individual's physical/physiological/behavioral characteristics (biometric characteristic information)
     - Information on race or ethnicity

- Cases where the processing of sensitive information is permitted
  1. Where consent has been obtained from the data subject separately from the consent to the processing of other personal information
  2. Where the law requires or permits the processing of sensitive information

Unique identifying information (excluding resident registration numbers) must be processed only when separate consent has been obtained from the data subject or when there is a specific basis in the relevant laws.

- Scope of unique identifying information
  1. Resident registration number (however, in accordance with the statutory principle governing the collection of resident registration numbers, collection based on consent is not permitted)
  2. Passport number
  3. Driver's license number
  4. Alien registration number

- Cases where the processing of unique identifying information (excluding resident registration numbers) is permitted
  1. Where consent has been obtained from the data subject separately from the consent to the processing of other personal information
  2. Where the law specifically requires or permits the processing of unique identifying information

When it is judged that there is a risk of infringing privacy because the information disclosed in the course of providing goods or services includes the data subject's sensitive information, the data subject must be informed, in an easily understandable manner and before the provision of the goods or services, of the possibility that the sensitive information may be disclosed and of the method for choosing non-disclosure.

- Where applicable, this must also be disclosed in the privacy policy

## Related laws

- Personal Information Protection Act Article 23 (Restrictions on Processing Sensitive Information)
- Personal Information Protection Act Article 24 (Restrictions on Processing Unique Identifying Information)

## Evidence (examples)

- Online personal information collection form (website membership sign-up screen, event participation, and the like)
- Offline personal information collection form (membership application form and the like)
- Privacy policy

## Nonconformity examples

- The case where, in order to grant benefits such as fee reductions for persons with disabilities, sensitive information about health such as whether a disability exists is collected, but blanket consent was obtained by including it among other personal information items
- The case where, when collecting alien registration numbers from foreigners only at the time of membership sign-up, blanket consent was obtained by including it among other personal information items
- The case where, when obtaining separate consent for the collection of sensitive information or unique identifying information, some of the four matters that must be notified are omitted or notified with incorrect content (such as failing to notify of the right to refuse consent and the disadvantages resulting from refusal of consent)
