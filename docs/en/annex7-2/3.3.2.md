## Certification criterion

When personal information processing tasks are outsourced to a third party, the content of the outsourced tasks and related matters such as the trustee (outsourcee) must be disclosed. In addition, when tasks that promote goods or services or solicit their sale are outsourced, the content of the outsourced tasks and the trustee must be notified to the data subject.

## Key checkpoints

1. When personal information processing tasks are outsourced to a third party (including sub-outsourcing), are the content of the outsourced tasks and the trustee disclosed on the internet website, etc., and kept up to date?
2. When tasks that promote goods or services or solicit their sale are outsourced, are the content of the outsourced tasks and the trustee notified to the data subject by methods such as written document, e-mail, or text message?

## Detailed explanation

> * This item is a relaxed certification criterion (Annex 7-2) under the special provisions for Information Security Management System certification. The above certification criterion / key checkpoints are the officially relaxed/adjusted text, and the Detailed explanation / Related laws / Evidence / Nonconformity examples below are the content of the "ISMS-P Certification Criteria Guide" for the same control domain, Annex 7 [3.3.2 Outsourcing of Personal Information Processing Tasks] (under the relaxed criteria, they apply within the scope of the above certification criterion / checkpoints).

When personal information processing tasks are outsourced to a third party (including sub-outsourcing), the content of the outsourced tasks and the trustee must be kept up to date and continuously disclosed on the internet website, etc., so that the data subject can easily verify them at any time.

- Matters to be notified to the data subject
  1. The content of the outsourced tasks
  2. The party that is entrusted with and processes the personal information processing tasks (trustee)
     - * The "trustee" includes a third party (sub-trustee) that is re-entrusted with the outsourced tasks from the party entrusted with and processing the personal information processing tasks.

- Methods of disclosing outsourcing of personal information processing tasks (Article 28(2) and (3) of the Enforcement Decree of the Personal Information Protection Act)
  - The method of continuously posting the content of the outsourced tasks and the trustee on the outsourcer's internet website
  - When it cannot be posted on the internet website, disclosure methods (use at least one of the following methods)
    1. The method of posting it in a conspicuous location such as the outsourcer's place of business
    2. The method of publishing it in the official gazette (only where the outsourcer is a public institution) or in a general daily newspaper, general weekly newspaper, or internet newspaper under Article 2(1)(a)/(c) and Article 2(2) of the "Act on the Promotion of Newspapers, etc." whose main distribution area is a region at or above the city/province level where the outsourcer's place of business, etc. is located
    3. The method of continuously publishing it in a publication / newsletter / promotional material or invoice, etc. that is issued at least twice a year under the same title and distributed to data subjects
    4. The method of including it in a contract, etc. prepared by the outsourcer and the data subject in order to provide goods or services and issuing it to the data subject

- Points to note
  - Even if there are many trustees, all of the trustee names must be listed and disclosed
  - Where sub-outsourcing exists, matters concerning sub-outsourcing must also be disclosed together
  - When the content of the outsourced tasks or the trustee changes, the changed content must be reflected without delay and disclosed through the internet website, etc.

When tasks that promote goods or services or solicit their sale are outsourced, the content of the outsourced tasks and the trustee must be notified to the data subject by methods such as written document, e-mail, or text message.

- Notification method : Methods such as written document (written document, e-mail, fax, telephone, text message, or a comparable method)
- Matters to be notified : The content of the outsourced tasks, the trustee

* For matters concerning the outsourcing contract, the outsourcer's consent for sub-outsourcing, and the management/supervision of the trustee in relation to outsourcing of personal information processing tasks, refer to the certification criteria of domain 2.3 (Security of External Parties).

## Related laws

- Personal Information Protection Act Article 26 (Restrictions on Processing of Personal Information Following Outsourcing of Tasks)

## Evidence (examples)

- Privacy policy (disclosure records related to outsourcing of personal information processing tasks)
- Personal information collection form
- Personal information processing outsourcing contract
- Records of notification to data subjects related to outsourcing of tasks for promoting goods or services or soliciting their sale

## Nonconformity examples

- The case where outsourcing of personal information processing tasks is disclosed in the privacy policy on the website, but some trustees and the content of the outsourced tasks are omitted
- The case where tasks that promote goods or services or solicit their sale are outsourced, but the content of the outsourced tasks and the trustee are not notified to the data subject by methods such as written document, and this is instead substituted with disclosure in the privacy policy
- The case where the trustee of personal information processing tasks changed due to termination of the contract with the existing trustee, but this was not reflected in the privacy policy without delay
- The case where the party entrusted with the personal information processing tasks is re-entrusting the tasks to a third party, but matters concerning the sub-outsourcing are not disclosed on the internet website, etc.
