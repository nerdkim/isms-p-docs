## Certification criterion

When signs or the occurrence of an intrusion incident or personal information leak are recognized, the organization shall comply with legal notification and reporting obligations, respond and recover promptly according to procedures, analyze the incident, and then establish measures to prevent recurrence and reflect them in the response system.

## Key checkpoints

1. When signs or the occurrence of an intrusion incident or personal information leak are recognized, is prompt response and reporting carried out according to the defined intrusion incident response procedures?
2. When a personal information incident occurs, are the procedures for notifying data subjects (users) and reporting to relevant agencies carried out in accordance with the relevant laws?
3. After an intrusion incident is closed, is the cause of the incident analyzed, the results reported, and shared with relevant organizations and personnel?
4. Are measures established to prevent similar incidents from recurring by using the information obtained through the intrusion incident analysis, and, where necessary, are the intrusion incident response procedures modified?

## Detailed explanation

> Note: This item is a **relaxed certification criterion (Annex 7-2)** under the special provisions for information security management system certification. The certification criterion / key checkpoints above are the officially relaxed/adjusted text, while the detailed explanation / related laws / evidence / nonconformity examples below are from the content of Annex 7 [2.11.5 Incident Response and Recovery] in the "ISMS-P Certification Criteria Guide", which is the same control area (under the relaxed criterion, they apply within the scope of the certification criterion / checkpoints above).

When signs or the occurrence of an intrusion incident or personal information leak are recognized, prompt response and reporting shall be carried out according to the defined intrusion incident response procedures.

- Initial response to an intrusion incident and evidence preservation measures
  - Measures to delete/change access privileges or block access for information systems suspected of intrusion
  - Security inspection of internal and external systems such as networks and firewalls, and remediation of vulnerabilities
  - Measures to preserve evidence such as external access logs necessary for incident investigation
  - Verification of whether personal information and important information has been leaked through log analysis, etc.
- Preparation of an intrusion incident report including the following items and internal reporting
  - Date and time the intrusion incident occurred
  - Reporter and date and time of report
  - Incident content (findings, damage details, etc.)
  - Progress of incident response
  - Time taken until incident response, etc.
- If the impact of the intrusion incident on the organization is severe, prompt reporting up to top management in accordance with the reporting procedures

When a personal information incident occurs, the procedures for notifying data subjects and reporting to relevant agencies shall be carried out in accordance with the relevant laws.

After an intrusion incident is closed, the cause of the incident shall be analyzed, the results reported, and shared with relevant organizations and personnel.

- After an intrusion incident has been handled and closed, perform an analysis of the cause of the incident, prepare a results report, and report it to the responsible person
- Share intrusion incident information and the discovered vulnerabilities, causes, and remediation measures with relevant organizations and personnel

Measures shall be established to prevent similar incidents from recurring by using the information obtained through the intrusion incident analysis, and, where necessary, the intrusion incident response procedures shall be modified.

- Establish recurrence prevention measures to prevent similar incidents from being repeated by using the information obtained through the intrusion incident analysis
- Where necessary based on the analyzed results, make changes to the intrusion incident response system, such as the intrusion incident response procedures and the information security policies and procedures

## Related laws

- Personal Information Protection Act Article 34 (Notification/Reporting of Leakage, etc. of Personal Information)
- Network Act Article 48-3 (Reporting of Intrusion Incidents, etc.), Article 48-4 (Cause Analysis of Intrusion Incidents, etc.)

## Evidence (examples)

- Intrusion incident response procedures
- Intrusion incident response report
- Intrusion incident management ledger
- Personal information leakage report
- Emergency contact list

## Nonconformity examples

- The internal intrusion incident response guidelines require that, when an intrusion incident occurs, it be reported to the internal information security committee and the interested departments, but when an intrusion incident occurred, the department in charge took response measures on its own and did not report to the information security committee and the interested departments.
- There was a recent case in which part of a service was interrupted due to an intrusion incident suspected to be a DDoS attack, but no cause analysis or recurrence prevention measures were established.
- A personal information leakage incident occurred due to external hacking, but notification and reporting were not carried out within 72 hours on the grounds that the number of leaked personal information records was small.
- Due to a staff member's mistake, personal information of 1,000 or more data subjects was leaked through an internet homepage bulletin board, but leakage notification was not provided to the relevant data subjects.
