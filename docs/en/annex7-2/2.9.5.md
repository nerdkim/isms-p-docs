# 2.9.5 Removable Storage Media Management  _( Annex 7-2 )_

| Field | Value |

## Certification criterion

Management procedures shall be established and implemented so that personal information or important information is not leaked and malicious code is not infected through removable storage media, and removable storage media containing personal information or important information shall be kept in a secure location.

## Key checkpoints

1. Are policies and procedures for the handling (use), storage, disposal, and reuse of removable storage media such as external hard drives, USB memory, and CDs established and implemented?
2. Are the holdings status, use, and management conditions of removable storage media periodically inspected?
3. Is the use of removable storage media restricted in controlled areas and important restricted areas where major information systems are located?
4. Are measures in place to prevent malicious code infection and leakage of important information through removable storage media?
5. Are removable storage media containing personal information or important information kept in a secure location with a locking device?

## Detailed explanation

> * This item is a **relaxed certification criterion (Annex 7-2)** under the special case of information security management system certification. The certification criterion / key checkpoints above are the officially relaxed/adjusted text, and the detailed explanation / related laws / evidence / nonconformity examples below are the content of the "ISMS-P Certification Criteria Guide" for the same control area, Annex 7 [2.10.7 Removable Storage Media Management] (under the relaxed criteria, they apply within the scope of the certification criterion / checkpoints above).

Policies and procedures for the handling (use), storage, disposal, and reuse of removable storage media such as external hard drives, USB memory, and CDs shall be established and implemented.

- Method for managing the holdings status of removable storage media: removable storage media management ledger, etc.
- Procedure for authorizing and registering the use of removable storage media
- Procedure for managing the carry-in/carry-out of removable storage media
- Procedure for disposing of and reusing removable storage media
- Scope of use of removable storage media: usage policies and procedures per protection area such as controlled areas and restricted areas
- Protection measures for removable storage media, etc.

The holdings status, use, and management conditions of removable storage media shall be periodically inspected.

- Inspection of management conditions such as reviewing evidence of use approval for removable storage media, holdings status, management ledger, and usage history

The use of removable storage media shall be restricted in controlled areas and important restricted areas where major information systems are located.

- When use is unavoidable, use in accordance with lawful procedures after going through the responsible person's approval procedure
- Periodic review of the removable storage media usage status within controlled areas and important restricted areas

Measures shall be prepared to prevent malicious code infection and leakage of important information through removable storage media.

- Establishment/implementation of protection measures such as preventing automatic execution of removable storage media and using them only after antivirus program scanning

Removable storage media containing personal information or important information shall be kept in a secure location with a locking device.

- Removable storage media containing personal information or important information (portable hard disks, USB memory, SSDs, etc.) shall be kept in a safe, a secure location with a locking device

## Related laws

- Personal Information Protection Act Article 29 (Duty of Safety Measures)
- Standards for Measures to Secure the Safety of Personal Information Article 10 (Physical Safety Measures)

## Evidence (examples)

- Policy for blocking removable storage media (USB, CD, etc.)
- Removable storage media management ledger
- History of removable storage media condition inspections

## Nonconformity examples

- A case where a policy restricting the use of removable storage media in the server room, a controlled area, has been established and operated, but multiple instances of using removable storage media without complying with the exception approval procedure were identified, and because periodic inspection of the removable storage media management conditions was not carried out, the removable storage media management ledger was not adequately kept up to date
- A case where removable storage media containing personal information are not kept in a secure location with a locking device but are left in office drawers, etc.
- A case where a removable storage media control solution has been introduced/operated, but for some users, writing and the like were permitted through exception processing without an appropriate approval procedure
- A case where writing to ordinary USB memory is possible on some shared PCs and computing equipment located in the computer room, but controls such as restricting the carry-in and use of media and recording and reviewing usage history are not applied
