## Certification criterion

When personal information is transferred or received due to a business transfer/merger, etc., appropriate protective measures such as notification of the data subject must be established and implemented.

## Key checkpoints

1. When personal information is transferred to another person due to the transfer/merger, etc. of all or part of a business, are the necessary matters notified to the data subject in advance?
2. Does the party receiving the personal information, where it falls under the statutory notification requirements, notify the data subject without delay of the necessary matters, such as the fact that the personal information has been received?
3. Does the party receiving the personal information use it or provide it to a third party only for the original purpose that applied at the time of transfer?

## Detailed explanation

> Note: This item is a relaxed certification criterion (Annex 7-2) under the special provisions for information security management system certification. The certification criterion/key checkpoints above are the relaxed/adjusted official original text, while the detailed explanation/related laws/evidence/nonconformity examples below are the content of the "ISMS-P Certification Criteria Guide" for the same control area, Annex 7 [3.3.3 Transfer of Personal Information Due to Business Transfer, etc.] (under the relaxed criteria, they apply within the scope of the certification criterion/checkpoints above).

When personal information is transferred to another person due to the transfer/merger, etc. of all or part of a business, the following matters must be notified to the data subject in advance.

- Matters to be notified
  1. The fact that the personal information is to be transferred
  2. The name, address, telephone number, and other contact information of the party receiving the personal information
  3. The methods and procedures by which the data subject can take action if they do not wish the personal information to be transferred
- Methods of notification
  1. Any one of the following methods: email/written document/fax/telephone, or a similar method
  2. Where the data subject cannot be notified directly because their contact information is unknown without fault, etc., posting on the internet website for at least 30 days
    - However, in the case of a transferor, etc. that does not operate an internet website, posting the notice for at least 30 days in a conspicuous place such as the place of business
    - Alternatively, using the method of publishing it in a general daily newspaper/general weekly newspaper or internet newspaper under Article 2, subparagraph 1, item (a)/item (c), or subparagraph 2 of the Act on the Promotion of Newspapers, etc., whose principal circulation area is the region at or above the level of the city/province (Si/Do) where the business transferor's, etc. place of business is located

The business transferee, etc., where it falls under the statutory notification requirements, must notify the data subject without delay of the necessary matters, such as the fact that the personal information has been received.

- If the transferor has notified the data subject of the fact of transfer, the transferee does not need to give an additional notification
- However, where personal information has been received through a business transfer, etc. but the party transferring the personal information has not notified the fact of transfer, the fact of transfer must be notified to the data subject

The party receiving the personal information must use it or provide it to a third party only for the original purpose that applied at the time of transfer.

- Where the party that has received the personal information intends to use or provide the personal information beyond the scope of the original purpose, they must separately obtain the consent of the data subject

## Related laws

- Personal Information Protection Act Article 27 (Restrictions on Transfer of Personal Information Due to Business Transfer, etc.)

## Evidence (examples)

- Records of notification to the data subject regarding the transfer of personal information (at the time of business transfer/acquisition)
- Personal information processing policy

## Nonconformity examples

- Where a personal information controller received personal information through a business acquisition but did not notify the data subject of the fact of the personal information transfer, even though the transferor had not notified the fact of the personal information transfer
- Where, upon receiving personal information through a business transfer/acquisition, etc., the methods and procedures by which the data subject can take action if they do not wish the transfer were not prepared, or these were not notified to the data subject
