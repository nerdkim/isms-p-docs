## Certification criterion

Vulnerability assessments shall be performed regularly to verify whether the information system's vulnerabilities are exposed, and any vulnerabilities found shall be remediated promptly. In addition, the occurrence of the latest security vulnerabilities shall be continuously identified, and their impact on the information system shall be analyzed and addressed.

## Key checkpoints

1. Has a procedure for information system vulnerability assessment been established, and are assessments performed regularly?
2. Are found vulnerabilities remediated, and are the results reported to the responsible manager?
3. Is the occurrence of the latest security vulnerabilities continuously identified, and is their impact on the information system analyzed and addressed?
4. Is the vulnerability assessment history recorded and managed so that protective measures are prepared for problems such as the recurrence of vulnerabilities identified in the previous year?

## Detailed explanation

> Note: This item is a **relaxed certification criterion (Annex 7-2)** under the special case of information security management system certification. The certification criterion / key checkpoints above are the official text as relaxed/adjusted, while the detailed explanation / related laws / evidence / nonconformity examples below are the content of the "ISMS-P Certification Criteria Guide" for the same control domain, Annex 7 [2.11.2 Vulnerability Assessment and Remediation] (under the relaxed criteria, they apply within the scope of the certification criterion / checkpoints above).

### A procedure for information system vulnerability assessment shall be established, and assessments shall be performed regularly.

- Matters to be included in the vulnerability assessment procedure
  - Vulnerability assessment targets (e.g., servers, network equipment, etc.)
  - Vulnerability assessment frequency (considering legal requirements, importance, etc.)
  - Designation of the person in charge of vulnerability assessment and the responsible manager
  - Vulnerability assessment procedures and methods, etc.
  - Remediation criteria based on importance
  - Procedure for reporting vulnerability assessment results
  - Security review of unremediated vulnerabilities, etc.
  - Other matters necessary for the prevention and recovery of security incidents, etc.
- Vulnerability assessment targets
  - Network system configuration and setting vulnerabilities, such as routers and switches
  - Server OS security setting vulnerabilities
  - Security system vulnerabilities, such as firewalls
  - Application vulnerabilities
  - Web service vulnerabilities
  - Vulnerabilities of smart devices and mobile services (mobile apps, etc.), etc.
- When conducting vulnerability assessments, consider performing penetration testing according to the size of the company and the importance of the information held
- Personal information controllers must include matters concerning vulnerability assessment for preventing leakage and theft of personal information in the internal management plan and establish/implement them (Standards for Measures to Secure the Safety of Personal Information, Article 4(1)(10))

### Found vulnerabilities shall be remediated, and the results shall be reported to the responsible manager.

- Prepare a report that includes the assessment date and time, assessment targets, assessment methods, assessment content and results, findings, and remediation actions, etc., so that a history can be managed during vulnerability assessment
- After completing response actions for each vulnerability, verify completion through follow-up checks, etc.
- For vulnerabilities that cannot unavoidably be remediated, clearly confirm the reason, and report the associated risk and compensating measures, etc., to the responsible manager

### The occurrence of the latest security vulnerabilities shall be continuously identified, and their impact on the information system shall be analyzed and addressed.

- In addition to regular security vulnerability assessments, continuously identify the latest security vulnerabilities
- When a latest security vulnerability is found, analyze the impact of the security vulnerability on the information system and take response action when necessary

### The vulnerability assessment history shall be recorded and managed, and protective measures shall be prepared for problems such as the recurrence of vulnerabilities identified in the previous year.

- Record management of the vulnerability assessment history
- During vulnerability assessment, compare and analyze against past vulnerability assessment results to verify whether vulnerabilities recur
- When similar vulnerabilities recur, analyze the root cause and prepare measures to prevent recurrence

## Related laws

- Personal Information Protection Act, Article 29 (Duty of Safety Measures)
- Standards for Measures to Secure the Safety of Personal Information, Article 4 (Establishment/Implementation and Inspection of the Internal Management Plan), Article 6 (Access Control)

## Evidence (examples)

- Vulnerability assessment plan
- Vulnerability assessment result report (web, mobile app, server, network system, security system, DBMS, etc.)
- Vulnerability assessment history
- Vulnerability remediation plan
- Vulnerability remediation completion report
- Penetration testing plan / result report

## Nonconformity examples

- The case where internal regulations stipulate that technical vulnerability assessments of major systems be conducted at least once a year, but some of the major systems are omitted from the vulnerability assessment scope
- The case where remediation measures for vulnerabilities found in a vulnerability assessment have not been implemented, or where there is no record of feasibility review and approval for vulnerabilities that cannot be remediated within a short period
