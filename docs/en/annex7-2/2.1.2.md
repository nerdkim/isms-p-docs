# 2.1.2 Awareness Raising and Education/Training  _( Annex 7-2 )_

| Field | Value |

## Certification criterion

Executives, employees, and relevant external parties shall establish and operate annual awareness-raising activities and education/training plans so that they can understand the organization's management system and policies and secure job-specific expertise, and the effectiveness shall be evaluated based on the results and reflected in the next plan.

## Key checkpoints

1. Is an annual education plan that includes the timing, period, target, content, and method of information security and personal information protection education established and approved by management?
2. Is education conducted regularly at least once a year according to the annual education plan for all executives, employees, and external parties within the scope of the management system, and is additional education conducted when there are significant changes to relevant laws and regulations?
3. Is information security and personal information protection education conducted before work begins when hiring executives/employees and when concluding new contracts with external parties?
4. Do executives and employees within the IT, information security, and personal information protection organizations receive separate education to enhance job-specific expertise related to information security and personal information protection?
5. Are records of education implementation kept, and are the effectiveness and adequacy of education evaluated and reflected in the next education plan?

## Detailed explanation

> * This item is a **relaxed certification criterion (Annex 7-2)** under the special provisions for information security management system certification. The certification criterion and key checkpoints above are the relaxed/adjusted official text, and the detailed explanation/related laws/evidence/nonconformity examples below are from the "ISMS-P Certification Criteria Guide" for Annex 7 [2.2.4 Awareness Raising and Education/Training], which is the same control area (under the relaxed criteria, they apply within the scope of the certification criterion/checkpoints above).

The annual information security and personal information protection education plan shall be established with specific inclusion of the timing, period, target, content, method, etc. of education as follows, and shall be approved by management.

- Education types : awareness-raising education for executives and employees, education for key job holders and personal information handlers, education for entrusted parties (processors), specialized education, etc.
- Education plan : education purpose, education target, education content, education method, education schedule, education time, etc. (differentiated according to business scale, amount of personal information held, nature of work, education target, education type, etc.)
- Education approval : reviewing and approving the education plan, and supporting budget allocation so that it can be implemented according to the plan, etc.

Education shall be conducted regularly at least once a year according to the annual education plan for all executives, employees, and external parties within the scope of the management system, and additional education shall be conducted when there are significant changes to relevant laws and regulations.

- Include all personnel who access information assets directly or indirectly, such as executives and employees, temporary staff, and outsourcing service provider staff
- In the case of entrusted parties (processors) and dispatched staff, provide relevant materials so that the relevant company can conduct education, and manage/supervise whether it is implemented
- Conduct education at least once a year (in particular, in the case of personal information handlers, personal information protection education is required at least once a year according to legal requirements)
- The education content shall include everything necessary so that executives, employees, and relevant external parties can understand and comply with the organization's management system and policies

  * Content to be included in information security and personal information protection education (examples)
  - Basic overview of information security and personal information protection, establishment of the management system and methods, relevant laws
  - Internal regulations related to information security and personal information protection, administrative/technical/physical measures
  - Cases of infringement (leakage) incidents involving important information and personal information and response measures, legal liability in case of violation of regulations, etc.

- Prepare and implement education methods for personnel who could not attend education due to business trips, vacations, work, etc. (additional education for non-attendees, cascade education, online education, etc.)
- Conduct additional education when there are significant changes to internal regulations and procedures, occurrence of internal/external infringement incidents, changes to relevant laws, etc. (however, when the matter is not important, it may be replaced with a bulletin board notice, email guidance, booklet distribution, etc.)

Information security and personal information protection education shall be conducted before work begins when hiring executives/employees and when concluding new contracts with external parties.

- Conduct information security and personal information protection education at the time new personnel arrive or before they perform work, so that they are familiar with the organization's policies, precautions, legal liability in case of violation of regulations, etc.

Executives and employees within the IT, information security, and personal information protection organizations shall be able to receive separate education to enhance job-specific expertise related to information security and personal information protection.

- Relevant job holders : IT job holders, chief information security officer, personal information protection officer, personal information handlers, information security job holders, etc.
- Education courses : participation in information security and personal information protection related conferences/seminars/workshops, education entrusted to specialized education institutions, internal education by inviting external experts, etc.

Records of education implementation shall be kept, and the effectiveness and adequacy of education shall be evaluated and reflected in the next education plan.

- After conducting education, keep records such as education notices, education materials, and attendance sheets, and evaluate the appropriateness and effectiveness of the education content through surveys or tests according to pre-established evaluation criteria
- Prepare measures for improvement points derived from the education evaluation results and reflect them when establishing the next education plan

## Related laws

- Personal Information Protection Act Article 26 (Restrictions on Processing of Personal Information Following Entrustment of Work), Article 28 (Supervision of Personal Information Handlers), Article 29 (Duty of Safety Measures)
- Standards for Measures to Ensure the Safety of Personal Information Article 4 (Establishment/Implementation and Inspection of Internal Management Plans)

## Evidence (examples)

- Information security and personal information protection education plan
- Education results report
- Common and job-specific education materials
- List of education attendees

## Nonconformity examples

- The case where an annual information security and personal information protection education plan was established and implemented in the previous year, but no annual information security and personal information protection education plan was established in the current year without a valid reason
- The case where the annual information security and personal information protection education plan specifies the education cycle and target, but does not include content such as the implementation schedule, content, and method
- The case where the annual information security and personal information protection education plan schedules a certain amount of time for personal information protection awareness education targeting all employees, but does not include job-specific personal information protection education plans required for the personal information protection officer, personal information staff, etc.
- The case where, upon reviewing the information security and personal information protection education plan and results report, outsourcing service provider staff who access information assets and facilities within the certification scope (cleaning staff entering the data center, security guards, external developers, etc.) were omitted from the education targets
- The case where information security and personal information protection education was conducted in the current year, but some of the records regarding education implementation and evaluation (education materials, attendance sheets, evaluation questionnaires, results report, etc.) are not kept
- The case where those who did not complete information security and personal information protection education are not identified, or additional education methods (cascade education, additional education, online education, etc.) for such non-completers are not established/implemented
