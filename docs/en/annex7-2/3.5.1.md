## Certification criterion

A personal information processing policy shall be established so that it is easy for the data subject to understand and includes all necessary matters such as the purpose of processing personal information, and it shall be disclosed by appropriate methods and continuously kept up to date so that the data subject can easily review it at any time.

## Key checkpoints

1. Is the personal information processing policy written specifically and clearly in plain, easy-to-understand terms, including all matters required by law?
2. Is the personal information processing policy disclosed on an Internet website or similar means, kept continuously up to date, so that the data subject can easily review it?
3. When the personal information processing policy is changed, are the reasons for and the details of the change announced without delay, and are measures taken so that the data subject can easily identify the changes at any time?

## Detailed explanation

> Note: This item is a relaxed certification criterion (Annex 7-2) under the special provisions for information security management system certification. The certification criterion and key checkpoints above are the official text as relaxed/adjusted; the detailed explanation, related laws, evidence, and nonconformity examples below are the content from the "ISMS-P Certification Criteria Guide" for the same control area, Annex 7 [3.5.1 Disclosure of the Personal Information Processing Policy] (under the relaxed criterion, they apply within the scope of the certification criterion and checkpoints above).

**The personal information processing policy shall be written specifically and clearly in plain, easy-to-understand terms, including all matters required by law.**

- Mandatory matters to be included in the personal information processing policy (see Article 30 of the Personal Information Protection Act and Article 31 of the Enforcement Decree of the same Act)
  1. The purpose of processing personal information
  2. The processing and retention period of personal information
  3. Matters concerning provision of personal information to third parties (specified only where applicable)
  4. The procedures and methods for destroying personal information (where personal information must be preserved under the proviso of Article 21 (1) of the Act, this shall include the grounds for preservation and the items of personal information preserved)
  5. The possibility of disclosure of sensitive information under Article 23 (3) of the Act and the method of choosing non-disclosure (specified only where applicable)
  6. Matters concerning the outsourcing of personal information processing (specified only where applicable)
  7. Matters concerning the processing of pseudonymized information under Articles 28-2 and 28-3 of the Act (specified only where applicable)
  8. Matters concerning the rights/obligations of data subjects and their legal representatives and the methods of exercising them
  9. The name of the chief privacy officer under Article 31, or the name of the department that handles personal information protection duties and related grievances, together with its telephone number and other contact information
  10. Matters concerning the installation/operation of devices that automatically collect personal information, such as Internet access information files, and how to refuse them (specified only where applicable)
  11. The items of personal information processed
  12. Matters concerning measures to ensure the safety of personal information under Article 30 of the Enforcement Decree
- Other matters to be included in the personal information processing policy (Article 19 of the Standard Personal Information Protection Guidelines)
  1. Where transfer abroad is necessary in order to outsource the processing/storage of personal information under Article 28-8 (1) 3 of the Act, the matters in each subparagraph of Article 28-8 (2) of the Act (specified only where applicable)
  2. Matters concerning changes to the personal information processing policy
  3. Where a domestic agent is designated under Article 31-2 (1) of the Act, the name, address, telephone number, and email address of the domestic agent (specified only where applicable)
  4. Matters concerning the rights/obligations of data subjects and their legal representatives and the methods of exercising them, such as the right to demand access, correction/deletion, and suspension of processing of personal information
  5. The department that receives/handles requests for access to personal information
  6. Remedies for infringement of the rights and interests of data subjects
- Personal information that may be processed without the consent of the data subject shall be disclosed in the personal information processing policy, with its items and the legal grounds for processing distinguished from personal information processed with the consent of the data subject
- The content of the personal information processing policy, such as the purpose of processing personal information, the items of personal information processed, and matters concerning provision to third parties, shall be consistent with the actual status of personal information processing, and shall be written specifically and clearly in plain, easy-to-understand terms reflecting the characteristics of the service and the data subject
  1. Matters that the Act requires to be included in the personal information processing policy, such as the grounds for processing personal information and the guarantee of data subjects' rights, shall be established specifically and appropriately
  2. The personal information processing policy shall be established in clear and plain language so that it is easy for the data subject to understand
- Where an exception under the Personal Information Protection Act applies, the personal information processing policy need not be established

  Note: Personal information files exempt from the requirement to establish a personal information processing policy (public institutions)
  1. Personal information files that record matters concerning national security, diplomatic secrets, or other significant national interests
  2. Personal information files that record matters concerning criminal investigation, institution and maintenance of prosecution, execution of sentences and custody, correctional dispositions, protective dispositions, dispositions of security surveillance, and immigration control
  3. Personal information files that record matters concerning investigations of offenses under the Punishment of Tax Evaders Act and investigations of offenses under the Customs Act
  4. Personal information files that are recognized as having a low need for continuous management, such as files operated on a one-time basis, and are prescribed by Presidential Decree
     - Personal information files operated to perform simple tasks such as paying attendance allowances for meetings, sending materials/goods, or settling money, for which the need for continuous management is low
     - Personal information files processed temporarily where urgently necessary for public safety and welfare, such as public health
     - Other personal information files collected solely for one-time task processing that are not stored or recorded
  5. Personal information files classified as confidential under other statutes

**The personal information processing policy shall be disclosed with secured accessibility, on an Internet website or similar means, and kept continuously up to date, so that the data subject can easily review it.**

- Use the standardized name "personal information processing policy"
- When disclosing it on the first screen of an Internet website, display it so that the data subject can easily identify it, distinguishing it from other notices by using font size, color, and the like
- Where no Internet website is operated, the personal information processing policy may be disclosed through other methods prescribed by law

  Note: Methods of disclosing the personal information processing policy where it cannot be posted on an Internet website (Article 31 (3) of the Enforcement Decree)
  - Posting it in a conspicuous place at the personal information controller's place of business, etc.
  - Publishing it in the Official Gazette (only where the personal information controller is a public institution), or in a general daily newspaper, general weekly newspaper, or Internet newspaper under Article 2 (1) (a)/(c) and (2) of the Act on the Promotion of Newspapers, etc., whose main distribution area is the Si/Do or a wider area where the personal information controller's place of business, etc. is located
  - Continuously publishing it in a publication/newsletter/promotional material distributed to data subjects at least twice a year under the same title, or in bills, etc.
  - Publishing it in a contract, etc. drawn up between the personal information controller and the data subject to provide goods or services, and issuing it to the data subject

**When the personal information processing policy is changed, the reasons for and the details of the change shall be announced without delay, and measures shall be taken so that the data subject can easily identify the changes at any time.**

- Disclose it by comparing the before/after so that the data subject can easily review the changes at any time

  Note: Methods of announcing the reasons for and details of a change to the personal information processing policy (examples)
  - Announcing it through the notice section on the first screen of the Internet website or through a separate window
  - Announcing it to the data subject in writing/by fax/by email or by similar methods
  - Writing and posting it, or keeping it available, in a conspicuous place within the store/office

## Related laws

- Personal Information Protection Act Article 30 (Establishment and Disclosure of the Personal Information Processing Policy), Article 30-2 (Evaluation of and Recommendation to Improve the Personal Information Processing Policy)

## Evidence (examples)

- Personal information processing policy
- Records of announcements concerning revisions of the personal information processing policy (bulletin boards, etc.)

## Nonconformity examples

- Where the personal information collection and third-party provision details disclosed in the personal information processing policy differ from the details actually collected and provided
- Where a change has occurred among the matters disclosed in the personal information processing policy, such as a change of the chief privacy officer or a change of the outsourced party, but the policy has not been amended to reflect it
- Where the personal information processing policy is disclosed but its name is "personal information protection policy" rather than "personal information processing policy," and it is not made easy for the data subject to find by using font size, color, and the like
- Where the personal information processing policy has been revised several times, but the content of previously drafted personal information processing policies is not disclosed in a way that allows it to be reviewed
- Where personal information is retained for a certain period without being destroyed under other statutes such as the Act on the Consumer Protection in Electronic Commerce or the Commercial Act, but the grounds for such preservation and the items of personal information preserved are not disclosed in the personal information processing policy
