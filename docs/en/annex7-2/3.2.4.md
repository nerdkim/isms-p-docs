## Certification criterion

Personal information must be used or provided only within the purpose disclosed to and consented by the data subject at the time of collection, or within the scope based on statutes. Where it is to be used/provided beyond this scope, additional consent from the data subject must be obtained, or it must be verified whether the case is lawful under the relevant statutes, and appropriate protective measures must be established/implemented.

## Key checkpoints

1. Is personal information used/provided only within the purpose consented to by the data subject at the time of initial collection, or within the scope based on statutes?
2. Where personal information was provided by a personal information controller, is it used/provided only within the scope of the purpose for which it was provided?
3. Where personal information is used or provided beyond the scope of the collection purpose or the purpose for which it was provided by a personal information controller, is this limited to cases where separate consent is obtained from the data subject or where there is a legal basis?
4. Where personal information is provided to a third party for a purpose other than intended, is the recipient requested to restrict the purpose/method, etc. of use or to prepare the measures necessary to secure safety?
5. Where a public institution uses personal information for a purpose other than intended or provides it to a third party, are the necessary matters concerning the legal basis, purpose, and scope of such use or provision, etc. posted in the Official Gazette or on the internet website, etc.?
6. Where a public institution, etc. uses personal information for a purpose other than intended or provides it to a third party, is a procedure in place such as recording/managing it in the register of use for purposes other than intended and provision to third parties?

## Detailed explanation

> * This item is a **relaxed certification criterion (Annex 7-2)** under the special provisions for information security management system certification. The certification criterion/key checkpoints above are the official text as relaxed/adjusted, while the detailed explanation/related laws/evidence/nonconformity examples below are the content of the "ISMS-P Certification Criteria Guide" for Annex 7 [3.2.4 Use and provision of personal information for purposes other than intended], which is the same control area (under the relaxed criteria, they apply within the scope of the certification criterion/checkpoints above).

- Personal information must be used or provided only within the purpose consented to by the data subject at the time of initial collection, or within the scope based on statutes.
  - It is prohibited to use or provide personal information beyond the scope disclosed to the data subject with respect to the purpose of use/provision and consented to, or beyond the scope permitted for use/provision by the Personal Information Protection Act or other statutes
    - * Examples of use/provision of personal information for purposes other than intended
      - Using personal information entered to participate in a customer satisfaction survey, promotional event, or prize event, without prior consent, to send advertising materials guiding one's own discount sales event
      - A home shopping company providing customer information collected to deliver ordered goods, without the data subject's consent, to an affiliated condominium company to be used for sending condominium sales promotional materials

- Where personal information was provided by a personal information controller, it must be used/provided only within the scope of the purpose for which it was provided.
  - A person who received personal information is prohibited from using it for a purpose different from the purpose for which the personal information was provided or received, or from providing it to a third party

- Where personal information is used or provided beyond the scope of the collection purpose or the purpose for which it was provided by a personal information controller, this must be limited to cases where separate consent is obtained from the data subject or where there is a legal basis.
  - Cases where personal information may be used/provided for a purpose other than intended (however, excluding cases where there is a concern that the interests of the data subject or a third party may be unfairly infringed)

| No. | Cases where use/provision for a purpose other than intended is possible | Public institution | Other than public institution |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | Where separate consent is obtained from the data subject | O | O |
| 2 | Where there is a special provision in another statute | O | O |
| 3 | Where it is deemed manifestly necessary for the urgent life, body, or property interests of the data subject or a third party | O | O |
| 4 | Where the duties within jurisdiction prescribed by another statute cannot be performed unless personal information is used for a purpose other than intended or provided to a third party, and the deliberation/resolution of the Personal Information Protection Commission has been passed | O | - |
| 5 | Where it is necessary to provide it to a foreign government or international organization for the implementation of a treaty or other international agreement | O | - |
| 6 | Where it is necessary for the investigation of a crime and the institution/maintenance of a public prosecution | O | - |
| 7 | Where it is necessary for the performance of a court's adjudication duties | O | - |
| 8 | Where it is necessary for the execution of a sentence, custody, or protective disposition | O | - |
| 9 | Where it is urgently necessary for public safety and security, such as public health | O | O |

  - Matters to be disclosed when obtaining consent to use/provide personal information for a purpose other than intended
    1. The recipient of the personal information
    2. The purpose of using the personal information (in the case of provision, the recipient's purpose of use)
    3. The items of personal information to be used or provided
    4. The period of retention and use of the personal information (in the case of provision, the recipient's retention and use period)
    5. The fact that the data subject has the right to refuse consent and, where there is any disadvantage resulting from the refusal of consent, the content of such disadvantage
  - A person who received personal information from another personal information controller must not use it for a purpose other than the purpose for which the personal information was provided, or provide it to a third party, except in a case falling under any of the following
    1. Where separate consent is obtained from the data subject
    2. Where there is a special provision in another statute

- Where personal information is provided to a third party for a purpose other than intended, the recipient must be requested to restrict the purpose/method, etc. of use or to prepare the measures necessary to secure safety.
  - Restrict the purpose of use, method of use, period of use, form of use, etc. for the recipient of the personal information
  - Request, in a document (including an electronic document), that the specific measures necessary to secure the safety of the personal information be prepared
  - Clarify the responsibilities regarding measures to secure the safety of personal information with the recipient of the relevant personal information

- Where a public institution uses personal information for a purpose other than intended or provides it to a third party, the necessary matters concerning the legal basis, purpose, and scope of such use or provision, etc. must be posted in the Official Gazette or on the internet website, etc.
  - Cases where posting in the Official Gazette or on the internet website is not required when using/providing personal information for a purpose other than intended (exceptions)
    1. When using/providing personal information for a purpose other than intended based on the data subject's consent
    2. When using/providing personal information for a purpose other than intended for the investigation of a crime and the institution/maintenance of a public prosecution
  - Matters to be posted in the Official Gazette or on the internet website
    1. The date of the use, etc. for a purpose other than intended
    2. The legal basis for the use, etc. for a purpose other than intended
    3. The purpose of the use, etc. for a purpose other than intended
    4. The items of personal information used, etc. for a purpose other than intended
  - Timing and period of posting in the Official Gazette or on the internet website
    1. Timing of posting: within 30 days from the date of use/provision for a purpose other than intended
    2. Period of posting: 10 days or more where posted on the internet website

- Where a public institution, etc. uses personal information for a purpose other than intended or provides it to a third party, it must be recorded/managed in the register of use for purposes other than intended and provision to third parties.
  - In the case of a public institution, where personal information is used for a purpose other than intended or provided to a third party, it must be recorded/managed in the 'Register of Use of Personal Information for Purposes Other than Intended and Provision to Third Parties'
    - * Matters to be recorded/managed in the 'Register of Use of Personal Information for Purposes Other than Intended and Provision to Third Parties' (Attached Form No. 1 of the "Notice on Methods of Processing Personal Information")
      1. The name of the personal information or personal information file being used or provided
      2. The name of the using institution or the recipient institution
      3. The purpose of use or the purpose of provision
      4. The legal basis for the use or provision
      5. The items of personal information being used or provided
      6. The date, cycle, or period of use or provision
      7. The form in which it is used or provided
      8. Where a request has been made to impose restrictions or to prepare necessary measures for the protection of personal information, the content thereof
  - Prepare a procedure to systematically respond to requests for the provision of data such as personal information from judicial or government agencies (warrants, orders, requests for submission of data, etc.)
    - * Considerations when preparing a procedure to respond to requests for the provision of data such as personal information from judicial or government agencies
      - Establish/implement response procedures such as the legal basis for provision, the request intake channel (including the designation of the responsible organization and person in charge), the request via an official document (warrant, etc.), verification of the requesting agency's person in charge, the principle of providing minimum information, internal reporting and approval procedures, and preservation of related records
      - Use safe procedures and methods so that personal information is not leaked/exposed in the course of provision, and record the details of the personal information provided and retain them for a certain period, etc.

## Related laws

- Personal Information Protection Act Article 18 (Restriction on Use/Provision of Personal Information for Purposes Other than Intended)
- Personal Information Protection Act Article 19 (Restriction on Use/Provision by a Person Who Received Personal Information)

## Evidence (examples)

- Details of use of personal information for purposes other than intended and provision to third parties (including related evidence such as request forms)
- Register of use of personal information for purposes other than intended and provision to third parties (in the case of a public institution)
- Details of posting on the website or in the Official Gazette (in the case of a public institution)
- Guidelines for responding to requests for the provision of data
- Official documents requesting the provision of data, details of personal information provided, registers, etc.

## Nonconformity examples

- Where personal information collected for the purpose of product delivery is used, without prior consent, for mail-order advertising of one's own products
- Where personal information collected to participate in a customer satisfaction survey or prize event is used to send advertisements guiding one's own discount sales event
- Where a public institution provides a petitioner's personal information to another institution for a purpose other than intended based on another statute, but does not post the related matters in the Official Gazette or on the internet website
- Where a public institution provides personal information to a police station for the purpose of a criminal investigation but does not record the related matters in the 'Register of Use of Personal Information for Purposes Other than Intended and Provision to Third Parties'
