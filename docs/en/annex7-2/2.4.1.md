# 2.4.1 User Account Management  _( Annex 7-2 )_

## Certification criterion

To control unauthorized access to information systems and to personal information and important information, and to grant access rights at the minimum level according to business purpose, procedures for user registration/deregistration and for granting/changing/revoking access rights must be established and implemented, and at the time of user registration and authorization it must be stipulated and made known that users bear security responsibility.

## Key checkpoints

1. Are formal procedures for the registration/change/deletion of user accounts and access rights that can access information systems and personal information and important information established and implemented?
2. When creating/registering/changing user accounts and access rights that can access information systems and personal information and important information, are only the minimum rights necessary for the work granted in accordance with the access rights classification scheme by duty?
3. When granting accounts and access rights to users, is it clearly made known that the security responsibility for the account rests with the user themselves?

## Detailed explanation

> * This item is a **relaxed certification criterion (Annex 7-2)** under the special provisions for information security management system certification. The certification criterion/key checkpoints above are the official text as relaxed/adjusted, while the detailed explanation/related laws/evidence/nonconformity examples below are the content of the "ISMS-P Certification Criteria Guide" for Annex 7 [2.5.1 User Account Management], which belongs to the same control domain (under the relaxed criterion, they apply within the scope of the certification criterion/checkpoints above).

To control unauthorized access to information systems and to personal information and important information, a formal procedure for the registration/change/deletion/termination of user accounts and access rights must be established and implemented, taking the following into account.

- Issuance of a unique user account for each user and personal information handler, and prohibition of sharing
- Review of appropriateness through approval procedures, etc., when issuing accounts and granting/changing access rights for users and personal information handlers
- Change or revocation of access rights without delay (including account deletion or deactivation) when a personnel change such as transfer or retirement occurs
- After installation of an information system, remove default accounts, test accounts, etc., from the manufacturer or vendor, or change them to accounts that are difficult to guess
- Maintenance/management of records related to the registration/change/deletion/termination of user accounts and access rights, etc.

When creating/registering/changing user accounts and access rights that can access information systems and personal information and important information, only the minimum rights necessary for the work must be granted in accordance with the access rights classification scheme by duty.

- Access rights to information systems and personal information processing systems are granted differentially to work personnel within the minimum scope according to the purpose of performing the work
- Access rights to important information and personal information are granted only within the scope strictly necessary for the work, in accordance with the principles of need-to-know and need-to-do
- Subdivision of rights so that important information or personal information cannot be accessed unnecessarily or excessively
- Review of appropriateness through approval procedures, etc., when granting or changing rights

When granting accounts and access rights to users, it must be clearly made known that the security responsibility for the account rests with the user themselves.

- Specify the responsibilities and obligations concerning accounts in information security and personal information protection policies, pledges, etc. (prohibition of sharing or lending one's own account and password to others, precautions when logging in at public places, etc.)
- Use various methods such as pledges, email, system notices, and training

## Related laws

- Personal Information Protection Act Article 29 (Duty of Safety Measures)
- Standards for Measures to Ensure the Safety of Personal Information Article 5 (Management of Access Rights)

## Evidence (examples)

- User account and rights application form
- User account and rights management ledger or screen
- Access rights classification table for each information system and personal information processing system
- List of users, administrators, and personal information handlers for each information system and personal information processing system

## Nonconformity examples

- When accounts/rights for users and personal information handlers are processed by verbal request, email, etc., without a user registration, termination, and approval procedure, so that the approval and processing history cannot be verified
- When a personal information handler, citing work backup due to vacation, business trip, official leave, etc., discloses a personal information handler account to a person not designated as a personal information handler without going through the formal procedure
- When excessive rights beyond what is necessary are granted to a user of an information system or personal information processing system, making it possible to access information or personal information unnecessary for the work
