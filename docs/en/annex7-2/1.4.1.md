# 1.4.1 Management System Review  _( Annex 7-2 )_

| Field | Value |

## Certification criterion

To verify whether the management system is being operated effectively in accordance with internal policies and implementation documents, an organization shall assemble personnel with assured independence and expertise to conduct a review at least once a year, and management shall confirm the accuracy and effectiveness of the improvement results for the identified problems.

## Key checkpoints

1. Has a management system review plan been established that includes the review criteria, scope, cycle, and qualification requirements for review personnel, in order to verify whether the information security and personal information protection management system is being operated effectively in accordance with legal requirements and the established policies?
2. In accordance with the management system review plan, is a review conducted at least once a year by personnel with assured independence, objectivity, and expertise, and are the identified problems reported to management?

## Detailed explanation

> ※ This item is a **relaxed certification criterion (Annex 7-2)** under the special provisions for information security management system certification. The certification criterion and key checkpoints above are the official relaxed/adjusted text; the detailed explanation, related laws, evidence, and nonconformity examples below are the "ISMS-P Certification Criteria Guide" content for Annex 7 [1.4.2 Management System Review], which belongs to the same control domain (it applies within the scope of the certification criterion and key checkpoints above).


An organization shall establish a management system review plan that includes the review criteria, scope, cycle, and qualification requirements for review personnel, in order to check whether the information security and personal information protection management system is operated effectively in accordance with legal requirements and established policies, and shall report the plan to management.

- Review criteria: Include the information security and personal information protection management system certification criteria
- Review scope: Include the entire organization or the certification scope
- Review cycle: Must be conducted at least once a year
- Qualification requirements for review personnel: Define qualification requirements to assure the objectivity, independence, and expertise of the review

In accordance with the management system review plan, an organization shall form a team of personnel with assured independence, objectivity, and expertise, conduct a review at least once a year, and report problems identified to management such as the Chief Information Security Officer and the Chief Privacy Officer.

- Compose the review organization so as to assure the objectivity, independence, and expertise of the review
- Conduct the review at least once a year in accordance with the review plan
- Prepare a review result report and report it to management such as the Chief Information Security Officer and the Chief Privacy Officer

## Related laws

- Personal Information Protection Act, Article 29 (Duty of Safeguards)
- Standards for Measures to Ensure the Safety of Personal Information, Article 4 (Establishment/Implementation and Review of the Internal Management Plan)

## Evidence (examples)

- Management system review plan (internal review plan, internal audit plan)
- Management system review result report
- Minutes of the information security and personal information protection committee

## Nonconformity examples

- A case where an IT team staff member identified as a review target was included among the management system review personnel and was involved in reviewing the IT team's management area, thereby undermining the independence of the review
- A case where a management system review was conducted this year, but the review scope was limited to certain areas and did not cover the full scope of the information security and personal information protection management system
- A case where the management system review team consisted only of internal staff and external consultants who had participated in the management system establishment process such as risk assessment or vulnerability review, so the independence of the review cannot be regarded as assured
