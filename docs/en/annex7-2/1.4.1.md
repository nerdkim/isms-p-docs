## Certification criterion

To verify whether the management system is being operated effectively in accordance with internal policies and implementation documents, an organization shall assemble personnel with assured independence and expertise to conduct a review at least once a year, and management shall confirm the accuracy and effectiveness of the improvement results for the identified problems.

## Key checkpoints

1. Has a management system review plan been established that includes the review criteria, scope, cycle, and qualification requirements for review personnel, in order to verify whether the information security and personal information protection management system is being operated effectively in accordance with legal requirements and the established policies?
2. In accordance with the management system review plan, is a review conducted at least once a year by personnel with assured independence, objectivity, and expertise, and are the identified problems reported to management?

## Detailed explanation

> * This item is a **relaxed certification criterion (Annex 7-2)** under the special provisions for information security management system certification. The certification criterion and key checkpoints above are the relaxed/adjusted official text, while the detailed explanation, related laws, evidence, and nonconformity examples below are drawn from the "ISMS-P Certification Criteria Guide" for the same control area, Annex 7 [1.4.2 Management System Review] (under the relaxed criteria, they apply within the scope of the above certification criterion and checkpoints).

To verify whether the information security and personal information protection management system is being operated effectively in accordance with legal requirements and the established policies, a management system review plan that includes the review criteria, scope, cycle, and qualification requirements for review personnel shall be established and reported to management.

- Review criteria: Include the information security and personal information protection management system certification criteria
- Review scope: Include the entire organization or the certification scope
- Review cycle: Must be performed at least once a year
- Qualification requirements for review personnel: Define qualification requirements so that the objectivity, independence, and expertise of the review can be assured

In accordance with the management system review plan, personnel with assured independence, objectivity, and expertise shall be assembled to conduct a review at least once a year, and the identified problems shall be reported to management such as the Chief Information Security Officer and the Chief Privacy Officer.

- Organize the review team so that the objectivity, independence, and expertise of the review can be assured
- Conduct the review at least once a year in accordance with the review plan
- Prepare a review results report and report it to management such as the Chief Information Security Officer and the Chief Privacy Officer

## Related laws

- Personal Information Protection Act Article 29 (Duty of Safety Measures)
- Standards for Measures to Ensure the Safety of Personal Information Article 4 (Establishment/Implementation and Review of the Internal Management Plan)

## Evidence (examples)

- Management system review plan (internal review plan, internal audit plan)
- Management system review results report
- Minutes of the information security and personal information protection committee

## Nonconformity examples

- The independence of the review was compromised because an IT team employee, who had been identified as a review target, was included among the management system review personnel and was involved in reviewing the IT team's management area
- A management system review was conducted for the current year, but the review scope was limited to some areas and did not cover the full scope of the information security and personal information protection management system
- The management system review team was composed solely of internal employees and external consultants who had participated in the management system establishment process, such as risk assessment or vulnerability review, so the independence of the review cannot be considered assured
