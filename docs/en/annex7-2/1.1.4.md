## Certification criterion

The scope of the management system shall be defined taking the organization's core services into account, and the related services as well as the organization, assets, physical locations, and so on shall be documented.

## Key checkpoints

1. Is the scope of the management system defined so as to include the core assets that may affect the organization's core services?
2. Where exceptions exist within the defined scope, are the relevant grounds, such as clear justification, consultation with relevant parties, and approval by the responsible officer, recorded and managed?
3. To allow the scope of the information security management system to be clearly confirmed, are documents containing the relevant content (status of major services and operations, list of information systems, list of documents, etc.) prepared and managed?

## Detailed explanation

> * This item is a **relaxed certification criterion (Annex 7-2)** applied under the special exception for information security management system certification. The certification criterion / key checkpoints above are the relaxed/adjusted official text, while the detailed explanation / related laws / evidence / nonconformity examples below are drawn from the "ISMS-P Certification Criteria Guide" for the same control area, Annex 7 [1.1.4 Scope Definition] (under the relaxed criterion, they apply within the scope of the certification criterion / checkpoints above).

The scope of the management system shall be defined so as to include the core assets that may affect the organization's core services and the processing of personal information.

- The scope of the management system shall include, without omission, tangible and intangible core assets related to the business (service), such as executives and employees, information systems, information, and facilities
- In particular, in the case of entities obligated to obtain the information security management system certification, the scope shall be defined so that the information and communications services and related information assets required by law are mandatorily included

Where exceptions exist within the defined scope, the relevant grounds, such as clear justification, consultation with relevant parties, and approval by the responsible officer, shall be recorded and managed.

- Where the scope of the information security management system differs from that of the personal information protection management system, the list of information assets within the certification scope (personal information, systems, networks, etc.) shall be clearly identified and defined from the perspectives of both the information security management system and the personal information protection management system
- For services, information systems, and so on that are excluded from the certification scope, the justification and grounds shall be recorded and managed after undergoing internal consultation and approval by the responsible officer

Documents containing the relevant content (status of major services and operations, list of information systems, list of documents, etc.) shall be prepared and managed so that the scope of the information security and personal information protection management system can be clearly confirmed.

- Status of major services and operations (including the status of personal information processing operations)
- Status of the organization related to service provision (organization chart, etc.)
- Status of the information security and personal information protection organization
- List of major facilities
- List of information systems and network configuration diagram
- Criteria for identifying information assets and personal-information-related assets, and asset status
- List of information security and personal information protection systems
- Service (system) configuration diagram and personal information processing flow (collection, use, provision, storage, management, destruction)
- List of documents (e.g., policies, guidelines, manuals, operation specifications, etc.)
- Method and procedures for establishing the information security and personal information protection management system, review of relevant legal compliance, internal audit
- Status of outsourcing (subcontracting) companies such as customer centers, IDC, IT development and operation, etc.

## Related laws

_No individual legal provision directly corresponding to this item is specified._

## Evidence (examples)

- Statement defining the scope of the information security and personal information protection management system
- List of information assets and personal information
- List of documents
- Service flow diagram
- Personal information flow diagram
- Company-wide organization chart
- System and network configuration diagram

## Nonconformity examples

- A case where development and test systems, outsourcing company staff, PCs, test terminals, etc. related to the development of information systems and personal information processing systems are omitted from the scope of the management system
- A case where core organizations (personnel), such as executives and employees or business departments that perform the role of key decision-makers for a service or business set as the scope of the information security and personal information protection management system, are not included in the certification scope
- A case where development and test systems, developer PCs, test terminals, development organizations, etc. related to the development of information systems and personal information processing systems are omitted from the scope of the management system
