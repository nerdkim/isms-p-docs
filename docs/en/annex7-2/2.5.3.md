# 2.5.3 Remote Access Control  _( Annex 7-2 )_

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Domain | 2. Protection Measure Requirements |

| Criteria type | Annex 7-2 (special case of certification, relaxed certification criteria) |

## Certification criterion

Management of information systems and processing of personal information from locations outside controlled areas shall in principle be prohibited, and where remote access is allowed for unavoidable reasons such as telecommuting/incident response/remote collaboration, protection measures shall be established and implemented.

## Key checkpoints

1. Is remote operation of information systems over external networks such as the internet prohibited in principle, and are compensating measures established where it is unavoidably allowed for reasons such as incident response?
2. Where information systems are operated remotely over the internal network, is access allowed only for specific terminals?
3. When performing remote work such as telecommuting, remote collaboration, and smart work, are protection measures established and implemented to prevent leakage of important information, hacking, and other security incidents?
4. Are terminals that remotely connect to the personal information processing system for purposes such as management, operation, development, and security designated as management terminals, and are safeguards such as prohibition of arbitrary manipulation and use for other purposes applied?

## Detailed explanation

> Note: This item is a relaxed certification criterion (Annex 7-2) under the special case of information security management system certification. The certification criterion/key checkpoints above are the relaxed/adjusted official text, while the detailed explanation/related laws/evidence/nonconformity examples below are the content of the "ISMS-P Certification Criteria Guide" for Annex 7 [2.6.6 Remote Access Control], the same control domain (under the relaxed criteria, they apply within the scope of the above criterion/checkpoints).

Remote operation of major assets (servers, network equipment, security equipment, etc.) associated with important information (personal information) processing, information systems, and personal information processing systems over external networks such as the internet shall in principle be prohibited, and where unavoidably allowed, the following measures shall be established and implemented.

- Approval by the person in charge for remote operation and access
- Application of secure authentication means (certificates, OTP, etc.)
- Application of secure connection means (VPN, etc.)
- Granting of temporary access rights and management of the status of authorized persons
- Terminal security for connecting terminals, such as antivirus installation and security patching
- Monitoring of remote operation status (periodic review of the status of VPN account issuance/use, etc.)
- Logging of remote access records and periodic analysis
- Security awareness training related to remote operation, etc.

Where information systems are operated remotely over the internal network, access shall be allowed only for specific terminals.

- Restricting the terminals that can connect by IP address, MAC address, etc.
- Blocking access paths that bypass the normal remote access path, etc.

When performing remote work such as telecommuting, remote collaboration, and smart work, protection measures shall be established and implemented to prevent leakage of important information, hacking, and other security incidents.

- Definition of smart work forms: telecommuting, smart work centers, remote collaboration, mobile office environments
- Setting the scope of work permission by smart work form: scope of allowed remote access to internal systems and services
- Smart work approval procedure: application, approval, revocation, etc. of remote access rights for smart work
- Technical protection measures required for remote access: encryption of the transmission section (VPN, etc.), strengthened user authentication (OTP, etc.)
- Security for connecting terminals (PCs, mobile devices, etc.): antivirus installation, application of security patches, terminal authentication, measures in case of loss/theft (reporting procedures, terminal lock, deletion of important information, etc.), prohibition of storing important information (encryption measures if necessary), etc.
- Establishment of an information protection guideline for the smart work environment and training, etc.

For terminals (management terminals or important terminals) that remotely connect to the personal information processing system for purposes such as management, operation, development, and security of the personal information processing system, the following protection measures shall be applied.

- Designation of management terminals and list management
- Measures to prevent unauthorized persons from accessing and arbitrarily manipulating management terminals
- Measures to prevent access from anything other than registered management terminals
- Measures to prevent use for purposes other than the original purpose
- Application of protection measures to prevent infection of management terminals by malicious programs, etc.

## Related laws

- Personal Information Protection Act Article 29 (Duty of Safety Measures)
- Standards for Measures to Ensure the Safety of Personal Information Article 6 (Access Control)

## Evidence (examples)

- Application forms for external connection such as VPN
- VPN account list
- Status of VPN access control policy settings
- IP management ledger
- Remote access control settings (server settings, security system settings, etc.)
- Status of management terminal designation and management
- Network configuration diagram

## Nonconformity examples

- The internal regulations state that remote access to systems is prohibited in principle and, where unavoidable, only approved users may access through IP-based access control, but remote desktop connections and SSH access to systems are not restricted by IP address, etc., so remote access is possible from all PCs.
- A VPN is built and operated for remote operation management, but it is allowed at all times without use approval or connection period restrictions for the VPN.
- A business mobile app is installed and operated on personal smart devices for external workers, but appropriate protection measures (antivirus, reset, encryption, etc.) to prevent leakage of personal information due to malicious code, loss/theft, etc. are not applied.
- The external-connection VPN does not restrict, per user, the network segments and information systems that can be remotely accessed, so a user authenticated for remote access can excessively access the entire internal network and information systems.
