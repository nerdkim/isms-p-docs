## Certification criterion

The organization must establish and implement methods and procedures for exercising rights so that data subjects can make requests such as access, correction/deletion, suspension of processing, objection, and withdrawal of consent regarding their personal information more easily than the collection methods/procedures, and, upon receiving a request from a data subject, must process it without delay and keep the related records. In addition, the organization must establish and implement criteria such as deletion requests and interim measures so that information infringing on the rights of others, such as invasion of privacy or defamation, is not circulated.

## Key checkpoints

1. Are methods and procedures for exercising rights prepared and disclosed so that a data subject or their agent can request access, correction/deletion, suspension of processing, withdrawal of consent, etc. (hereinafter "requests for access, etc.") regarding personal information in a manner no more difficult than the personal information collection method/procedure?
2. When a data subject or their agent makes a request for access, etc., are the necessary measures taken in response within the prescribed period?
3. When a data subject or their agent withdraws consent to the collection/use/provision, etc. of personal information, are the necessary measures taken, such as destroying the collected personal information without delay?
4. Are the necessary procedures prepared and provided so that objections can be raised when there is a dissatisfaction with the measures taken in response to a data subject's request for access, etc.?
5. Are records kept of the data subject's requests for access, etc. and the results of processing?
6. When the rights of others, such as invasion of privacy or defamation, have been infringed on an information and communications network, has a procedure been prepared and implemented so that the injured party can request the information and communications service provider to delete the information, etc.?

## Detailed explanation

> ※ This item is a **relaxed certification criterion (Annex 7-2)** under the special provisions for information security management system certification. The certification criterion/key checkpoints above are the relaxed/adjusted official text, and the detailed explanation/related laws/evidence/nonconformity examples below are the content of the "ISMS-P Certification Criteria Guide" for Annex 7 [3.5.2 Guarantee of Data Subject Rights], which is the same control domain (under the relaxed criteria, they apply within the scope of the certification criterion/checkpoints above).

Methods and procedures for exercising rights must be prepared and disclosed so that a data subject or their agent can request access, correction/deletion, suspension of processing, withdrawal of consent, etc. (hereinafter "requests for access, etc.") regarding personal information in a manner no more difficult than the personal information collection method/procedure.

- Specific methods and procedures by which data subjects can make requests for access, etc. must be prepared and disclosed so that data subjects can easily be aware of them
- The methods and procedures for the data subject to exercise rights must be at least as easy and convenient as the personal information collection procedure or the membership registration procedure, and supporting documents that were not required at the time of personal information collection must not be additionally required
- It is necessary to prepare and provide as diverse methods of exercising rights as possible so that data subjects can conveniently choose (visit, in writing, telephone, e-mail, internet website, etc.)

**Matters to be observed when preparing access request methods/procedures (Article 41(2) of the Enforcement Decree of the Personal Information Protection Act)**

1. Provide by methods that data subjects can easily use, such as in writing, telephone, e-mail, and internet
2. Except where there are justifiable grounds, such as where continuous operation of the channel through which personal information was collected is difficult, allow personal information access requests to be made at least through the same channel or method by which the personal information was collected
3. A personal information controller operating an internet website must disclose the access request method and procedure on the website

※ When preparing the method and procedure, they must be no more difficult than the collection method and procedure for the relevant personal information

- It must be confirmed whether the person who made the request for access, etc. is the data subject themselves or a legitimate agent, and the confirmation method must be one objectively recognized as a reasonable means (electronic signature, i-PIN, ID verification, etc.)

**Scope of agents (Article 45 of the Enforcement Decree of the Personal Information Protection Act)**

1. The legal representative of the data subject
2. A person delegated by the data subject (a voluntary agent granted the power of agency by the data subject on the basis of a delegation contract, etc.)

- Where a personal information controller is a public institution, if identity verification is possible through the joint use of administrative information under the "Electronic Government Act," verification must be made through the joint use of administrative information
- A fee and postage may be charged to the person who made the request for access, etc. within the scope of actual costs necessary for performing the relevant work, but where the reason for the request for access, etc. lies with the relevant personal information controller, the fee and postage may not be charged

When a request to access personal information is received from a data subject or their agent, the necessary measures must be taken within 10 days so that the data subject can access the relevant personal information.

- A data subject may request access to the following matters regarding their own personal information processed by the personal information controller

**Matters that may be requested for access (Article 41(1) of the Enforcement Decree of the Personal Information Protection Act)**

1. The items and content of the personal information
2. The purpose of collection/use of the personal information
3. The retention and use period of the personal information
4. The status of provision of the personal information to third parties
5. The fact of and content of consent to the processing of personal information

- Where there are justifiable grounds for being unable to grant access within 10 days, the data subject may be notified of the grounds and access may be postponed, and where the grounds cease to exist after the personal information access has been postponed, access must be granted within 10 days from the date the grounds for postponement ceased to exist
- Where there are grounds for restricting or refusing personal information access, the data subject may be notified of the grounds and access may be restricted or refused

**Grounds on which a data subject's access request may be restricted/refused (Article 35(4) of the Personal Information Protection Act)**

1. Where access is prohibited or restricted by law
2. Where there is a risk of harm to another person's life/body, or a risk of unjustly infringing on another person's property and other interests
3. Where a public institution would suffer a serious hindrance in performing any of the following duties:
   - a. Duties concerning the imposition/collection or refund of taxes
   - b. Duties concerning grade evaluation or the selection of admitted students at schools of each level under the "Elementary and Secondary Education Act" and the "Higher Education Act," lifelong education facilities under the "Lifelong Education Act," and other higher education institutions established under other laws
   - c. Duties concerning tests on academic achievement/skills and employment, and qualification examinations
   - d. Duties concerning evaluations or judgments in progress regarding the calculation of compensation/benefits, etc.
   - e. Duties concerning audits and investigations in progress under other laws

- Where some of the matters requested for access have grounds for restriction and refusal of access, access to that portion may be restricted, and access must be allowed for the portion excluding the matters for which access is restricted

Where the organization falls under those subject to legal obligations, procedures and measures must be established/implemented to respond to requests from data subjects or agents for the transmission of personal information.

- Persons under a legal obligation to comply with a data subject's request for transmission of personal information (information transmitters)

| Field | Person under legal obligation | Remarks |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Request to transmit to the data subject themselves | A personal information controller meeting the criteria prescribed by Presidential Decree in consideration of personal information processing capacity | Article 35-2(1) of the Personal Information Protection Act |
| Request to transmit to another personal information controller (provided, within a reasonable scope technically permitted) | A personal information controller meeting the criteria prescribed by Presidential Decree in consideration of revenue, the scale of personal information held, personal information processing capacity, and industry-specific characteristics | Article 35-2(2) of the Personal Information Protection Act |

- Information for which transmission may be requested (personal information that satisfies all of the following requirements)

| No | Requirements for information for which transmission may be requested |
| --- | --- |
| 1 | The information for which the data subject requests transmission is personal information about the data subject themselves and falls under any of the following:<br>a. Personal information processed with consent obtained under Article 15(1)1, Article 23(1)1, or Article 24(1)1<br>b. Personal information processed to perform a contract concluded under Article 15(1)4 or to carry out measures requested by the data subject in the process of concluding a contract<br>c. Among personal information processed under Article 15(1)2, the same paragraph 3, Article 23(1)2, or Article 24(1)2, personal information designated as a subject of transmission requests by resolution of the Protection Commission upon deliberation, at the request of the head of the relevant central administrative agency, for the benefit of the data subject or for public-interest purposes |
| 2 | The personal information for which transmission is requested is not information separately generated by analyzing/processing personal information collected by the personal information controller |
| 3 | The personal information for which transmission is requested is personal information processed by an information processing device such as a computer |

- Requirements for a personal information controller who may receive personal information transmitted pursuant to a transmission request (information recipient)

| No | Requirements for the information recipient |
| --- | --- |
| 1 | A specialized personal information management institution under Article 35-3(1) of the Personal Information Protection Act |
| 2 | A person who fulfills the safety measure obligation under Article 29 of the Personal Information Protection Act and meets the facility and technical criteria prescribed by Presidential Decree |

※ The amended provisions of Article 35-2 of the Personal Information Protection Act concerning the right to request transmission of personal information take effect on a date prescribed by Presidential Decree within a range from one year after promulgation (2023.3.14) up to no more than two years after promulgation

When a request for the correction/deletion of personal information is received from a data subject or their agent, and if the data subject's request is deemed justifiable, the relevant personal information must be investigated within 10 days, measures such as correction/deletion of the relevant personal information must be taken in accordance with the data subject's request, and the result must then be notified to the data subject.

- Reply on the results of measures within 10 days from the date the personal information correction/deletion request is received
- For correction requests and withdrawal of consent regarding personal information outsourced externally or provided to a third party, contact the trustee or third party to request that measures be taken
- Where the personal information is specified as subject to collection in another law, the deletion request may be refused, and in this case, the fact that the request will not be complied with, the content of the underlying law and the reason therefor, and the method of raising an objection must be notified to the relevant data subject by a personal information correction/deletion notice within 10 days from the date the correction/deletion request is received (records of contract/subscription withdrawals under the Act on Electronic Commerce, etc.)

When a request for the suspension of processing of personal information is received from a data subject or their agent, all or part of the processing must be suspended without delay, unless there are special grounds, and the result must be notified to the data subject.

- Reply on the results of measures within 10 days from the date the personal information processing suspension request is received
- Where there are grounds on which the suspension of processing of personal information may be refused, the relevant facts must be notified to the person requesting suspension of processing within 10 days from the date the request is received

**Grounds for refusing a suspension of processing request (Article 37(2) of the Personal Information Protection Act)**

1. Where there is a special provision in a law or it is unavoidable to comply with a legal obligation
2. Where there is a risk of harm to another person's life/body, or a risk of unjustly infringing on another person's property and other interests
3. Where a public institution would be unable to perform the duties within its jurisdiction prescribed by other laws unless it processes the personal information
4. Where performance of the contract would be difficult, such as being unable to provide the service agreed upon with the data subject unless the personal information is processed, and the data subject has not clearly expressed the intent to terminate the contract

When a data subject or their agent withdraws consent to the collection/use/provision, etc. of personal information, the necessary measures such as destroying the collected personal information without delay must be taken.

※ Measures when a data subject withdraws consent (examples)
- Destruction without delay of personal information related to the relevant data subject
- Where a preservation obligation is imposed under another law, separate storage for the period prescribed by that law
- In the case of a withdrawal of consent to third-party provision, measures to no longer provide the personal information to the third party
- Measures to ensure that text messages, e-mails, etc. for promotion, marketing, etc. are no longer sent

When a data subject or agent refuses a decision made by processing personal information through a fully automated system (including a system to which artificial intelligence technology is applied) (hereinafter "automated decision") or requests an explanation, etc., the necessary measures must be taken.

- A data subject has the right to refuse an automated decision or to request an explanation, etc.

**Data subject's rights regarding automated decisions (Article 37-2 of the Personal Information Protection Act)**

① Where a decision made by processing personal information through a fully automated system (including a system to which artificial intelligence technology is applied) (excluding automatic dispositions by an administrative agency under Article 20 of the "General Act on Public Administration"; hereinafter in this Article referred to as an "automated decision") has a significant effect on their rights or obligations, a data subject has the right to refuse the relevant decision against the relevant personal information controller. However, this shall not apply where the automated decision is made under Article 15(1)1/2 and 4.

② Where a personal information controller has made an automated decision, a data subject may request an explanation, etc. regarding that decision.

- Where a data subject refuses an automated decision or requests an explanation, etc. thereof, establish/implement the relevant procedures so that, unless there are justifiable grounds, the necessary measures can be carried out, such as not applying the automated decision or re-processing/explaining through human intervention
- When making an automated decision, disclose the criteria and procedures for the automated decision, the manner in which personal information is processed, etc. so that data subjects can easily verify them

※ The amended provisions of Article 37-2 of the Personal Information Protection Act concerning refusal of automated decisions and requests for explanation, etc. take effect on the date one year after promulgation (effective 2024.3.15)

The necessary procedures must be prepared and provided so that objections can be raised when there is a dissatisfaction with the measures taken in response to a data subject's request for access, etc.

- In this case, it is necessary to involve external experts or to prepare internal checks so that the objection procedure can be operated fairly

Records must be kept of the results of receiving and processing a data subject's requests for access, etc.

- Periodically review the results of receiving and processing data subjects' requests for access, etc. to confirm whether the guarantee of data subject rights is being properly carried out, and take supplementary measures if necessary

When the rights of others, such as invasion of privacy or defamation, have been infringed on an information and communications network, a procedure must be prepared and implemented so that the injured party can request the information and communications service provider to delete the information, etc., and the necessary measures must be taken so that personal information is not exposed to the public through the information and communications network.

- Where the rights of others, such as invasion of privacy or defamation, have been infringed by information provided for the purpose of disclosure to the general public through an information and communications network, the injured party must be able to substantiate the fact of the infringement to the information and communications service provider that processed the relevant information and request the deletion of the information or the posting of a rebuttal
- Where the rights of others have been infringed, when an information and communications service provider is requested to delete the relevant information, etc., it must take the necessary measures such as deletion/interim measures without delay and immediately notify the applicant and the person who posted the information
- The content/procedures, etc. concerning remedy procedures and other necessary measures for cases where the rights of others have been infringed must be specifically set forth in advance in the terms of service
- A personal information controller must ensure that personal information such as unique identifying information, account information, and credit card information is not exposed to the public through the information and communications network, and where there is a request from the Personal Information Protection Commission or the Korea Internet & Security Agency regarding personal information exposed to the public, must take the necessary measures such as deleting or blocking the relevant information

## Related laws

- Personal Information Protection Act Article 34-2 (Deletion/Blocking of Exposed Personal Information), Article 35 (Access to Personal Information), Article 35-2 (Request for Transmission of Personal Information), Article 36 (Correction/Deletion of Personal Information), Article 37 (Suspension of Processing of Personal Information, etc.), Article 37-2 (Data Subject's Rights Regarding Automated Decisions, etc.), Article 38 (Methods and Procedures for Exercising Rights)
- Network Act Article 44 (Protection of Rights on Information and Communications Networks), Article 44-2 (Request for Deletion of Information, etc.), Article 44-3 (Discretionary Interim Measures)

## Evidence (examples)

- Personal information processing policy
- Procedures and related forms for handling requests for access, etc. to personal information
- Records of measures taken upon requests for access, etc. to personal information
- Membership withdrawal and consent withdrawal procedures

## Nonconformity examples

- Where the methods for requesting access, correction/deletion, and suspension of processing of personal information are not disclosed so that data subjects can be aware of them
- Where a reply to a request for access to personal information is made more than 10 days after the date the access request was received, without notice of justifiable grounds
- Where records of the handling of access complaints regarding personal information are not kept and preserved
- Where access notification is made without a procedure to confirm whether the person is the data subject themselves or a legitimate agent
- Where a reply to a request for correction/deletion of personal information is made more than 10 days after the date the correction/deletion request was received
- Where membership registration was easily possible online at the time of joining, but membership withdrawal requires submission of additional documents such as an ID card or is possible only through an offline visit
