## Certification criterion

To protect key facilities and data from internal and external threats, physical protection areas such as controlled areas / restricted areas / reception areas shall be designated, protection measures for each area shall be established, and their implementation shall be reviewed periodically.

## Key checkpoints

1. To protect personal information and important information, documents, storage media, key facilities and systems, etc. from physical and environmental threats, have criteria for designating physical protection areas such as controlled areas, restricted areas, and reception areas been established?
2. Are protection areas designated according to the physical protection area designation criteria, and are protection measures for each area established and implemented?
3. Are protection areas controlled so that only persons authorized for entry may enter, in accordance with entry procedures?
4. Are entry records of internal/external persons for each protection area retained for a certain period, and are entry records and entry authorizations reviewed periodically?

## Detailed explanation

> ※ This item is a **relaxed certification criterion (Annex 7-2)** under the special case of information security management system certification. The certification criterion / key checkpoints above are the relaxed/adjusted official original text, while the detailed explanation / related laws / evidence / nonconformity examples below are the content of the "ISMS-P Certification Criteria Guide" for Annex 7 [2.4.1 Designation of Protection Areas], which is the same control domain (under the relaxed criterion, they apply within the scope of the certification criterion / checkpoints above).

To protect personal information and important information, documents, storage media, key facilities and systems, etc. from physical/environmental threats, criteria for designating physical protection areas such as controlled areas, restricted areas, and reception areas shall be established.

- Designate physical protection areas as reception areas, restricted areas, controlled areas, etc.
- Select the terminology and classification of protection areas to suit the organization's environment

  ※ Physical protection areas (examples)
  - Reception area : An area where outsiders can enter without a separate entry pass (e.g. : reception location, etc.)
  - Restricted area : A place equipped with separate access control devices and monitoring systems to prevent unauthorized access, where an entry pass such as an employee card is required to enter (e.g. : departmental offices, etc.)
  - Controlled area : A place that includes all control items of a restricted area, keeps entry eligibility to a minimum number of personnel, and requires additional procedures for entry (e.g. : computer room, communication equipment room, control room, air conditioning room, power generation room, power supply room, etc.)

Protection areas shall be designated according to the physical protection area designation criteria, and protection measures for each area shall be established and implemented.

- Apply protection measures for each area, such as the access control method (ID card, biometrics, etc.), persons permitted to enter, entry procedures, and video surveillance
- Since controlled areas restrict authorized entrants to a minimum even within the organization, mark them as controlled areas when necessary to fundamentally block access attempts themselves, and periodically review whether there have been illegal access attempts

## Related laws

- Personal Information Protection Act Article 29 (Duty of Safety Measures)
- Standards for Measures to Ensure the Safety of Personal Information Article 10 (Physical Safety Measures)

## Evidence (examples)

- Physical security guidelines (criteria for designating protection areas)
- Status of protection area designation
- Protection area markings
- Status of protection measures for each protection area

## Nonconformity examples

- A case where the internal physical security guidelines specify that personal information storage facilities and system areas are designated as controlled areas, but some target areas such as the document storage room where membership application documents are kept are omitted from the controlled areas
- A case where the internal physical security guidelines specify that controlled-area signs of a designated format shall be installed for controlled areas, but signs were not installed in some controlled areas
