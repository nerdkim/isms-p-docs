## Certification criterion

For collected personal information, a management procedure must be provided to the data subject so that the accuracy/completeness/currency of the personal information is ensured within the scope necessary for the processing purpose.

## Key checkpoints

1. Are procedures and measures to keep personal information accurate and up to date established/implemented?
2. Is a method provided by which the data subject can maintain the accuracy, completeness, and currency of their own personal information?

## Detailed explanation

> * This item is a **relaxed certification criterion (Annex 7-2)** under the special provisions for information security management system certification. The certification criterion/key checkpoints above are the official text as relaxed/adjusted, while the detailed explanation/related laws/evidence/nonconformity examples below are the content of the "ISMS-P Certification Criteria Guide" for Annex 7 [3.2.2 Ensuring personal information quality], which is the same control area (under the relaxed criteria, they apply within the scope of the certification criterion/checkpoints above).

Procedures and measures to keep personal information accurate and up to date must be established/implemented.

- Apply safeguards to prevent the forgery/alteration/damage of personal information
- Build/implement a system such as backup/recovery so that the accuracy/completeness of personal information can be secured even if unlawful alteration or damage of personal information occurs due to external hacking, misuse/abuse of privileges by insiders, disasters/calamities, etc.
- Apply administrative/technical measures so that misentries, etc. do not occur when a personal information handler changes personal information
- Where a data subject changes their name or resident registration number due to a change of name (改名), a leak of the resident registration number, etc., establish/implement a procedure to reflect this and keep the data subject's personal information up to date

A method by which the data subject can maintain the accuracy, completeness, and currency of their own personal information must be provided.

- Post notices so that the correction of personal information through the website can be carried out periodically
- Provide various methods (online, offline, etc.) so that the data subject can easily check and change the registration status of their own personal information
- Prepare and implement a secure identity verification procedure when personal information is changed
- Post the changes to the privacy policy and history-related content so that the data subject can easily recognize the status of the personal information being collected and processed

## Related laws

- Personal Information Protection Act Article 3 (Principles for Protecting Personal Information)

## Evidence (examples)

- Data subject personal information correction/change forms (online, offline)
- Procedure for maintaining the currency of personal information

## Nonconformity examples

- Where, when changing member information through an internet website, an identity verification procedure is carried out, but when changing member information through a call with a customer center agent, the identity verification procedure is inadequate, making unlawful changes to member information possible
- Where a method to change personal information is provided for online members, but no method to change personal information is provided for offline members
