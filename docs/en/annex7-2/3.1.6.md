## Certification criterion

When installing/operating fixed video information processing devices in a publicly accessible place, or operating mobile video information processing devices in a publicly accessible place for work purposes, the organization must comply with legal requirements according to the purpose and location of installation and establish/implement appropriate protective measures.

## Key checkpoints

1. When installing/operating fixed video information processing devices in a publicly accessible place, is it being reviewed whether the case falls under the legally permitted requirements?
2. When a public institution, etc., intends to install/operate fixed video information processing devices in a publicly accessible place, is it collecting the opinions of relevant experts and interested parties by going through procedures prescribed by law, such as holding public hearings/briefing sessions?
3. When installing/operating fixed video information processing devices, are necessary measures such as installing information signs being taken so that data subjects can easily recognize them?
4. When operating mobile video information processing devices in a publicly accessible place for work purposes, is it being reviewed whether the case falls under the legally permitted requirements?
5. When capturing images of persons or objects related to those persons with mobile video information processing devices in a publicly accessible place for work purposes, is the fact of filming being indicated and notified by methods such as light, sound, or information signs?
6. Has an operating/management policy for video information processing devices been established and implemented for the safe management of video information processing devices and video information?
7. Is a retention period for video information set, and is it destroyed without delay upon expiration of the retention period?
8. When entrusting the affairs concerning installation/operation of video information processing devices, are they reflected in the contract in accordance with the relevant procedures and requirements?

## Detailed explanation

> * Note: This item is a **relaxed certification criterion (Annex 7-2)** under the special case of Information Security Management System certification. The certification criterion/key checkpoints above are the official relaxed/adjusted text, while the detailed explanation/related laws/evidence/nonconformity examples below are the content of the "ISMS-P Certification Criteria Guide" for the same control area, Annex 7 [3.1.6 Installation/Operation of Video Information Processing Devices] (under the relaxed criterion, they apply within the scope of the certification criterion/checkpoints above).

- When installing/operating fixed video information processing devices in a publicly accessible place, it must be reviewed whether the case falls under the legally permitted requirements.
  - **Definition of fixed video information processing devices** : Devices installed in a fixed space that continuously or periodically capture images, etc., of persons or objects, or that transmit them through a wired/wireless network, referring to closed-circuit television and network cameras (Personal Information Protection Act Article 2 Subparagraph 7 and Article 3 Paragraph 1 of the Enforcement Decree of the same Act)
    - Scope of fixed video information processing devices (Article 3 Paragraph 1 of the Enforcement Decree of the Personal Information Protection Act)
      1. Closed-circuit television: a device falling under any of the following items
         - a. A device that continuously or periodically captures images, etc., through a camera installed in a fixed space, or transmits the captured video information to a specific place through a transmission line such as a wired/wireless closed circuit
         - b. A device that enables the recording/logging of video information captured or transmitted pursuant to item a
      2. Network camera: a device that enables the person who installs/manages the device to process, such as collect/store, from any location via wired/wireless internet, the video information continuously or periodically captured through a device installed in a fixed space
  - Cases where fixed video information processing devices may be installed/operated in a publicly accessible place
    1. Where specifically permitted by statute
    2. Where necessary for the prevention and investigation of crime
    3. Where installed/operated by a person with legitimate authority for the safety and management of facilities and the prevention of fire
    4. Where installed/operated by a person with legitimate authority for traffic enforcement
    5. Where installed/operated by a person with legitimate authority for the collection/analysis and provision of traffic information
    6. Where the captured video information is not stored, and the case falls under any of the following and the captured images are not separately stored
       - Where necessary to produce statistical values such as the number of visitors
       - Where necessary to derive statistical characteristic values such as gender and age group
       - In other cases equivalent to the above two that have gone through deliberation/resolution by the Personal Information Protection Commission
  - It is prohibited to install/operate video information processing devices so as to view the interior of places where there is a significant risk of infringing on personal privacy, such as public bathhouses, restrooms, saunas (發汗室), and changing rooms used by an unspecified number of people. However, this is exceptionally permitted for facilities prescribed by Presidential Decree that confine or protect persons based on statute, such as correctional facilities and mental health facilities (correctional facilities, psychiatric medical institutions equipped with accommodation facilities, mental care facilities, and mental rehabilitation facilities)

- When a public institution, etc., intends to install/operate fixed video information processing devices in a publicly accessible place, it must collect the opinions of relevant experts and interested parties by going through legal procedures such as public hearings/briefing sessions.
  - Those required to go through the opinion collection procedure
    1. The head of a public institution that intends to install/operate fixed video information processing devices in a publicly accessible place
    2. Correctional facilities, psychiatric medical institutions, mental care facilities, and mental rehabilitation facilities that intend to install/operate fixed video information processing devices so as to view the interior of places where there is a significant risk of infringing on personal privacy
  - Opinion collection procedure
    1. Conducting administrative pre-announcement or hearing opinions pursuant to the "Administrative Procedures Act"
    2. Briefing sessions/surveys or opinion polls targeting local residents, etc., who are directly affected by the installation of the relevant video information processing device
  - Persons subject to opinion collection
    1. Relevant experts
    2. Interested parties such as persons employed at the relevant facility, persons confined in or protected by the relevant facility, or their guardians

- When installing/operating fixed video information processing devices, an information sign must be installed so that data subjects can easily recognize it.
  - Matters to be included on the information sign
    1. Purpose and location of installation
    2. Filming range and time
    3. Contact information of the manager
    4. Name and contact information of the entrusted party (when entrusting the affairs of installation/operation of video information processing devices)
  - Exceptions to information sign installation
    1. Military facilities
    2. National key facilities
    3. National security facilities
  - Considerations when installing information signs
    - Install at a location where data subjects can easily recognize it
    - When installing multiple fixed video information processing devices inside a building, an information sign indicating that the entire facility or place is an area where fixed video information processing devices are installed may be installed at a conspicuous location such as the entrance
    - Where a public institution installs fixed video information processing devices for purposes such as long-distance filming, enforcement of speeding/signal violations, or traffic flow surveys and the risk of infringing personal information is low, or where fixed video information processing devices are installed for forest fire monitoring, and where it is impossible to install an information sign due to the characteristics of the location or, even if installed, data subjects cannot easily recognize it, the relevant matters may be posted on the internet website

- When operating mobile video information processing devices in a publicly accessible place for work purposes, it must be reviewed whether the case falls under the legally permitted requirements and the corresponding measures must be implemented.
  - **Definition of mobile video information processing devices** : Devices prescribed by Presidential Decree that capture images, etc., of persons or objects, or transmit them through a wired/wireless network, by being worn on or carried on the body or attached to or mounted (据置) on a movable object (Personal Information Protection Act Article 2 Subparagraph 7-2 and Article 3 Paragraph 2 of the Enforcement Decree of the same Act)
    - Scope of mobile video information processing devices (Article 3 Paragraph 2 of the Enforcement Decree of the Personal Information Protection Act)
      1. Wearable device: a device such as glasses or a watch worn on a person's body or clothing that captures images, etc., or collects/stores or transmits the captured video information
      2. Portable device: a device such as a mobile communications terminal or digital camera that a person carries while capturing images, etc., or collects/stores or transmits the captured video information
      3. Attached/mounted device: a device attached to or mounted (据置) on a movable object such as a vehicle or drone that captures images, etc., or collects/stores or transmits the captured video information
  - Cases where mobile video information processing devices may be operated in a publicly accessible place for work purposes
    1. Where the case falls under any of the subparagraphs of Article 15 Paragraph 1 of the Personal Information Protection Act (such as the data subject's consent)
    2. Where the data subject did not express an intention to refuse filming despite the fact of filming being clearly indicated so that the data subject could be aware of it. In this case, limited to cases where there is no risk of unfairly infringing on the rights of the data subject and the reasonable scope is not exceeded
    3. In other cases equivalent to Subparagraphs 1 and 2 that are prescribed by Presidential Decree
  - No one may capture images of persons or objects related to those persons with mobile video information processing devices in places where the interior of places with a significant risk of infringing on personal privacy, such as public bathhouses, restrooms, saunas, and changing rooms used by an unspecified number of people, can be viewed. However, filming is exceptionally possible where video filming is necessary for the rescue/emergency of human lives in situations such as crime, disaster, fire, or equivalent situations

- When capturing images of persons or objects related to those persons with mobile video information processing devices in a publicly accessible place for work purposes, the fact of filming must be indicated and notified by methods such as light, sound, or information signs so that the data subject can easily be aware of the fact of filming.
  - Methods of indicating the fact of filming : light, sound, information sign, written notice, public announcement, or other equivalent means
  - Exception to indicating the fact of filming : Where it is difficult to indicate and notify the data subject of the fact of filming easily due to the characteristics of the filming method, such as aerial filming by drone, the matters such as the fact and purpose of filming and the date/time and place of filming are announced through the website established/operated by the Personal Information Protection Commission to support the indication of the fact of filming by mobile video information processing devices (Standard Personal Information Protection Guidelines Article 39-2 Paragraph 2)

- The video information processing device operator must establish and implement an operating/management policy for video information processing devices for the safe management of video information processing devices and video information.
  - A fixed video information processing device operator establishes an operating/management policy for fixed video information processing devices, and a mobile video information processing device operator establishes an operating/management policy for mobile video information processing devices
  - Matters to be included in the operating/management policy for video information processing devices
    1. The basis for installation and the purpose of installation of the video information processing devices
    2. The number of installed video information processing devices, installation locations, and filming range
    3. The manager, the responsible department, and the persons who have access rights to the video information
    4. The filming time, retention period, storage location, and processing method of the video information
    5. The method and location by which the video information processing device operator verifies the video information
    6. Measures in response to data subjects' requests such as access to video information
    7. Technical/administrative and physical measures for the protection of video information
    8. Other matters necessary for the installation/operation and management of the video information processing devices
  - Where matters concerning the operation/management of video information processing devices are included when establishing the personal information processing policy, an operating/management policy for video information processing devices may not be separately established
  - Include in the regulations that fixed video information processing devices are not arbitrarily manipulated for a purpose different from the purpose for which they were installed or aimed at another place, and carry out management/supervision
  - Take measures so that the recording function of fixed video information processing devices cannot be used

- A retention period for video information must be set, and it must be destroyed without delay upon expiration of the retention period.
  - Determine the retention period as the minimum period for achieving the purpose of retaining the video information
  - However, where there are special provisions in other statutes regarding the retention period of video information, retain it in accordance with the relevant provisions
  - Where it is difficult for the video information processing device operator, according to its circumstances, to calculate the minimum period for achieving the purpose of retention, the retention period shall be within 30 days after the collection of personal video information (Standard Personal Information Protection Guidelines Article 41 Paragraph 2)

- When entrusting the affairs concerning installation/operation of video information processing devices, the necessary content must be reflected in the contract in accordance with the relevant procedures and requirements.
  - Content to be included in the contract for entrusting the affairs of installation/operation of video information processing devices by a public institution (Article 26 Paragraph 1 of the Enforcement Decree of the Personal Information Protection Act)
    1. The purpose and scope of the entrusted affairs
    2. Matters concerning restrictions on re-entrustment
    3. Matters concerning measures to ensure safety, such as restrictions on access to video information
    4. Matters concerning inspection of the management status of video information
    5. Matters concerning liability, such as compensation for damages, in the event the entrusted party violates the obligations it must comply with

## Related laws

- Personal Information Protection Act Article 25 (Restrictions on Installation/Operation of Fixed Video Information Processing Devices), Article 25-2 (Restrictions on Operation of Mobile Video Information Processing Devices)

## Evidence (examples)

- Operation status of video information processing devices
- Information signs for video information processing devices
- Operating/management policy for video information processing devices
- Management screen of video information processing devices (account/authority details, video information retention period, etc.)
- Contract with the entrusted operator of video information processing devices and inspection history

## Nonconformity examples

- Cases where some of the notice text on the information sign for video information processing devices is missing during operation, or an operating/management policy for video information processing devices has not been established/operated
- Cases where an operating/management policy for video information processing devices has been established and operated, but management is inadequate, such as the retention period not being complied with contrary to the policy content during operation, or matters described in the policy such as access control and logging for the protection of video information not being complied with
- Cases where the affairs of installation/operation of video information processing devices are entrusted to an external company, but the content required by statute, such as matters concerning inspection of the management status of video information and matters concerning liability for compensation for damages, is not specified in the video information processing device work entrustment contract
- Cases where the affairs of installation/operation of video information processing devices are entrusted to an external company, but the name and contact information of the entrusted party are omitted from the notice on the information sign for video information processing devices
