# 2.11.2 Vulnerability Assessment and Remediation

| Field | Value |

| Domain | 2. Protection Measure Requirements |

## Certification criterion

To verify whether the vulnerabilities of information systems are exposed, vulnerability assessments shall be performed regularly and any vulnerabilities discovered shall be remediated promptly. In addition, the occurrence of the latest security vulnerabilities shall be continuously monitored, and their impact on information systems shall be analyzed and addressed.

## Key checkpoints

1. Has a procedure for information system vulnerability assessment been established, and are assessments performed regularly?
2. Are discovered vulnerabilities remediated, and are the results reported to the responsible officer?
3. Is the occurrence of the latest security vulnerabilities continuously monitored, and is their impact on information systems analyzed and addressed?
4. Is the vulnerability assessment history recorded and managed so that protective measures are prepared for problems such as the recurrence of vulnerabilities identified in the previous year?

## Detailed explanation

### A procedure for information system vulnerability assessment shall be established, and assessments shall be performed regularly.

- Matters to be included in the vulnerability assessment procedure
  - Targets of vulnerability assessment (e.g., servers, network devices, etc.)
  - Vulnerability assessment cycle (considering legal requirements, criticality, etc.)
  - Designation of persons in charge of and responsible for vulnerability assessment
  - Vulnerability assessment procedure and methods, etc.
  - Remediation criteria according to criticality
  - Procedure for reporting vulnerability assessment results
  - Security review of unremediated vulnerabilities, etc.
  - Other matters necessary for the prevention of and recovery from security incidents, etc.
- Targets of vulnerability assessment
  - Vulnerabilities in the configuration and settings of network systems such as routers and switches
  - Vulnerabilities in server OS security settings
  - Vulnerabilities in security systems such as firewalls
  - Application vulnerabilities
  - Web service vulnerabilities
  - Vulnerabilities in smart devices and mobile services (mobile apps, etc.), etc.
- Consider performing penetration testing depending on the size of the company and the criticality of the information held during vulnerability assessment
- Personal information controllers must include matters concerning vulnerability assessment for preventing the leakage or theft of personal information in their internal management plan and establish/implement it (Standards for Measures to Ensure the Safety of Personal Information, Article 4, Paragraph 1, Item 10)

### Discovered vulnerabilities shall be remediated, and the results shall be reported to the responsible officer.

- During vulnerability assessment, prepare a report that includes the date and time of the assessment, the assessment target, the assessment method, the assessment content and results, findings, and remediation actions, etc., so that history management is possible
- After completing the response actions for each vulnerability, confirm completion through implementation checks, etc.
- For vulnerabilities that unavoidably cannot be remediated, clearly identify the reason and report the associated risk and supplementary measures to the responsible officer

### The occurrence of the latest security vulnerabilities shall be continuously monitored, and their impact on information systems shall be analyzed and addressed.

- In addition to regular security vulnerability assessments, continuously monitor the latest security vulnerabilities
- When the latest security vulnerability is discovered, analyze the impact of that security vulnerability on information systems and take response actions as necessary

### The vulnerability assessment history shall be recorded and managed, and protective measures shall be prepared for problems such as the recurrence of vulnerabilities identified in the previous year.

- Record management of the vulnerability assessment history
- During vulnerability assessment, compare and analyze against previous vulnerability assessment results to check whether vulnerabilities have recurred
- If similar vulnerabilities are recurring, analyze the root cause and prepare measures to prevent recurrence

## Related laws

- Personal Information Protection Act, Article 29 (Duty of Safety Measures)
- Standards for Measures to Ensure the Safety of Personal Information, Article 4 (Establishment/Implementation and Inspection of Internal Management Plan), Article 6 (Access Control)

## Evidence (examples)

- Vulnerability assessment plan
- Vulnerability assessment result report (web, mobile app, server, network system, security system, DBMS, etc.)
- Vulnerability assessment history
- Vulnerability remediation plan
- Vulnerability remediation completion report
- Penetration testing plan/result report

## Nonconformity examples

- A case where internal regulations require a technical vulnerability assessment of major systems at least once a year, but some of the major systems are omitted from the vulnerability assessment scope
- A case where supplementary actions for vulnerabilities discovered in a vulnerability assessment were not implemented, or where there is no record of feasibility review and approval for vulnerabilities that cannot be remediated within a short period
