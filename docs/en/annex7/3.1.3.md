# 3.1.3 Restrictions on Processing Resident Registration Numbers

| Field | Value |
|---|---|

## Certification criterion

Resident registration numbers may not be processed, including collection and use, except where there is a legal basis, and even where the processing of a resident registration number is permitted, an alternative means must be provided on internet websites and the like.

## Key checkpoints

1. Are resident registration numbers processed only where there is a clear legal basis?
2. Is the specific legal provision that serves as the basis for collecting the resident registration number identified in concrete terms?
3. Even where a resident registration number is processed pursuant to a legal basis, is a method provided by which a data subject can register as a member without using a resident registration number at the stage of registering as a member through the internet website?

## Detailed explanation

Resident registration numbers may not be processed, including collection, except where there is a legal basis, as follows.

- Cases in which the processing of a resident registration number, including collection, is permitted (collection based on consent is not permitted)

| Article 24-2(1) of the Personal Information Protection Act | Article 23-2(1) of the Network Act |
| --- | --- |
| 1. Where an Act, Presidential Decree, National Assembly Regulations, Supreme Court Regulations, Constitutional Court Regulations, National Election Commission Regulations, or Board of Audit and Inspection Regulations specifically require or permit the processing of resident registration numbers<br>2. Where it is deemed clearly necessary for the urgent life, bodily, or property interests of a data subject or a third party<br>3. Where the processing of resident registration numbers is unavoidable and the Protection Commission so prescribes by public notice | 1. Where designated as an identity verification agency<br>2. Where a telecommunications business operator that resells mobile communications services and the like received from a common telecommunications business operator pursuant to Article 38(1) of the Telecommunications Business Act collects/uses a user's resident registration number in connection with the performance of identity verification duties by a mobile communications business operator designated as an identity verification agency pursuant to Article 23-3 |

Where a resident registration number is processed, the legal provision that serves as the basis for such processing must be specifically identified so that it can be proven.

- The phrase "where an Act, Presidential Decree, National Assembly Regulations, Supreme Court Regulations, Constitutional Court Regulations, National Election Commission Regulations, or Board of Audit and Inspection Regulations specifically require or permit the processing of resident registration numbers" means that a specific provision requiring or permitting the personal information controller to process resident registration numbers exists in at least one of the Acts and the like.
- Because Article 24-2(1)1 of the Personal Information Protection Act limits the scope of the statutes under which resident registration numbers may be processed, resident registration numbers may not be processed on the basis of enforcement rules.
- Pursuant to Article 24-2(1)2 of the Personal Information Protection Act, a resident registration number may exceptionally be processed where it is deemed clearly necessary for the urgent life, bodily, or property interests of a data subject or a third party.
- Unless one of the exceptional grounds specified in the subparagraphs of Article 24-2(1) of the Personal Information Protection Act applies, collecting a resident registration number, providing it to a third party, or storing/retaining it is also prohibited.

Note: Examples of judging the lawfulness of processing resident registration numbers
- Enforcement rules, directives/established rules/public notices of administrative agencies at each level, and ordinances/rules of local governments and the like cannot serve as a basis for collecting resident registration numbers.
- If a statute merely prescribes an obligation to verify identity or verify age and the like, this does not constitute a specific provision of a basis for processing resident registration numbers.
- Collecting/using only the last 7 digits rather than the entire resident registration number is an act that utilizes the assignment scheme of the resident registration number and thus uses the unique characteristics of the resident registration number, namely its uniqueness and identifiability, so this can be regarded as collecting/using the entire resident registration number (the same applies where only part of the last 7 digits is processed).
- Until a job applicant is finally selected and becomes an employee, there is no provision in an Act or Presidential Decree that requires a company to process the applicant's resident registration number, so requiring the resident registration number to be entered on a resume/application form and the like is prohibited. However, after final selection, it is permissible for the company to collect and otherwise process the applicant's resident registration number as prescribed by the relevant Act or Presidential Decree for purposes such as enrollment in the four major insurances including employment insurance and withholding of wages.
- The act of visually checking an identification card bearing a resident registration number for the purpose of identity verification and returning it is not an act of collecting the resident registration number, so it does not violate the principle prohibiting the processing of resident registration numbers.

Even where a resident registration number is processed pursuant to a legal basis, a method (an alternative registration means to the resident registration number) by which a data subject can register as a member without using a resident registration number must be provided at the stage of registering as a member through the internet website.

- Examples of alternative registration means to the resident registration number: i-PIN, mobile phone, credit card, certificate, and the like

Note: Identification values using an alternative registration means (examples)
- CI (Connection Information): a one-way encrypted 88-byte string generated in the case of identity verification through an alternative means at an identity verification agency; it should be used only as external connection information, and it is advisable not to use it for internal identification purposes.
- DI (Duplication Information): a one-way encrypted 64-byte string generated in the case of identity verification through an alternative means at an identity verification agency; it is used to restrict the creation of duplicate accounts by a user within a site.

## Related laws

- Article 24-2 of the Personal Information Protection Act (Restrictions on Processing Resident Registration Numbers)
- Article 23-2 of the Network Act (Restrictions on the Use of Resident Registration Numbers)

## Evidence (examples)

- Personal information collection forms (website membership registration screen, event participation, membership application form, and the like)
- Online personal information collection form (screen providing alternative registration means such as identity verification)
- Where a resident registration number is processed, evidence materials for the basis of processing the resident registration number
- Personal information processing policy

## Nonconformity examples

- Where a resident registration number was collected based on the data subject's consent for a simple member management purpose such as real-name verification in connection with website registration
- Where a data subject's resident registration number was collected on the basis of enforcement rules or an ordinance of a local government
- Where the last 6 digits of a resident registration number are collected for purposes such as identity verification when a password is lost, but there is no relevant legal basis
- Where a job applicant's resident registration number was collected without a legal basis during the recruitment screening process
- Where a resident registration number was collected for identity verification when inquiring about products or services at a call center
- Where, on the ground that there is a legal basis for collecting the resident registration number, only an identity verification and membership registration method that receives the resident registration number was provided at the website membership registration stage without providing an alternative registration means
