# 2.4.1 Designation of Protection Zones

## Certification criterion

To protect personal information and critical information, documents, storage media, key facilities and systems, and the like from physical/environmental threats, physical protection zones such as controlled zones/restricted zones/reception zones shall be designated, and protective measures for each zone shall be established/implemented.

## Key checkpoints

1. Have criteria been established for designating physical protection zones such as controlled zones, restricted zones, and reception zones in order to protect personal information and critical information, documents, storage media, key facilities and systems, and the like from physical and environmental threats?
2. Are protection zones being designated in accordance with the physical protection zone designation criteria, and are protective measures for each zone being established/implemented?

## Detailed explanation

To protect personal information and critical information, documents, storage media, key facilities and systems, and the like from physical/environmental threats, criteria for designating physical protection zones such as controlled zones, restricted zones, and reception zones shall be established.

- Designate physical protection zones as reception zones, restricted zones, controlled zones, and the like
- Select the terminology and classification of protection zones to suit the organization's environment

  ※ Physical protection zones (examples)
  - Reception zone : an area where outsiders can enter without a separate access pass (e.g. : reception area)
  - Restricted zone : a place where separate access control devices and monitoring systems are installed to prevent unauthorized access, and where an access pass such as a staff card is required to enter (e.g. : departmental offices)
  - Controlled zone : a place that includes all the control items of a restricted zone, where access eligibility is kept to the minimum number of persons, and where additional procedures are required for entry (e.g. : computer room, communications equipment room, control room, HVAC room, power generation room, power supply room)

Protection zones shall be designated in accordance with the physical protection zone designation criteria, and protective measures for each zone shall be established/implemented.

- Apply protective measures for each zone, such as access control methods (ID card, biometrics, etc.), authorized entrants, entry procedures, and video surveillance
- Since access authorization to controlled zones is kept to a minimum even within the organization, mark them as controlled zones where necessary to fundamentally block access attempts in the first place, and periodically review whether any unlawful access attempts have occurred

## Related laws

- Personal Information Protection Act Article 29 (Duty of Safeguards)
- Standards for Measures to Ensure the Safety of Personal Information Article 10 (Physical Safeguards)

## Evidence (examples)

- Physical security guideline (protection zone designation criteria)
- Status of protection zone designation
- Protection zone markings
- Status of protective measures by protection zone

## Nonconformity examples

- The internal physical security guideline specifies that personal information storage facilities and system areas are to be designated as controlled zones, but some target areas, such as the document archive where membership application documents are stored, were omitted from the controlled zones
- The internal physical security guideline specifies that a controlled zone sign of a designated format is to be installed for controlled zones, but signs were not installed in some controlled zones
