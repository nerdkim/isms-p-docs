# 2.11.5 Incident Response and Recovery

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Domain | 2. Protection Measure Requirements |
| Section | 2.11 Incident Prevention and Response |
| Item | 2.11.5 Incident Response and Recovery |
| Applicable certification | ISMS `●` / ISMS-P `●` |

## Certification criterion

When signs or occurrences of an intrusion incident or personal information breach are recognized, the legal notification and reporting obligations must be complied with, response and recovery must be carried out promptly in accordance with the procedures, and after analyzing the incident, recurrence prevention measures must be established and reflected in the response system.

## Key checkpoints

1. When signs or occurrences of an intrusion incident or personal information breach are recognized, are response and reporting carried out promptly in accordance with the defined intrusion incident response procedures?
2. When a personal information breach incident occurs, are the procedures for notifying data subjects (users) and reporting to relevant agencies carried out in accordance with the applicable laws?
3. After an intrusion incident is closed, is the cause of the incident analyzed, and are the results reported and shared with the relevant organizations and personnel?
4. Using the information obtained through the analysis of the intrusion incident, are measures established to prevent similar incidents from recurring, and where necessary, are the intrusion incident response procedures and the like modified?

## Detailed explanation

When signs or occurrences of an intrusion incident or personal information breach are recognized, response and reporting must be carried out promptly in accordance with the defined intrusion incident response procedures.

- Initial response to the intrusion incident and evidence preservation measures
  - Deleting/changing access rights to the information system suspected of intrusion, or blocking access
  - Security inspection of internal and external systems such as networks and firewalls, and vulnerability remediation measures
  - Preserving evidence such as external access records needed for the incident investigation
  - Verifying whether personal information and important information have been leaked through log analysis and the like
- Preparing an intrusion incident report including the following items, and reporting it internally
  - Date and time the intrusion incident occurred
  - Reporter and date and time of the report
  - Incident details (findings, damage details, etc.)
  - Progress details of the incident response
  - Time taken until the incident response, etc.
- If the impact of the intrusion incident on the organization is severe, reporting promptly up to top management in accordance with the reporting procedures

When a personal information breach incident occurs, the procedures for notifying data subjects and reporting to relevant agencies must be carried out in accordance with the applicable laws.

After an intrusion incident is closed, the cause of the incident must be analyzed, and the results must be reported and shared with the relevant organizations and personnel.

- After the intrusion incident is handled and closed, performing an analysis of the cause of the incident, preparing a results report, and reporting it to the responsible person
- Sharing the intrusion incident information and the discovered vulnerabilities, causes, and remediation measures with the relevant organizations and personnel

Using the information obtained through the analysis of the intrusion incident, measures must be established to prevent similar incidents from recurring, and where necessary, the intrusion incident response procedures and the like must be modified.

- Using the information obtained through the analysis of the intrusion incident, establishing recurrence prevention measures so that similar incidents are not repeated
- Where necessary based on the analyzed results, modifying the intrusion incident response system, including the intrusion incident response procedures and the information protection policies and procedures

## Related laws

- Personal Information Protection Act, Article 34 (Notification/Reporting of Leakage etc. of Personal Information)
- Network Act, Article 48-3 (Reporting of Intrusion Incidents, etc.), Article 48-4 (Cause Analysis of Intrusion Incidents, etc.)

## Evidence (examples)

- Intrusion incident response procedures
- Intrusion incident response report
- Intrusion incident management ledger
- Personal information leakage report
- Emergency contact list

## Nonconformity examples

- The internal intrusion incident response guidelines stipulate that, when an intrusion incident occurs, it must be reported to the internal information protection committee and the relevant stakeholder departments; however, when an intrusion incident occurred, the department in charge took response measures on its own and did not report to the information protection committee and the relevant stakeholder departments.
- There was a case in which part of the service was interrupted due to an intrusion incident suspected to be a recent DDoS attack, but no cause analysis or recurrence prevention measures were established for it.
- A personal information leakage incident occurred due to external hacking, but on the grounds that the number of leaked personal information records was small, notification and reporting were not carried out within 72 hours.
- Due to an error by a person in charge, personal information of 1,000 or more data subjects was leaked through the internet website bulletin board, but leakage notification to the relevant data subjects was not carried out.
