# 2.2.6 Actions in Case of Security Violations

## Certification criterion

When employees and relevant external parties violate laws, regulations, or internal policies, the organization shall establish and implement action procedures accordingly.

## Key checkpoints

1. Has the organization established disciplinary provisions for cases where employees and relevant external parties violate their information security and personal information protection responsibilities and obligations under laws, regulations, and internal policies?
2. When a violation of information security or personal information protection is detected, are actions carried out in accordance with internal procedures?

## Detailed explanation

The organization shall establish disciplinary provisions for cases where employees and relevant external parties violate their information security and personal information protection responsibilities and obligations under laws, regulations, and internal policies.

- Establish standards and procedures for actions such as investigation, explanation/justification, and disciplinary measures when noncompliance with relevant laws and internal rules, failure to fulfill responsibilities, or damage, leakage/exposure, or misuse/abuse of important information and personal information is discovered
- Also consider reward measures for cases where information security and personal information protection responsibilities and obligations have been faithfully fulfilled

When a violation of information security or personal information protection is detected, actions shall be carried out in accordance with internal procedures.

- Carry out actions in accordance with the reward and punishment provisions and record the results
- Where necessary, use them as company-wide announcements or as training case studies

## Related laws

_No individual statutory provision directly corresponding to this item is specified._

## Evidence (examples)

- HR regulations (disciplinary provisions for violations of information security and personal information protection regulations)
- Records of disciplinary actions against violators of information security and personal information protection guidelines
- Incident case records (company-wide announcements, training content)

## Nonconformity examples

- The case where handling standards and procedures for violators of information security and personal information protection regulations are not included at all in the internal rules
- The case where warning messages are delivered to relevant persons whose policy violations were detected through security systems (DLP, database access control systems, internal information leakage control systems, etc.), but follow-up actions in accordance with internal rules, such as explanation/justification, additional investigation, and disciplinary measures, are not implemented
