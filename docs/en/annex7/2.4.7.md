# 2.4.7 Work Environment Security

| Field | Value |

| Domain | 2. Protective Measure Requirements |

## Certification criterion

Protective measures for the work environment, such as clean desk practices and periodic inspections, shall be established and implemented so that personal information and important information are not exposed to or leaked to unauthorized persons through shared office equipment (document storage rooms, shared PCs, multifunction printers, file servers, etc.) and individual work environments (work PCs, desks, etc.).

## Key checkpoints

1. Are protective measures established and implemented for shared facilities and office equipment such as document storage rooms, shared PCs, multifunction printers, and file servers?
2. Are protective measures established and implemented to prevent the leakage/exposure of personal information and important information through individual work environments such as work PCs, desks, and drawers?
3. Are the protective measures necessary to safely manage printed/copied materials of personal information, such as paper printouts containing personal information, being taken?
4. Is compliance with information protection requirements in individual and shared work environments reviewed periodically?

## Detailed explanation

Protective measures shall be established and implemented for shared facilities and office equipment such as document storage rooms, shared PCs, multifunction printers, and file servers.

- Document storage rooms : minimize the number of persons with access, grant access rights by department/task, manage access records
- Shared PCs : designate a person in charge, set up screen savers, set login passwords, change passwords periodically, restrict storage of important information, install antivirus software, apply security updates, etc.
- Shared office equipment : prohibit leaving important documents around shared office equipment such as fax machines, copiers, and printers
- File servers : grant access rights by department/task, minimize unnecessary disclosure of information, issue accounts per user
- Shared offices : prohibit leaving important information (personal information) documents around shared offices such as meeting rooms and project rooms
- Establish protective measures for other shared work environments

Protective measures shall be established and implemented to prevent the leakage/exposure of personal information and important information through individual work environments such as work PCs, desks, and drawers.

- Protective measures when away from the desk : prohibit leaving personal information, documents containing personal information, and auxiliary storage media unattended (clean desk), set up screen savers and passwords, etc.
- Prohibit exposing login information (passwords, etc.) on monitors, desks, etc.
- Store documents and auxiliary storage media containing personal information and important information in a secure location with locking devices
- Shred documents containing personal information and important information using a shredder, etc., so that they cannot be recovered
- Take measures to prevent unauthorized persons from accessing and arbitrarily operating management terminals, etc.

Necessary protective measures shall be taken to prevent the loss/theft/leakage of personal information through printed/copied materials of personal information, such as paper printouts containing personal information, and to safely manage printed/copied materials.

※ Protective measures for printed/copied materials (examples)
- Establish policies, regulations, and guidelines for the protection and management of printed/copied materials
- Prepare and maintain a production/management register for printed/copied materials
- Designate and operate a department that operates/manages printed/copied materials
- Control/report/restrict the external removal and reproduction of printed/copied materials
- Store and manage records of printed/copied materials, such as the person who printed them and the print date/time
- Establish a disposal plan that includes disposal procedures for paper printouts and confirmation of disposal, and conduct periodic inspections
- Apply security technologies for printed/copied materials, such as multifunction printer security and output watermarks, etc.

Compliance with information protection requirements in individual and shared work environments shall be reviewed periodically.

- Manage persons who fail to comply with individual and shared work environment security regulations in accordance with the reward and penalty regulations

※ Office security inspection methods (examples)
- Individual work environment : self-diagnose compliance with information protection requirements; the managing department periodically inspects compliance with information protection requirements
- Shared work environment : periodically inspect compliance with shared work protective measures; announce or provide training on non-compliance matters

## Related laws

- Personal Information Protection Act Article 29 (Duty of Safety Measures)
- Standards for Measures to Ensure the Safety of Personal Information Article 10 (Physical Safety Measures), Article 12 (Safety Measures When Printing/Copying)

## Evidence (examples)

- Office and shared space security inspection report
- Office and shared space security inspection checklist
- Actions taken against non-compliant persons (training, rewards and penalties, etc.)
- Status of protective measures for printed/copied materials

## Nonconformity examples

- A case where the internal personal information management plan specifies that everyday security inspections for personal information protection (clean desk operation, etc.) are to be conducted regularly, but this is not implemented
- A case where documents containing personal information, such as membership application forms, are stored in an office document cabinet without a locking device
- A case where employees' computers do not have screen savers and passwords set, and important documents are left on the desk of an employee on leave for a long time
- A case where protective measures for shared PCs installed in shared office spaces such as meeting rooms have not been established, so that files containing personal information are stored unencrypted, or the PCs are maintained in a vulnerable state, such as security updates not being applied or antivirus software not being installed
