# 2.2.4 Awareness Raising and Education/Training

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Domain | 2. Protection Measure Requirements |
| Section | 2.2 Personnel Security |
| Item | 2.2.4 Awareness Raising and Education/Training |
| Applicable certification | ISMS `●` / ISMS-P `●` |
| Korean source | 2.2.4 인식제고 및 교육훈련 |

## Certification criterion

The organization shall establish and operate an annual awareness-raising activity and education/training plan so that employees and relevant external parties can understand the organization's management system and policies and secure job-specific expertise, and shall evaluate the effectiveness of the results and reflect it in the next plan.

## Key checkpoints

1. Is an annual education plan that includes the timing, duration, targets, content, and methods of information security and personal information protection education established and approved by management?
2. Is education conducted regularly at least once a year according to the annual education plan for all employees and external parties within the scope of the management system, and is additional education conducted when there are significant changes to relevant laws and regulations?
3. When hiring employees and entering into new contracts with external parties, is information security and personal information protection education provided before work begins?
4. Do employees within IT, information security, and personal information protection organizations receive separate education to enhance job-specific expertise related to information security and personal information protection?
5. Are records of education implementation kept, and are the effectiveness and appropriateness of education evaluated and reflected in the next education plan?

## Detailed explanation

The annual information security and personal information protection education plan shall be established by specifically including the timing, duration, targets, content, methods, etc. of education as follows, and shall be approved by management.

- Education types : employee awareness-raising education, education for key job holders and personal information handlers, education for entrusted parties (trustees), specialized education, etc.
- Education plan : education purpose, education targets, education content, education methods, education schedule, education hours, etc. (differentiated according to business scale, amount of personal information held, nature of work, education targets, education types, etc.)
- Education approval : reviewing and approving the education plan and supporting budget allocation, etc. so that it can be implemented according to the plan

Education shall be conducted regularly at least once a year according to the annual education plan for all employees and external parties within the scope of the management system, and additional education shall be conducted when there are significant changes to relevant laws and regulations.

- Includes all personnel who directly/indirectly access information assets, such as employees, temporary staff, and outsourcing service company staff
- In the case of entrusted parties (trustees) and dispatched staff, provide relevant materials so that the relevant company can conduct the education, and manage/supervise whether it is implemented
- Conduct education at least once a year (in particular, for personal information handlers, personal information protection education is required at least once a year in accordance with legal requirements)
- The education content shall include all content necessary for employees and relevant external parties to understand and comply with the organization's management system and policies

  ※ Content to be included in information security and personal information protection education (examples)
  - Basic overview of information security and personal information protection, management system establishment and methods, related laws
  - Internal regulations related to information security and personal information protection, administrative/technical/physical measures
  - Cases and response measures for infringement (leakage) incidents of important information and personal information, legal liability in case of violation of regulations, etc.

- Prepare and implement education methods for personnel who could not attend education due to business trips, vacations, work, etc. (additional education for absentees, cascade/relay education, online education, etc.)
- Conduct additional education when significant changes to internal regulations and procedures, internal/external infringement incidents, changes to relevant laws, etc. occur (however, if the matter is not important, it may be substituted with bulletin board notices, email guidance, booklet distribution, etc.)

When hiring employees and entering into new contracts with external parties, information security and personal information protection education shall be provided before work begins.

- Conduct information security and personal information protection education at the time new personnel arise or before work is performed, so that they become familiar with content such as organizational policies, precautions, and legal liability in case of violation of regulations

Employees within IT, information security, and personal information protection organizations shall be enabled to receive separate education to enhance job-specific expertise related to information security and personal information protection.

- Relevant job holders : IT job holders, chief information security officer, personal information protection officer, personal information handlers, information security job holders, etc.
- Education courses : participation in conferences/seminars/workshops related to information security and personal information protection, education entrusted to specialized education institutions, internal education by inviting external experts, etc.

Records of education implementation shall be kept, and the effectiveness and appropriateness of education shall be evaluated and reflected in the next education plan.

- After conducting education, keep records such as education notices, education materials, and attendance sheets, and evaluate the appropriateness and effectiveness of the education content through surveys or tests according to predefined evaluation criteria
- Prepare measures for improvement points derived from the education evaluation results and reflect them when establishing the next education plan

## Related laws

- Personal Information Protection Act Article 26 (Restrictions on Processing of Personal Information Following Entrustment of Work), Article 28 (Supervision of Personal Information Handlers), Article 29 (Duty of Safety Measures)
- Standards for Measures to Secure the Safety of Personal Information Article 4 (Establishment/Implementation and Inspection of Internal Management Plan)

## Evidence (examples)

- Information security and personal information protection education plan
- Education result report
- Common and job-specific education materials
- List of education attendees

## Nonconformity examples

- A case where an annual information security and personal information protection education plan was established and implemented in the previous year, but the annual information security and personal information protection education plan was not established in the current year without a valid reason
- A case where the annual information security and personal information protection education plan specifies the education cycle and targets, but does not include content such as the implementation schedule, content, and methods
- A case where the annual information security and personal information protection education plan schedules a certain amount of time for personal information protection awareness education targeting all employees, but does not include job-specific personal information protection education plans required for roles such as the personal information protection officer and personal information handlers
- A case where, upon reviewing the information security and personal information protection education plan and result report, outsourcing service company staff (server room cleaning staff, security guards, external developers, etc.) who access information assets and facilities within the certification scope were omitted from the education targets
- A case where information security and personal information protection education was conducted in the current year, but some records of education implementation and evaluation (education materials, attendance sheets, evaluation questionnaires, result reports, etc.) were not kept
- A case where personnel who did not complete information security and personal information protection education are not identified, or additional education methods for such non-completers (cascade/relay education, additional education, online education, etc.) are not established/implemented
