# 2.9.3 Backup and Recovery Management

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Domain | 2. Protection Measure Requirements |
| Section | 2.9 System and Service Operation Management |
| Item | 2.9.3 Backup and Recovery Management |
| Applicable certification | ISMS `●` / ISMS-P `●` |
| Korean source | 2.9.3 백업 및 복구관리 |

## Certification criterion

To maintain the availability of information systems and the integrity of data, procedures for backup targets, cycles, methods, storage locations, retention periods, off-site dispersal, and the like must be established and implemented. In addition, they must be managed so that timely recovery is possible in the event of an incident.

## Key checkpoints

1. Are backup and recovery procedures that include backup targets, cycles, methods, and steps established and implemented?
2. Are recovery tests conducted regularly to verify the completeness and accuracy of backed-up information and the adequacy of recovery procedures?
3. For backup media on which important information is stored, are the backup media dispersed to a physically separated location so as to cope with disasters and calamities?

## Detailed explanation

Backup and recovery procedures that include backup targets, cycles, methods, and steps must be established and implemented so that timely recovery is possible when an information system is damaged due to disasters/calamities, faults, or intrusion incidents.

- Establishment of criteria for selecting backup targets
- Designation of backup personnel and the responsible person
- Definition of backup cycles and retention periods for each backup target
- Backup methods and procedures : use of a backup system, manual methods, etc.
- Management of backup media (e.g., labeling, storage location, access control, etc.)
- Backup recovery procedures : for key information systems, from an IT disaster recovery perspective, regular recovery tests must be performed to check the completeness, accuracy, etc. of backup information
- Management of the backup management ledger, etc.

※ Main backup targets (examples) (selected in consideration of the importance of the target information and information systems)
- Important information (personal information, confidential information, etc.)
- Important databases
- Various logs (information system audit logs, event logs, security system event logs, etc.)
- Configuration files, etc.

Recovery tests must be conducted regularly to verify the completeness and accuracy of backed-up information and the adequacy of recovery procedures.

- Recovery test plan (recovery test cycle and timing, responsible person, method, etc.)
- Establishment of recovery test scenarios
- Conduct of recovery tests and reporting of results
- Establishment and implementation of an improvement plan when problems are found in recovery test results

For backup media on which important information is stored, the backup media must be dispersed to a physically separated location so as to cope with disasters and calamities.

- Backup media on which important information is stored are dispersed and stored at a location physically distant from where the operating information system or the backup system is located, and the dispersal history is managed with a management ledger
  - Dispersal date (removal, receipt, etc.)
  - Dispersed backup media and content of backup information
- Periodically check whether dispersal is being carried out appropriately
- Prepare the following security measures for the dispersal location
  - Measures against natural disasters such as fire and flood (e.g., fire-resistant safe, flame-retardant treatment, etc.)
  - Access control for the dispersal location and media, etc.

## Related laws

- Personal Information Protection Act Article 29 (Duty of Safety Measures)
- Standards for Measures to Ensure the Safety of Personal Information Article 11 (Safety Measures in Preparation for Disasters/Calamities)

## Evidence (examples)

- Backup and recovery procedures
- Recovery test results
- Off-site backup dispersal status

## Nonconformity examples

- When backup and recovery procedures that include backup targets, cycles, methods, and steps have not been established
- When a backup policy has been established, but backup target information that must be retained for a long period (6 months, 3 years, 5 years, etc.) in accordance with legal requirements is not being retained in accordance with the backup policy
- When backup for some systems (security system policies and logs, etc.) that are specified to be separately backed up and managed in accordance with higher-level guidelines or internal guidelines is not being carried out
- When higher-level guidelines or internal guidelines require periodic recovery tests of backup media, but recovery tests have not been conducted for a long time
