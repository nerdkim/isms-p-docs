# 1.1.5 Policy Establishment

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Domain | 1. Establishment and Operation of the Management System |
| Section | 1.1 Laying the Foundation of the Management System |
| Item | 1.1.5 Policy Establishment |
| Applicable certification | ISMS `●` / ISMS-P `●` |
| Korean source | 1.1.5 정책 수립 |

## Certification criterion

Information security and personal information protection policies and their implementing documents shall be established/drafted, and in doing so the organization's information security and personal information protection principles and direction shall be clearly presented. In addition, the policies and implementing documents shall obtain approval from management and be delivered to employees and relevant parties in an easily understandable form.

## Key checkpoints

1. Is a top-level information security and personal information protection policy that includes the basis for all information security and personal information protection activities performed by the organization being established?
2. Are guidelines, procedures, manuals, etc. that stipulate the detailed methods, procedures, cycles, etc. necessary for implementing the information security and personal information protection policy being established?
3. Upon the establishment/amendment of the information security and personal information protection policy/implementing documents, is approval being obtained from the chief executive officer or a person delegated with authority by the chief executive officer?
4. Is the latest version of the information security and personal information protection policy/implementing documents being provided to relevant employees in a form that is easy to access?

## Detailed explanation

The top-level information security and personal information protection policy, which includes the basis for all information security and personal information protection activities performed by the organization, shall be established to include the following contents.

- The will and direction of management, including the chief executive officer, regarding the organization's information security and personal information protection
- The roles/responsibilities and targets/scope for the organization's information security and personal information protection
- The basis for the managerial/technical/physical information security and personal information protection activities performed by the organization

Lower-level implementation documents such as guidelines, procedures, manuals, and guides that stipulate the detailed methods, procedures, cycles, performing entities, etc. necessary to specifically implement the information security and personal information protection matters specified in the information security and personal information protection policy shall be established in accordance with the organization's characteristics.

- Lower-level implementation documents shall be able to specifically present the basis for all information security and personal information protection activities performed by the organization, and shall be established in accordance with the organization's characteristics from various perspectives, such as the protection-target perspective or the performing-entity perspective.
  - ※ Lower-level implementation documents (examples)

  | Protection-target perspective | Performing-entity perspective |
  | --- | --- |
  | Server security guideline | Employee security guideline |
  | Network security guideline | Developer security guideline |
  | Database security guideline | Operator security guideline, etc. |
  | Application security guideline | |
  | Web service security guideline | |
  | Cloud security guideline | |

- The policies and implementing documents (guidelines, procedures, etc.) shall reflect the legal requirements related to personal information protection (statutes, enforcement decrees, enforcement rules, subordinate public notices, guides, etc.) relevant to the services, business, etc. provided by the organization.
- When personal information is processed, an internal management plan pursuant to the Personal Information Protection Act shall be established, including all matters required by the relevant laws and regulations.
  - Matters that must be included in the internal management plan pursuant to the Personal Information Protection Act
    1. Matters concerning the composition and operation of the personal information protection organization
    2. Matters concerning the qualification requirements for and the designation of the personal information protection officer
    3. Matters concerning the roles and responsibilities of the personal information protection officer and personal information handlers
    4. Matters concerning the management/supervision and training of personal information handlers
    5. Matters concerning the management of access rights
    6. Matters concerning access control
    7. Matters concerning encryption measures for personal information
    8. Matters concerning the retention and inspection of access records
    9. Matters concerning the prevention of malicious programs, etc.
    10. Matters concerning vulnerability inspection to prevent the leakage, theft, etc. of personal information
    11. Matters concerning physical safeguards
    12. Matters concerning the establishment/implementation of a personal information leakage incident response plan
    13. Matters concerning risk analysis and management
    14. Matters concerning the management and supervision of trustees when personal information processing tasks are entrusted
    15. Matters concerning the establishment, modification, and approval of the personal information internal management plan
    16. Other matters necessary for personal information protection
    - ※ However, for small business owners/individuals/organizations that process personal information concerning fewer than 10,000 data subjects, this may be omitted.

Upon the establishment/amendment of the information security and personal information protection policy/implementing documents, approval shall be obtained from the chief executive officer or a person delegated with authority by the chief executive officer.

- When establishing/amending the policy and implementing documents, the relevant contents shall be sufficiently consulted/reviewed with stakeholders.
- The impact on the organization's operations and services and legal compliance, etc. arising from changes to the policy and implementing documents shall be considered.
- Records of reviewed matters, such as meeting minutes, shall be kept and the relevant matters shall be reflected in the policy/guidelines, etc.
- The reviewed policy and implementing documents shall be reported to and approved by management.

Upon the establishment/amendment of the information security and personal information protection policy/implementing documents, the latest version shall be provided to relevant employees in an easily understandable form.

- It shall be provided in a form that employees and external parties can easily refer to (electronic bulletin board, booklet, training materials, manuals, etc.).
- When establishment/amendment matters arise for the policy and implementing documents, they shall be announced immediately and the latest version shall be maintained.

## Related laws

- Personal Information Protection Act Article 29 (Duty of Safety Measures)
- Standards for Measures to Ensure the Safety of Personal Information Article 4 (Establishment/Implementation and Inspection of the Internal Management Plan)

## Evidence (examples)

- Information security and personal information protection policy/guidelines/procedures (including establishment/amendment history)
- Meeting minutes of stakeholder review upon establishment/amendment of the information security and personal information protection policy/guidelines/procedures
- Personal information internal management plan
- Notification records of the establishment/amendment of the information security and personal information protection policy/guidelines (groupware, in-house bulletin board, etc.)
- Meeting minutes of the information security and personal information protection committee

## Nonconformity examples

- A case where, according to internal regulations, the establishment/amendment of the information security and personal information protection policy is required to go through a resolution of the information security and personal information protection committee, but a recent policy amendment was made without tabling the matter as an agenda item to the committee, based only on the approval of the chief information security officer and the personal information protection officer.
- A case where the information security and personal information protection policy and guidelines were recently amended, but the relevant matters were not shared/delivered to the relevant departments and employees, so that some departments perform their work based on the old version of the guidelines.
- A case where the information security and personal information protection policy and guidelines are managed only by the security department and are not provided through methods such as a bulletin board or documents so that employees can view them.
